Thailand honeymoon and couples guide

A Thailand honeymoon is known for a Chao Phraya stay, a jungle or Lanna pause, then a private-pool villa on Krabi, Phuket, Samui, or Koh Yao Noi. Fits couples who split 12 nights 3-4-5.

A Chao Phraya river house is the first honeymoon lock: Capella, The Siam, or a Mandarin Oriental river room for three nights. Add four nights in Chiang Mai for wats and cooler air, or Khao Sok for karst lake mornings. Finish with five nights in a private-pool villa: Phulay Bay or Rayavadee in Krabi; Keemala, Trisara, or Kata Rocks in Phuket; Four Seasons, Six Senses, or Sala Samui on Samui; Six Senses Yao Noi on Koh Yao Noi. Named pages live under resort living.

Book a private longtail at 06:30 if you want a bay before the tour boats. Sunset rooftops in Bangkok have dress codes: no flip-flops, sleeves for men. Pick one roof, not a cruise and a tower the same night. See rooftop bars. Fly BKK to the villa airport on Trip.com. Do not stack Andaman and Gulf villas in one honeymoon.

Common questions

How many nights in each place?
3 on the Bangkok river, 4 in Chiang Mai or Khao Sok, 5 at one villa coast. Two hotel changes after the city is enough.
Krabi, Phuket, Samui, or Yao Noi?
Andaman villas (Krabi, Phuket, Yao Noi) from November to April. Samui from January to August. Pick one sea.
When do you charter the longtail?
06:30. Tour boats reach the same bays after 09:00. Park fees of 200 to 400 THB still apply.
