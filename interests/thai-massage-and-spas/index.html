Thai massage, day spas, and onsen in Thailand

Thai massage is known for Wat Pho school tables at 340 to 520 THB and clean day-spa chains. Come for a 30 to 120 minute table, a Bangkok onsen soak, or a Chiang Mai herbal compress.

Wat Pho is known for the massage school on the temple grounds, not a Khao San spa. A 2026 traditional session is 340 THB for 30 minutes or 520 THB for 60 minutes (1,040 THB for 120 minutes), 08:00 to 19:30. Book at the school desk after the 300 THB temple ticket, or check whether the school entrance is separate that day. See Wat Pho.

For a longer, cleaner room, Health Land is about 650 THB for two hours of traditional massage. Let's Relax runs 700 to 1,200 THB depending on length and extras. Bangkok Japanese onsen houses: Yunomori on Sathorn Soi 10 and Sukhumvit Soi 26, and Panpuri Wellness at Gaysorn. Chiang Mai is known for Lanna herbal-compress rooms at Fah Lanna and Oasis Spa. Tip 50 to 100 THB for a street or shop therapist, 100 to 200 THB at a day spa.

School table, chain spa, or onsen soak

Pick one format. The school is the short traditional table. The chain is the two-hour air-conditioned room. The onsen is a soak with a massage add-on. Do not book three treatments the same afternoon.

Common questions

Do you need the Wat Pho temple ticket for the school?
Often yes if you enter through the temple. Ask the school desk. The massage is 340 THB for 30 minutes or 520 THB for 60 minutes on top of the 300 THB gate fee.
How much do you tip?
50 to 100 THB at a street or shop table. 100 to 200 THB at Health Land, Let's Relax, or a hotel spa.
Onsen or traditional massage?
Yunomori or Panpuri if you want a Japanese soak. Wat Pho or Health Land if you want a Thai table. They are different jobs.
