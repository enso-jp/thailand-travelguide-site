Thailand beaches and island hopping guide

Thai beaches are known for three coasts with different dry months. Pick one sea first: Andaman November to April, Lower Gulf January to August, or the east coast November to April.

Andaman Nov-AprGulf Jan-AugLongtail vs speedboat
AndamanPhuket, Krabi, Phi Phi, Lanta, Lipe; best November to April
Lower GulfSamui, Phangan, Tao; drier Jan to Aug; heavy rain Oct to early Dec
Eastern seaboardSamet, Chang, Kood; November to April

The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Phi Phi, Koh Lanta, Koh Lipe) is known for calmer seas from November to April. The Lower Gulf (Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, Koh Tao) stays drier January to August and takes its heaviest rain from October to early December. The eastern seaboard (Koh Samet, Koh Chang, Koh Kood) follows the Andaman clock: November to April. Pick one coast. See Phuket, Koh Samui, and Koh Chang and Koh Kood.

Longtails are cheap, wet, and local: Railay crossings run about 100 THB a person. Speedboats cut time and add spray; they cancel in swell. Marine national park fees for a foreign adult sit at 200 to 400 THB at the pier or the beach desk. Confirm whether a Klook boat includes the fee. For a 10-day Andaman hop, use 2 weeks Andaman or cut it to one base.

Three coasts, two boat types, one park fee

A longtail is a shallow inshore taxi. A speedboat is a longer crossing that hates weather. Do not book both for the same bay. Pay the park fee once per park per day unless the sign says otherwise.

Common questions

Andaman or Gulf for a first beach week?
Andaman from November to April. Lower Gulf from January to August. Do not stack Phuket and Samui on a first week.
Longtail or speedboat?
Longtail for a short hop such as Railay. Speedboat for Phi Phi or a distant snorkel day. The park fee is still 200 to 400 THB.
When is Koh Phangan a bad swim?
October to early December is the heavy Gulf rain. Full Moon is a crowd plan, not a calm-beach plan.
