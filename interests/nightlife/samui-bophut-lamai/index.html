Koh Samui: Bophut and Lamai

Bophut is known for Fisherman's Village, a walkable north-coast promenade. Lamai is a wider, older beach town. Neither is the Chaweng club strip.

These Samui nights are Bophut's Fisherman's Village and Lamai's wider beach, not the Chaweng club strip. Pick one bay. Beach Republic sits in Maret. Nikki Beach is on Lipa Noi, a dedicated crossing already named on the island beach-club page.

January to September is the easier Gulf sea. Book a car on the ring road. Sleep near the village you will walk.

Bophut promenade versus Lamai bay evenings

Timber Shophouse Bay

Bophut and Fisherman Village

Pedestrian-friendly wooden shophouses, beachfront beanbags, and sunset cocktails facing Koh Phangan.

  • Coco Tam and Summer by Coco Tam anchor the western sand with open-air cocktails at 320 to 480 THB.
  • Friday Walking Street closes the lane to traffic from 17:00 to 22:30 for street stalls and bar tables.
  • 15 minutes by car from USM Airport and Choeng Mon headland resorts.
Southeast Cove

Lamai and Maret Sunday sessions

A calmer, wider curve of southeastern sand with long-running oceanfront brunch clubs.

  • Beach Republic in northern Lamai hosts Sunday poolside sessions and Mediterranean tapas.
  • Central Lamai bars pour 150 to 320 THB drinks without Chaweng high-volume club noise.
  • Best paired with a southern Samui stay so you skip the Chaweng hill drive after dark.
Northern Cape Contrast

Choeng Mon and Plai Laem resort bars

Cliffside sunset decks for a quiet aperitif before a 10-minute taxi into Bophut.

  • Dining on the Rocks at Six Senses offers panoramic Gulf views for an early reservation.
  • Taxis between Choeng Mon and Bophut run 300 to 500 THB each way.
  • Never ride a rented scooter over the northern headlands after dark.

The village and the club

Fisherman's Village is Bophut's beach road. Low-rise bars, Coco Tam's on the sand, and a Friday walking street. Other nights are dinner and one drink, not a club crawl. The village is the point.

Beach Republic is the Lamai-side daybed club. 176/34 Moo 4, Tambon Maret, Koh Samui 84310. +66 77 458 100. Pool, sand, and a sunset that can run late. Book a table on weekends.

Nikki Beach sits on Lipa Noi, south-west Samui. It is not a hop from Bophut. Use the island beach-club page if that brand is the night.

North coast versus south-east

Bophut to Lamai is 25 to 40 minutes around the hills. Do one pocket a night. The airport sits between Choeng Mon and Bophut. Chaweng fills the gap with the easiest late food and the most noise.

Boats to Phangan leave from the north-east. A Full Moon is a different trip. Keep a buffer before a Samui flight. Island weather cancels small aircraft first.

Common questions

Bophut or Lamai for a first stay?
Bophut if you want a walkable village and north-coast hotels. Lamai if you want a broader beach and a slightly older town. Chaweng if you want clubs. Do not pick all three.
Do you need a booking?
For Beach Republic and Nikki Beach, yes on weekends. For Fisherman's Village bars, walk in early on weeknights.
