Ari and Pradipat

Ari is known for low-rise bars in a residential grid around BTS Ari. Pradipat is the parallel road. You walk sois. You do not queue on Sukhumvit.

Ari nightlife is low-rise bars in converted houses, not a Sukhumvit queue. West of BTS Ari, Soi Ari 1 to 4 (Ari Samphan) is the walkable village. East of Phahonyothin is a thinner main-road strip. Pradipat holds the home-bar holdouts. Stay in Phaya Thai or Ari if this is the night. Do not commute from Sukhumvit Soi 11 just to say you did.

This is not a club district. Last trains are around midnight. After that, a 2026 Grab is often 80 to 160 THB. Do not rent a scooter because the sois look quiet.

Craft beer gardens, vinyl rooms, and Pradipat homebars

Craft Beer Gardens

Ari Soi 1 to Soi 4 pours

Leafy mid-century yards pouring rotating Thai and Nordic craft taps.

  • Mikkeller Bar Ari and O glee pour 250 to 420 THB craft pints under string-lit trees.
  • Weeknights are walk-in friendly; Friday evenings fill by 19:30.
  • Pair with grilled Northern pork or Isaan plates along Ari Soi 1 before your first pint.
Vinyl Listening

Hi-fi bars on Ari Soi 4

Intimate analog sound rooms built for conversation rather than club volume.

  • Tempo and Long Play spin funk, soul, and Japanese city pop on custom horn speakers.
  • Cocktails and highballs run 340 to 480 THB in 25-seat rooms.
  • Ideal for a two-drink evening when you want to catch the last BTS train before midnight.
Living-Room Bars

Pradipat Road homebars

Converted shophouses and second-floor bars ten minutes south toward Saphan Khwai.

  • Eight Days A Week and BRUT pour natural wine, craft cans, and house highballs to a local creative crowd.
  • BTS Ari Exit 3 and BTS Saphan Khwai sit on either end of the neighbourhood walk.
  • A Grab back to Siam or Sukhumvit after midnight runs 100 to 180 THB.

Rooms with a door number

Mikkeller Bar Ari, 68 Ari Samphan 1 Alley: the beer room that put the soi on visitor maps. Go for the list, not for a cocktail theatre.

Tempo Bar Ari, 50/4 Ari 4 Fang Nua Alley, and Long Play Music and Bar, 19/2 Ari 4 Fang Nua Alley, sit on the same small soi. Music is the point. Check the night's bill; rooms this size cancel.

  • O'glee, 18/1 Soi Ari 1, is the early, casual stop if the group has not agreed on a room: 224 Winesphere, 2/24 Soi Ari (Phahon Yothin 5), is the wine counter for a shorter evening.
  • On the Pradipat side: Eight Days A Week Homebar, 24 Soi Pradiphat 14, and BRUT, 29 Prachanimit 1 Alley (Pradipat 10 pocket). These are living-room bars. Message ahead on a weekend. They are not walk-in clubs.

How the pocket sits

BTS Ari Exit 1 and Exit 3 feed the west sois. Exit numbers get relabelled; follow the signs for Soi Ari / Phaya Thai, then walk. Food here is neighbourhood Thai and cafes, not a Bib crawl. Pair a night with a separate lunch in the Old City. Do not eat three courses of western bistro and call it Bangkok.

Saphan Khwai is one stop north and a different, more local energy. South is Sanam Pao and the Phaya Thai shrine. Stop when the sois repeat. Thonglor is a BTS ride if someone wants a second neighbourhood; that is a new night, not a 01:00 idea.

Common questions

Is Ari safer than Soi 11?
It is quieter and more residential. You still Grab home. The risk here is a dead Monday, not a tout strip.
Do you need a booking?
For the tiny home bars, a message helps on Friday. Mikkeller and the beer rooms are walk-in most weeknights. Confirm current hours on the morning you go.
