Sukhumvit Soi 11

Soi 11 is known for one crafted rum room and a loud tourist strip. Use it if you already sleep at Nana or Asok. Skip it as a citywide plan.

Back to Bangkok
WhenAfter dinner, usually after 21:00
HomeGrab or Bolt, not a new scooter

A 2026 walk-in beer is cheap. Havana Social is often 350 to 500 THB a drink. Go early or accept a wait.

The soi sits off Sukhumvit between Nana and Asok BTS. After midnight the train is gone. Grab from Soi 11 to Thonglor is often 80 to 160 THB. Do not rent a scooter. The frontage road is a highway.

How a night on Sukhumvit Soi 11 breaks down

20:00 Speakeasy

Havana Social and craft cocktail doors

Pre-revolutionary 1940s Havana rum room hidden behind a phone booth code door.

  • Havana Social pours aged rum cocktails at 380 to 520 THB with Latin percussion and cigar lounge seating upstairs.
  • Arrive between 19:30 and 20:30 before the weekend door line builds down the alley.
  • Smart-casual dress (closed shoes for men) is enforced at the booth.
Rooftop Sunset

Above Eleven and open-air decks

Nikkei Peruvian-Japanese plates and park views 33 floors above the Sukhumvit neon.

  • Above Eleven pairs pisco sours and ceviche with open-air skyline views from 18:00 onward.
  • Book a sunset table if you want dinner on the same block before heading downstairs.
  • Drinks run 380 to 550 THB plus service charge and VAT.
Late Strip & Transit

Street-level bars and BTS Nana exit

High-volume open-front bars, late DJs, and straightforward midnight logistics.

  • Late clubs on Soi 11 switch to 200 to 500 THB entry-with-drink-token covers after 22:00.
  • BTS Nana Exit 3 sits a 5-minute walk from the main cluster until 24:00.
  • After midnight, hail Grab from the top or bottom of the soi rather than waiting in mid-soi taxi gridlock.

What the night is known for

  • Havana Social is the crafted room. The rest of the soi is beer, clubs, and people who landed today. That can be fine. Know which you booked. This is not Thonglor and not Charoenkrung. See those pages if you want a dinner-first night or a seated cocktail door.
  • Sleep a block off the soi if you want rest. Towers that face Soi 11 take the bass until late. Two hours on the soi is enough to know if it is your night. Then Grab home. See Sukhumvit for the hotel strip.
  • Soi 11 sits off Sukhumvit between Nana and Asok BTS. Havana Social is the crafted rum room, often 350 to 500 THB a drink in 2026. Go at 20:00 or accept a wait.

Getting there and 2026 spend

  • After midnight the BTS is gone. A 2026 Grab from Soi 11 to Thonglor is often 80 to 160 THB. A hop to Charoenkrung is longer, often 120 to 220 THB. Stay on this soi if you already sleep at Nana or Asok. Do not start the night in Thonglor and "drop by" unless you like a second car.
  • Dress is loose on most doors and tighter at Havana. Flip-flops still get turned away at the crafted room. A beer on the loud stretch is much less than 350 THB.
  • Cover charges appear on some club doors after 22:00, often 200 to 500 THB as a drink token. Ask before you sit.

Where to sleep after

  • This is not Thonglor dinner and not Charoenkrung seated cocktails. Get home on Grab or Bolt. The frontage road is a highway with buses. A 2026 late water is 20 to 40 THB at a 7-Eleven and 120 THB at the bar.

Field notes for 2026

  • Havana at 20:00: The crafted rum room. After 23:00 the soi is a different street.
  • BTS gone after midnight: Grab to Thonglor is 80 to 160 THB. Do not start a second district unless you like that car.
  • Frontage road buses: No rented scooter. Bolt or Grab home.

Common questions

Do you need a booking?
For Havana, early is better than a reservation culture. Weekends fill. The rest of the soi is walk-in.
Is this the best Bangkok night?
No. It is the convenient night if you already sleep here. Thonglor and Charoenkrung are the better rooms.
