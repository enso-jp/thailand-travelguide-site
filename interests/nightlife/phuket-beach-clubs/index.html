Phuket beach clubs

Phuket beach clubs are known for afternoon daybeds that become sunset. Use them in the November to April window if the club is the day.

WhenAfter dinner, usually after 21:00
HomeGrab or Bolt, not a new scooter

A 2026 daybed minimum is often 2,000 to 5,000 THB before drinks. Bring sunscreen and a booked car home. Patong Walking Street is a different product and not listed here as a recommendation.

Catch Beach Club and Cafe del Mar Phuket are the named rooms. They sit on the west coast. HKT plus a car is 45 to 75 minutes depending on the beach. In the wet months, check the sky before you prepay. A cancelled daybed is still a fight.

Andaman sunset daybeds by beach and spend

Bang Tao Flagship

Catch Beach Club on Bang Tao sand

Phuket original white-lounger institution for long lunches, pool sessions, and Friday night DJs.

  • 2026 daybed minimums run 3,000 to 5,000+ THB redeemable on food and bottles during peak dry season.
  • Direct west-facing sand on Bang Tao gives a full sunset horizon from November to April.
  • Book weekend beds 48 hours ahead and arrange your Cherngtalay or Laguna car home before dark.
Kamala Headland

Cafe del Mar at northern Kamala

Ibiza-born poolside club with wood-fired dining, sax-and-DJ sunsets, and cabanas.

  • Single beds and pool cabanas start around 2,000 to 6,000 THB minimum spend depending on row.
  • Sits on the quiet northern end of Kamala Beach, 15 minutes from Surin and Bang Tao.
  • Cocktails run 380 to 550 THB; sunset arrivals should check in by 16:30.
Laguna & Hotel Decks

XANA and Pine calmer sundowners

Family-friendly and resort-backed beachfront decks with lower daytime minimums.

  • XANA at Angsana Laguna and Pine at InterContinental Kamala offer relaxed sunset tables without a late nightclub floor.
  • May to October brings southwest monsoon surf and red-flag swim days; stick to pool decks in wet months.
  • Grab between Kamala, Surin, and Bang Tao runs 250 to 500 THB each way.

What the night is known for

  • Sleep on the same coast: Kamala, Surin, and Bang Tao are the usual pairings. A late scooter from Patong to a west-coast hill hotel is the wrong ending. See Kamala and Surin and Kamala and Bang Tao nightlife.
  • Dress is beach-smart, not Bangkok cocktail: Tables want a minimum. Book your daybed online 48 hours ahead. One club day per week is enough. The second day is better as a boat or a beach without a DJ.
  • Phuket clubs sit on the Andaman west coast: A 2026 private car is often 900 to 1,800 THB.

Getting there and 2026 spend

  • November to April is the window: Wet season is roughly May to October: swell, closed decks, and a prepaid bed that becomes a refund fight. Check the sky before you pay. Patong Walking Street is a different product and not listed here as a plan.
  • Sleep in Kamala, Surin, or Bang Tao if the club is the day: A 2026 late Grab along that coast is often 200 to 500 THB. Save the number.
  • Dress is beach-smart, not a Bangkok cocktail door: Recheck the page the week you travel.

Where to sleep after

  • Cover is usually the daybed, not a stamp: Ask what the minimum includes. Photos on the sand can include people who did not ask. Keep the camera on your own table. A 2026 late water is cheaper at a 7-Eleven in Kamala than at the bar.
