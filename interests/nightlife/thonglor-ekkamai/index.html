Thonglor and Ekkamai

Thonglor is known for dinner first, then bars. Stay on this BTS strip if you want a night that starts with a table, not a soi crawl.

Back to Bangkok
WhenAfter dinner, usually after 21:00
HomeGrab or Bolt, not a new scooter
Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

BTS Thong Lo and Ekkamai are the rails. A 2026 Grab home after midnight is often 80 to 180 THB. After midnight, Grab is faster than walking Sukhumvit Road.

Rabbit Hole is a serious cocktail room. Find The Locker Room is the joke door with drinks that are not a joke. Bar Us is later and louder. Mahaniyom is dinner that becomes a night. A 2026 crafted drink is often 400 to 650 THB. Reservations help on Friday and Saturday.

How Thonglor and Ekkamai split by soi

Velvet Speakeasies

Lower and Mid-Thonglor (Soi 5 to Soi 13)

Unmarked doors, multi-level cocktail rooms, and Bangkok dinner-to-drink energy.

  • Rabbit Hole behind its unmarked timber door between Soi 5 and Soi 7 pours 420 to 580 THB cocktails across three levels.
  • Find The Locker Room on Thonglor Soi 14 hides a championship cocktail program behind a row of lockers.
  • Book a 19:30 dinner on the same soi so you reach the bar door by 21:00 without a second taxi.
Chef-Driven Cocktail Rooms

Phrom Phong to Thonglor Spine

Savory-sweet Thai ingredient menus and intimate bartender-led counters.

  • Bar Us and Mahaniyom serve progressive savoury-and-fruit Thai cocktail flights at 450 to 650 THB.
  • Seats are limited; reserve 3 to 5 days ahead for Friday or Saturday nights.
  • BTS Thong Lo Exit 3 connects to the strip until midnight, followed by 80 to 180 THB Grab rides.
Late Live Rooms

Ekkamai (Sukhumvit Soi 63)

Indie live-music halls, casual beer gardens, and late Thai crowd energy one BTS stop east.

  • Ekkamai beer houses and live rooms run louder and looser than Thonglor speakeasies after 22:30.
  • Thonglor Commons anchors early-evening craft pours and outdoor tables before the late doors open.
  • Keep both stops on the same night only by short taxi; walking between upper Thonglor and Ekkamai in humidity takes 25 minutes.

What the night is known for

  • Eat first: Thonglor restaurants fill at 19:30. The bars fill after 21:00. This is not Soi 11. It is not Charoenkrung. You can do one room here and skip a second neighborhood the same night. A late hop to Soi Nana is a 20 to 35 minute Grab.
  • Sleep on Thong Lo or Ekkamai if this is more than one night: Phrom Phong is one stop west and still works. Nana is a cheaper sleep and a longer late Grab. See Sukhumvit and Phrom Phong and Thong Lo.
  • Thonglor nights start with a table: Rabbit Hole, Find The Locker Room, Bar Us, and Mahaniyom sit on this strip. Eat at 19:30.

Getting there and 2026 spend

  • This is not Soi 11 and not Charoenkrung: Stay on Thong Lo or Ekkamai if this is more than one night. A late hop to Chinatown Soi Nana is a 20 to 35 minute Grab, often 120 to 220 THB. Most groups should not take it. One district is enough.
  • After midnight the BTS is gone: A 2026 Grab home along Sukhumvit is often 80 to 180 THB. Phrom Phong is one stop west and still works as a sleep. Nana is a cheaper room and a longer late car. Do not rent a scooter on this frontage road.
  • Cover charges are uncommon at the cocktail rooms and more common if you drift to a club door after 22:00, often 200 to 500 THB as a drink token. Ask before you sit. A surprise table fee is a walk-out.

Where to sleep after

  • Dress the room: These doors still turn away flip-flops. Photos on the soi can include people who did not ask. Keep the camera on your own table. A 2026 late water is 20 to 40 THB at a 7-Eleven and 120 THB at the bar.

Common questions

Do you need a booking?
For the small cocktail rooms, a note or a walk-in early is enough most weeknights. Weekends fill.
Thonglor or Soi 11?
Thonglor if you want dinner and a door. Soi 11 if you already sleep at Nana and want a loud walk.
