Solo and first-time travel in Thailand

Solo first trips to Thailand are known for Grab and Bolt, BTS/MRT tap-in, and private-room hostels in Chiang Mai, Bangkok, Lanta, and Phangan. Land with the apps, the train, and a short list of scams to skip.

Thailand is a straightforward solo first trip if you use the apps. Grab and Bolt cover Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket, and Samui. BTS and MRT take contactless Visa or Mastercard tap-in; a 2026 ride is often 17 to 62 THB. Buy an eSIM before landing, or use the AIS, True, or DTAC desk after immigration. Full steps sit on Grab and BTS and eSIM.

Overnight trains: book 12GO or the official SRT D-Ticket app. See trains and ferries. Social hubs with private-room hostels and cafes: Old City and Nimman in Chiang Mai; Ari and Talat Noi in Bangkok; Long Beach on Koh Lanta; Lonely Beach and the south-west of Koh Phangan. Scams to skip: the Grand Palace "closed today" tuk-tuk pitch, and jet-ski scratch deposits on island beaches. Walk to the palace gate. Photograph the jet-ski before you start.

Common questions

Is Thailand safe for solo travelers?
In the main tourist grid, yes if you use Grab or Bolt, tap the BTS, and skip the palace-closed tuk-tuk and jet-ski scratch deposit.
eSIM or airport SIM?
eSIM if your phone unlocks it. Airport AIS, True, or DTAC if you want a physical card after immigration. Both beat hotel Wi-Fi only.
Where do solo travelers sleep?
Private-room hostels and guesthouses in Old City or Nimman (Chiang Mai), Ari or Talat Noi (Bangkok), Long Beach (Lanta), Lonely Beach or south-west Phangan.
