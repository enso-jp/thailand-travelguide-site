Chiang Mai food guide

Chiang Mai food is known for khao soi at lunch, not a 21:00 coconut curry. It fits planners who will walk the moat at 07:00 and eat before 15:00.

Khao soiLannaWarorot Thai food hub

Photo: Neuroticoblivion (talk) (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Khao soi50 to 80 THB; most rooms close by 15:00
Bib18 Bib Gourmand in Chiang Mai in 2026
MarketsWarorot in the morning; Sunday walking street after 16:00
GrabMoat to Nimman often 80 to 120 THB

Chiang Mai cooking is a lunch city. Khao soi rooms open late morning and empty by 15:00. A 2026 bowl is 50 to 80 THB. Warorot (Kad Luang) is the morning market for sai ua and fruit. Huen Phen still does a Lanna table at lunch. Sunday walking street is snacks on Ratchadamnoen, not a second dinner plan after a late arrival.

Nimman is pour-over at 80 to 150 THB, not khao soi. Maya basement is air-con. For the bowl circuit use khao soi and markets. For the broader north use Northern Lanna.

Khao Soi ข้าวซอย

Khao soi is the northern coconut curry noodle with a fried-noodle nest. Eat it at lunch. Evening tourist bowls are a different dish. Khao Soi Mae Sai is the named start. 50 to 90 THB.

Khao Soi Mae Sai, 29/1 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

08:00 to 16:00 Mon to Sat; Sunday closed · 50 to 90 THB

Sai Ua ไส้อั่ว

Sai ua is northern herb sausage known for lemongrass, makrut lime, and chili. Buy it at Warorot and pair it with sticky rice. Warorot Market (Kad Luang) is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a stick.

Warorot Market (Kad Luang), Wichayanon Rd, Chang Moi

06:00 to 16:00 · 20 to 40 THB a stick

Nam Prik Ong น้ำพริกอ่อง

Nam prik ong is a pork-and-tomato dip with boiled veg and pork crackling. It is a Lanna table plate, not a snack cup. Huen Phen is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch room from about 08:00; dinner wing later · 80 to 150 THB

Khao Kha Moo ข้าวขาหมู

Khao kha moo is stewed pork leg on rice. Chang Phuak (North Gate) is the evening cluster in Chiang Mai. Chang Phuak (North Gate) shophouses is the named start. 50 to 70 THB.

Chang Phuak (North Gate) shophouses, North Gate strip, Chang Phueak, Chiang Mai

Evening strip; some lunch rooms earlier · 50 to 70 THB

Nam Ngiao ขนมจีนน้ำเงี้ยว

Kanom jeen nam ngiao is Tai Yai rice-noodle soup known for its tomato and pork broth. It is not southern nam ya. Kiat Ocha is the named start. 40 to 60 THB.

Kiat Ocha, 5-7 Inthawarorot Rd, Si Phum

06:00 to 15:00; closed Monday · 40 to 60 THB

Nam Prik Noom น้ำพริกหนุ่ม

Nam prik noom is a smoky green chili dip. Buy it at Warorot and eat it with sticky rice and boiled veg or pork crackling. Warorot Market (Kad Luang) is the named start. Market tub; eat with 20 to 40 THB sides.

Warorot Market (Kad Luang), Wichayanon Rd, Chang Moi

06:00 to 16:00 · Market tub; eat with 20 to 40 THB sides

Gaeng Hang Lay แกงฮังเล

Gaeng hang lay is a Burmese-root pork curry with ginger and tamarind. It is a Chiang Mai sit-down plate, not a street cup. Huen Phen is the named start. 80 to 180 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 80 to 180 THB

Larb Kua ลาบคั่ว

Larb kua is Northern cooked mince known for blood and dry spice. It is not the lime Isan salad on Sukhumvit. Huen Muan Jai is the named start. 80 to 160 THB.

Huen Muan Jai, 24 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

11:00 to 21:00; closed Wednesday; Saturday split lunch and dinner · 80 to 160 THB

Gaeng Ho แกงโฮะ

Gaeng ho is a Lanna mix of leftover curry, glass noodle, and veg. It is a house plate, not a tourist first order. Huen Phen is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 80 to 150 THB

Khao Kan Jin ข้าวกั๊นจิ๊น

Khao kan jin is Tai Yai blood rice steamed in a banana leaf. It is a market breakfast, not a hotel buffet item. Warorot morning packs is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a pack.

Warorot morning packs, Warorot Market, Chang Moi

06:00 to 11:00 · 20 to 40 THB a pack

Kaeng Khae แกงแค

Kaeng khae is a Northern herb curry with little coconut and a lot of local veg. It is a garden plate, not a sweet gaeng. Huen Muan Jai is the named start. 80 to 160 THB.

Huen Muan Jai, 24 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

11:00 to 21:00; closed Wednesday · 80 to 160 THB

Nam Prik Kha น้ำพริกข่า

Nam prik kha is a galangal chili dip served with steamed mushrooms. Huen Muan Jai is the named room for it. Huen Muan Jai is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Huen Muan Jai, 24 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

11:00 to 21:00; closed Wednesday · 80 to 150 THB

Aep แอ็บ

Aep is minced meat or fish grilled in a banana leaf. It is a Lanna snack plate from a Northern table, not a wrap to go. Huen Phen is the named start. 60 to 120 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 60 to 120 THB

Jin Som จิ๊นส้ม

Jin som is Northern sour fermented pork. It is a market snack related to naem, eaten with chili, ginger, and peanuts. Warorot fermented-pork stalls is the named start. 30 to 80 THB.

Warorot fermented-pork stalls, Warorot Market, Chang Moi

06:00 to 16:00 · 30 to 80 THB

Naem แหนม

Naem is sour fermented pork eaten with ginger, peanut, and raw chili. It is a beer snack and a market bite. Warorot and Sunday walking-street snack rows is the named start. 40 to 80 THB.

Warorot and Sunday walking-street snack rows, Warorot by day; Ratchadamnoen after 16:00 on Sunday

Market day; Sunday from 16:00 · 40 to 80 THB

Tam Khanun ตำขนุน

Tam khanun is a Northern shredded jackfruit salad. It is a Lanna som tum cousin without green papaya. Tong Tem Toh is the named start. 60 to 120 THB.

Tong Tem Toh, 11 Nimmanhaemin Soi 13, Suthep

Lunch and dinner; confirm the morning you go · 60 to 120 THB

Gaeng Om แกงอ่อม

Gaeng om is a dill-and-herb Northern soup with pork or fish. It is lighter than hang lay and not a coconut gaeng. Huen Muan Jai is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Huen Muan Jai, 24 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

11:00 to 21:00; closed Wednesday · 80 to 150 THB

Moo Ping หมูปิ้ง

Moo ping is grilled pork on a stick, known for its sweet garlic marinade. Warorot morning grills are the Chiang Mai buy. Warorot morning grills is the named start. 15 to 25 THB a stick.

Warorot morning grills, Warorot Market, Chang Moi

06:00 to 11:00 for the best row · 15 to 25 THB a stick

Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียว

Khao niew is sticky rice, the utensil for sausage, dips, and grilled pork. Ask for a basket, not jasmine, on a Lanna table. Any Lanna table or Warorot grill is the named start. 10 to 22 THB a basket.

Any Lanna table or Warorot grill, Warorot Market or Huen Phen, Old City

With the grill or the lunch room · 10 to 22 THB a basket

Gaeng Pa แกงป่า

Gaeng pa is jungle curry: hot, herbal, and without coconut milk. It is a sharp pot beside the sweeter hang lay. Huen Phen is the named start. 80 to 160 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 80 to 160 THB

Nam Prik Ta Daeng น้ำพริกตาแดง

Nam prik ta daeng is a sharp red chili relish. It is a Lanna heat plate for rice and boiled veg, not a first dip. Tong Tem Toh is the named start. 40 to 80 THB as a side.

Tong Tem Toh, 11 Nimmanhaemin Soi 13, Suthep

Lunch and dinner · 40 to 80 THB as a side

Tod Mun Khao Pod ทอดมันข้าวโพด

Tod mun khao pod is fried sweetcorn cakes. Walking-street and market bags are the Chiang Mai snack, not a fish cake. Sunday Walking Street snack rows is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a bag.

Sunday Walking Street snack rows, Ratchadamnoen Rd, Phra Sing

Sunday from 16:00 · 20 to 40 THB a bag

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is a season dessert here too. Buy it when nam dok mai is in, not as a July hunt. Warorot fruit stalls and Sunday street boxes is the named start. 60 to 120 THB.

Warorot fruit stalls and Sunday street boxes, Warorot by day; Ratchadamnoen on Sunday

Day market; Sunday from 16:00 · 60 to 120 THB

Khanom Jeen Sao Nam ขนมจีนซาวน้ำ

Khanom jeen sao nam is cool rice noodles with pineapple, garlic, chili, and minced pork. It is a heat-break lunch, not a soup. Old City lunch kanom jeen rooms is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

Old City lunch kanom jeen rooms, Inthawarorot and Ratchamanka lunch shops, Si Phum / Phra Sing

10:00 to 14:00 · 40 to 70 THB

Khao Soi Nam Na ข้าวซอยน้ำหน้า

Khao soi nam na is the Tai Yai wide-noodle curry, darker and less coconut-sweet than the Chiang Mai egg-noodle bowl. Tai Yai lunch rooms north of the moat is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Tai Yai lunch rooms north of the moat, Chang Phueak lunch shophouses, Chiang Mai

10:00 to 15:00 · 50 to 80 THB

Pak Neam ผักเหนียง

Pak neam is a bitter Northern leaf, often stir-fried with egg or pork. It is a garden side, not a main. Huen Phen is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 60 to 100 THB

Lam หลาม

Lam is a Northern soup cooked in a bamboo tube. You see it at festival tables and a few Lanna rooms, not at every lunch stall. Huen Muan Jai is the named start. 80 to 160 THB when it is on.

Huen Muan Jai, 24 Ratchaphruek Rd, Chang Phueak

11:00 to 21:00; closed Wednesday · 80 to 160 THB when it is on

Yam Nang Moo ยำหนังหมู

Yam nang moo is a pork-skin salad with herbs and chili. It is a beer plate on a Northern table, not a diet bowl. Tong Tem Toh is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Tong Tem Toh, 11 Nimmanhaemin Soi 13, Suthep

Lunch and dinner · 80 to 140 THB

Pla Som ปลาส้ม

Pla som is sour fermented fish, usually fried. It is a Northern and Isan sour plate, eaten with sticky rice and raw veg. Warorot and Northern lunch rooms is the named start. 60 to 120 THB.

Warorot and Northern lunch rooms, Warorot Market; Huen Phen for a table plate

Morning market; lunch room later · 60 to 120 THB

Sai Krok Prieo ไส้กรอกเปรี้ยว

Sai krok prieo is a sour Northern sausage, smaller and tangier than sai ua. Market grills sell it as a snack stick. Warorot sausage grills is the named start. 15 to 30 THB a stick.

Warorot sausage grills, Warorot Market, Chang Moi

06:00 to 16:00 · 15 to 30 THB a stick

Gaeng Khanun แกงขนุน

Gaeng khanun is a Northern young-jackfruit curry. It is savoury and a little sour, not a sweet fruit pot. Huen Phen is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Huen Phen, 112 Ratchamanka Rd, Phra Sing

Lunch and dinner sessions · 80 to 150 THB

Bua Loi บัวลอย

Bua loi is rice balls in warm coconut milk, known as a gentle night-market dessert. It fits the end of a Chiang Mai grill dinner. Sunday Walking Street dessert cups is the named start. 30 to 50 THB.

Sunday Walking Street dessert cups, Ratchadamnoen Rd, Phra Sing

Sunday from 16:00 · 30 to 50 THB

Khanom Tuay ขนมถ้วย

Khanom tuay is a two-layer coconut cup: salty bottom, sweet cream top. Market trays in Chiang Mai sell a set of small cups. Warorot dessert trays is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a set.

Warorot dessert trays, Warorot Market, Chang Moi

07:00 to 16:00 · 20 to 40 THB a set

Jok โจ๊ก

Jok is rice porridge with pork ball and ginger. Warorot morning halls are the Chiang Mai bowl, not a hotel buffet cup. Warorot breakfast jok shops is the named start. 30 to 60 THB.

Warorot breakfast jok shops, Warorot Market, Wichayanon Rd, Chang Moi

05:00 to 10:00 for the useful pot · 30 to 60 THB

Khao Soi Islam ข้าวซอยอิสลาม

Khao Soi Islam is the Muslim beef curry noodle on Charoen Prathet. It is a different kitchen from the moat-yard chicken bowl. Khao Soi Islam is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Khao Soi Islam, Charoen Prathet Rd, Chang Khlan, south of the Old City

Lunch clock; closed some late afternoons · 50 to 80 THB

Khao Soi Lam Duan ข้าวซอยลำดวน

Khao Soi Lam Duan on Faham Road is the darker-broth classic on the same lunch clock. Order one bowl at 50 to 70 THB, then walk the river.

Khao Soi Lam Duan Faham, 352/22 Charoenrat Rd, Wat Ket

10:00 to 15:00 · 50 to 70 THB

Khao Soi Mae Manee ข้าวซอยแม่มณี

Khao Soi Mae Manee is the Chang Phueak lunch room north of the moat, not inside the Old City. TAT lists 09:00 to 15:30 at 40 to 120 THB. Arrive before 13:00.

Khao Soi Mae Manee, 18 Soi Chotana 24, Chotana Rd, Chang Phueak, Chiang Mai 50300

09:00 to 15:30 · 50 to 80 THB

Sunday Walking Street snacks ถนนคนเดิน

Ratchadamnoen after 16:00 on Sunday. Grills and dessert cups, not a temple substitute. 20 to 60 THB.

Ratchadamnoen Rd, Phra Sing, Chiang Mai 50200

Sunday from 16:00 · 20 to 60 THB

Warorot breakfast กาดหลวง

Morning wholesale hall for jok, coffee, and fruit. Go before 09:00. 30 to 60 THB.

Warorot Market, Wichayanon Rd, Chang Moi, Chiang Mai

05:00 to 17:00 · 30 to 60 THB

Nimman pour-over นิมมานเหมินท์

Cafe morning west of the moat. GRAPH Quarter sits at 25 Soi 13 Sirimangkalajarn. 80 to 150 THB. Maya basement is air-con only.

GRAPH Quarter, 25 Soi 13 Sirimangkalajarn Rd, Suthep, Chiang Mai 50200

09:00 to 17:00 · 80 to 150 THB

Common questions

When should you eat khao soi?
Lunch. Most named rooms close by 15:00. A 21:00 coconut curry on a tourist menu is a different dish.
Old City or Nimman for food?
Old City and Warorot for Lanna plates. Nimman for coffee. Do not hunt khao soi in Maya.
