Bangkok food guide

Bangkok food is known for rail-fed stalls and a late Chinatown walk. It fits first-trip eaters who will pick one serious queue and one market, not three celebrity woks.

Street foodYaowaratBib Gourmand Thai food hub

Photo: Marcin Konsek (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Street plates60 to 150 THB in 2026
YaowaratAfter 18:00; Monday stalls thin out
Jay FaiWalk-in, Wed to Sat; 1,000 THB+ a head
RailBTS and MRT beat a dinner-hour taxi across the river

Eat Bangkok by rail and by clock. Lunch is shophouses and boat-noodle alleys. Night is Yaowarat from MRT Wat Mangkon or Banthat Thong from MRT Sam Yan. A 2026 street plate is often 60 to 150 THB. Jay Fai on Maha Chai is the one-star exception that still cooks like a street wok and will take your evening.

Do not stack Jay Fai, Chatuchak, and Texas Lane on the same calendar. One serious queue and one market is enough. For the stall map use Bangkok street food. For the gold-shop night use Yaowarat.

Pad Thai ผัดไทย

Pad Thai is the tamarind rice-noodle wok Bangkok still argues about. The late classic is a shared plate on Maha Chai, not a hotel lunch. Thip Samai (Pad Thai Pratu Phi) is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Thip Samai (Pad Thai Pratu Phi), 313 Maha Chai Rd, Samran Rat, Phra Nakhon

Evenings until late; confirm the morning you go · 80 to 150 THB

Pad Kra Pao ผัดกะเพรา

Pad kra pao is the holy-basil wok with minced pork or chicken and a fried egg. It is the usual first Bangkok lunch. Thewet and Samsen lunch shophouses is the named start. 60 to 90 THB.

Thewet and Samsen lunch shophouses, Near Krua Apsorn, Dusit, Chao Phraya N15 Thewet

10:00 to 20:00; many close Sunday · 60 to 90 THB

Tom Yum Goong ต้มยำกุ้ง

Tom yum goong is hot-and-sour prawn soup. Nam khon is the creamy coconut bowl. Nam sai is the clear, sharper one. Je Ngor is the named start. 180 to 320 THB sit-down; cheaper at stalls.

Je Ngor, 68/2 Soi Sukhumvit 20, Khlong Toei

11:00 to 22:00 · 180 to 320 THB sit-down; cheaper at stalls

Guay Tiew Reua ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

Guay tiew reua is boat noodles known for a dark, rich broth served in small cups. It fits first-timers who want to try a few cups in one sitting. Victory Monument boat-noodle alleys is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a cup.

Victory Monument boat-noodle alleys, Phaya Thai Rd alleys, Ratchathewi

10:00 to 20:00 · 20 to 40 THB a cup

Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่

Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice with a fermented-bean dip. It is a Bangkok lunch, not a dinner plate. Go-Ang Pratunam Chicken Rice is the named start. 50 to 70 THB.

Go-Ang Pratunam Chicken Rice, 516-518 Phetchaburi Rd, Ratchathewi

07:00 to 18:00; closed Monday · 50 to 70 THB

Hoi Tod หอยทอด

Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette meant for sharing. Nai Mong in Phraeng Phuton is the named room. Nai Mong Hoi Thod is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Nai Mong Hoi Thod, 539 Phraeng Phuton, Phra Nakhon

18:00 to 01:00 · 80 to 150 THB

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is ripe nam dok mai mango with coconut sticky rice. It is a season plate, not a July hunt. Mae Varee is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Mae Varee, 1210 Sukhumvit Rd, Phra Khanong, Khlong Toei (Thong Lo)

06:00 to 22:00 · 80 to 150 THB

Guay Jub ก๋วยจั๊บ

Guay jub is peppery rolled rice noodles with pork offal. Nai Ek on Yaowarat is the named sit-down bowl. Nai Ek Rolled Noodles is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Nai Ek Rolled Noodles, 398-402 Yaowarat Rd, Samphanthawong

16:00 to 23:00; Monday rest · 60 to 100 THB

Crab Omelette ไข่เจียวปู

The crab omelette at Raan Jay Fai is the one-star wok that still cooks like a street stall. It is a queue, not a reservation. Raan Jay Fai is the named start. 1,000 THB plus a head.

Raan Jay Fai, 327 Maha Chai Rd, Samran Rat, Phra Nakhon

09:00 to 19:30 Wed to Sat; confirm @jayfaibangkok · 1,000 THB plus a head

Khao Kha Moo ข้าวขาหมู

Khao kha moo is stewed pork leg on rice, known for the soft jelly around the bone. It fits a cheap, filling lunch. Lunch shophouses near Victory Monument is the named start. 50 to 70 THB.

Lunch shophouses near Victory Monument, Phaya Thai Rd side streets, Ratchathewi

08:00 to 15:00 · 50 to 70 THB

Jok โจ๊ก

Jok is rice porridge with pork ball and ginger. Jok Prince on Charoen Krung is the named Bang Rak bowl. Jok Prince is the named start. 45 to 80 THB.

Jok Prince, 1391 Charoen Krung Rd, Si Lom, Bang Rak

06:00 to 13:00 and a later evening window · 45 to 80 THB

Pad See Ew ผัดซีอิ๊ว

Pad see ew is wide rice noodles in dark soy with Chinese broccoli and egg. It is the mild wok if kra pao is too hot. Wang Lang lunch woks is the named start. 60 to 90 THB.

Wang Lang lunch woks, Wang Lang Market, Arun Amarin Rd, Bangkok Noi

07:00 to 14:00 · 60 to 90 THB

Rad Na ราดหน้า

Rad na is wide rice noodles under a starch gravy with Chinese broccoli. It is the wet cousin of pad see ew. Sam Yan and Banthat Thong lunch woks is the named start. 60 to 90 THB.

Sam Yan and Banthat Thong lunch woks, Banthat Thong Rd, Pathum Wan

10:00 to 20:00 · 60 to 90 THB

Pad Kee Mao ผัดขี้เมา

Pad kee mao is the wide-noodle drunken wok with chili and holy basil. It is a late plate, hotter than pad see ew. Banthat Thong night woks is the named start. 70 to 120 THB.

Banthat Thong night woks, Banthat Thong Rd, Pathum Wan

17:00 to midnight · 70 to 120 THB

Green Curry แกงเขียวหวาน

Gaeng keow wan is green curry known for its sweet-hot coconut sauce, Thai eggplant, and basil. Ask how sweet before you order. Khao gaeng stalls at Or Tor Kor is the named start. 120 to 220 THB sit-down; 60 to 90 THB at khao gaeng stalls.

Khao gaeng stalls at Or Tor Kor, Or Tor Kor Market, Kamphaeng Phet Rd, Chatuchak

06:00 to 18:00 · 120 to 220 THB sit-down; 60 to 90 THB at khao gaeng stalls

Panang Curry พะแนง

Panang is a thick, sweet-savory curry with less liquid than green curry. Beef is the usual meat. Krua Apsorn, Thewet is the named start. 140 to 240 THB sit-down.

Krua Apsorn, Thewet, Samsen Rd near the National Library, Dusit

10:30 to 19:30 Mon to Sat; closed Sunday · 140 to 240 THB sit-down

Tom Kha Gai ต้มข่าไก่

Tom kha gai is coconut chicken soup known for its galangal scent. It suits anyone who finds tom yum too sour or too hot. Je Ngor is the named start. 120 to 220 THB.

Je Ngor, 68/2 Soi Sukhumvit 20, Khlong Toei

11:00 to 22:00 · 120 to 220 THB

Moo Ping หมูปิ้ง

Moo ping is grilled pork on a stick with sticky rice. It is a morning market snack, not a sit-down dinner. Wang Lang morning grills is the named start. 15 to 25 THB a stick.

Wang Lang morning grills, Wang Lang Market, Bangkok Noi

07:00 to 14:00 · 15 to 25 THB a stick

Gai Yang ไก่ย่าง

Gai yang is charcoal grilled chicken with sticky rice and jaew. Sabai Jai on Ekkamai is the named Bangkok room. Sabai Jai Gai Yang is the named start. 100 to 220 THB.

Sabai Jai Gai Yang, 87 Sukhumvit 63 (Soi Ekkamai 3), Khlong Tan Nuea, Watthana

10:30 to 22:00 · 100 to 220 THB

Som Tum Thai ส้มตำไทย

Som tum Thai is green papaya salad with peanut and dried shrimp. Order this first. Thai hot is a different heat. Somtum Der is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Somtum Der, 5/3-5 Soi Sukhumvit 49, Khlong Tan Nuea

11:00 to 22:00 · 80 to 140 THB

Som Tum Poo Plara ส้มตำปูปลาร้า

Som tum poo pla ra is papaya salad with field crab and fermented fish. It is the Isan plate, not a first som tum. Jae Koy Somtum is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Jae Koy Somtum, 362/2-3 Phetchaburi Rd between sois 12 and 14, Ratchathewi

10:00 to 04:00 · 60 to 100 THB

Larb Moo ลาบหมู

Larb moo is minced pork salad known for toasted rice, lime, and chili. Isan larb is hotter and sourer than the northern cooked mince. Jae Koy Somtum is the named start. 80 to 120 THB.

Jae Koy Somtum, 362/2-3 Phetchaburi Rd, Ratchathewi

10:00 to 04:00 · 80 to 120 THB

Nam Tok น้ำตก

Nam tok is grilled-beef salad known for the same lime, chili, and toasted-rice dressing as larb. Pick it if you want chunkier meat, with sticky rice on the side. Soi Sukhumvit 38 late Isan rooms is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Soi Sukhumvit 38 late Isan rooms, Soi Sukhumvit 38, Phra Khanong

17:00 to 02:00 · 80 to 140 THB

Khanom Krok ขนมครก

Khanom krok is small coconut pancakes known for crisp edges and a soft centre. Buy a set, not one piece. Wang Lang coconut carts is the named start. 20 to 40 THB a set.

Wang Lang coconut carts, Wang Lang Market, Bangkok Noi

09:00 to 17:00 · 20 to 40 THB a set

Pa Tong Go ปาท่องโก๋

Pa tong go is Chinese-style fried dough eaten with condensed milk or savory dips. Pa Tong Go Savoey is the named Yaowarat shop. Pa Tong Go Savoey is the named start. 50 to 60 THB.

Pa Tong Go Savoey, Yaowarat / Plaeng Nam pocket, Samphanthawong

Late snack hours on the Chinatown walk · 50 to 60 THB

Yen Ta Fo เย็นตาโฟ

Yen ta fo is a pink, slightly sweet-sour noodle soup with fish balls and morning glory. It is a Bangkok lunch bowl. Victory Monument noodle alleys is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Victory Monument noodle alleys, Phaya Thai Rd alleys, Ratchathewi

10:00 to 20:00 · 50 to 80 THB

Bamee Moo Daeng บะหมี่หมูแดง

Bamee moo daeng is egg noodles with red roast pork and wonton. It is a Chinatown and Pratunam lunch. Yaowarat and Plaeng Nam noodle rooms is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Yaowarat and Plaeng Nam noodle rooms, Plaeng Nam Rd, Samphanthawong

10:00 to 21:00 · 50 to 80 THB

Khao Gaeng ข้าวแกง

Khao gaeng is curry-over-rice from a row of pots. Point at two curries. It is the Bangkok office lunch. Or Tor Kor curry stalls is the named start. 60 to 120 THB.

Or Tor Kor curry stalls, Or Tor Kor Market, MRT Kamphaeng Phet Exit 3

06:00 to 18:00 · 60 to 120 THB

Pu Ob Woon Sen ปูอบวุ้นเส้น

Pu ob woon sen is crab or prawn steamed on glass noodles. Somsak Pu Ob on Charoen Rat is the 2026 Bib cart. Somsak Pu Ob (Charoen Rat) is the named start. Crab pot from 310 THB; prawn pot from about 290 THB.

Somsak Pu Ob (Charoen Rat), 199-205 Soi Charoen Rat 1, Khlong San

16:00 to 21:30 Tue to Sun; Monday closed · Crab pot from 310 THB; prawn pot from about 290 THB

Khao Kluk Kapi ข้าวคลุกกะปิ

Khao kluk kapi is shrimp-paste fried rice with sweet pork, egg, and raw veg. It is a Bangkok lunch plate with many toppings. Wang Lang and palace-side lunch rooms is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Wang Lang and palace-side lunch rooms, Wang Lang Market, Bangkok Noi

09:00 to 14:00 · 60 to 100 THB

Pork Satay สะเต๊ะหมู

Thai satay is grilled pork or chicken with peanut sauce and cucumber relish. It is a snack row, not a main. Lumphini and Silom evening carts is the named start. 40 to 80 THB a set.

Lumphini and Silom evening carts, Lumphini Park edge and Silom lunch sois

16:00 to 21:00 · 40 to 80 THB a set

Tod Mun Pla ทอดมันปลา

Tod mun pla is fried fish cakes known for a bouncy bite and cucumber relish. Order a small plate to share before a curry. Krua Apsorn, Thewet is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Krua Apsorn, Thewet, Samsen Rd near the National Library, Dusit

10:30 to 19:30 Mon to Sat; closed Sunday · 80 to 140 THB

Khao Pad ข้าวผัด

Khao pad is Thai fried rice with egg, onion, and a lime-chili fish-sauce dip. It is the safe lunch when the wok list is long. Thewet lunch shophouses is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Thewet lunch shophouses, Samsen and Thewet, Dusit

10:00 to 20:00 · 50 to 80 THB

Duck Noodles ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเป็ด

Kuay teow ped is braised duck noodle soup. Plaeng Nam is the quieter Chinatown lane for a stew bowl. Plaeng Nam stew rooms is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Plaeng Nam stew rooms, Plaeng Nam Rd, Samphanthawong

10:00 to 21:00 · 60 to 100 THB

Massaman Curry แกงมัสมั่น

Massaman is a Muslim-root curry with potato and peanut. It is milder than a southern sour curry. Muslim and sit-down rooms near Silom is the named start. 180 to 350 THB sit-down.

Muslim and sit-down rooms near Silom, Silom and Bang Rak lunch rooms

11:00 to 21:00 · 180 to 350 THB sit-down

Kanom Jeen Nam Ya ขนมจีนน้ำยา

Kanom jeen nam ya is thin rice noodles in a fish-and-herb curry. It is a Bangkok lunch pot that empties early. Wang Lang kanom jeen pots is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

Wang Lang kanom jeen pots, Wang Lang Market, Bangkok Noi

07:00 to 13:00 · 40 to 70 THB

Thai Suki สุกี้

Thai suki is glass noodles, greens, egg, and meat known for a sweet-hot chili dipping sauce. Elvis Suki on Soi Yotse is the Bib Gourmand pick for a wok-fried plate. Elvis Suki is the named start. Suki plates about 95 to 125 THB.

Elvis Suki, 200/37 Soi Yotse, Pom Prap Sattru Phai

Dinner hours; confirm the morning you go · Suki plates about 95 to 125 THB

Roti Mataba โรตีมะตะบะ

Roti mataba is a stuffed Muslim flatbread with egg and minced meat or veg. It is a Bangkok snack near mosques and night carts. Silom and Bang Rak Muslim carts is the named start. 30 to 60 THB.

Silom and Bang Rak Muslim carts, Silom sois and Charoen Krung near Bang Rak mosque

Evening carts; some lunch windows · 30 to 60 THB

Khanom Buang ขนมเบื้อง

Khanom buang is a crisp Thai crepe with merengue and a sweet or savoury shred. Buy a few, not a box. Wang Lang and Rattanakosin dessert carts is the named start. 10 to 20 THB each.

Wang Lang and Rattanakosin dessert carts, Wang Lang Market and the palace-side walk

09:00 to 17:00 · 10 to 20 THB each

Tub Tim Grob ทับทิมกรอบ

Tub tim grob is water-chestnut cubes in coconut milk over ice. It is a Bangkok heat-break cup, not a bakery cake. Siam and Pathumwan dessert shops is the named start. 30 to 50 THB.

Siam and Pathumwan dessert shops, Siam Square and nearby shophouses

11:00 to 20:00 · 30 to 50 THB

Khao Tom ข้าวต้ม

Khao tom is rice soup with a tray of small sides. It is the late, gentle Bangkok bowl when a wok is too much. Hia Wan Khao Tom Pla is the named start. 90 to 160 THB for the fish pot.

Hia Wan Khao Tom Pla, 816 Chan Rd, Thung Wat Don, Sathon

16:00 to 23:00 daily · 90 to 160 THB for the fish pot

Tom Yum Noodles ก๋วยเตี๋ยวต้มยำ

Guay tiew tom yum is a hot-and-sour noodle bowl, creamier than clear tom yum soup. It is a lunch stall, not a hotel pot. Victory Monument and Pratunam noodle stalls is the named start. 50 to 80 THB.

Victory Monument and Pratunam noodle stalls, Ratchathewi lunch alleys

10:00 to 16:00 · 50 to 80 THB

Pla Pao ปลาเผา

Pla pao is salt-crusted grilled fish stuffed with lemongrass, known for its moist flesh. It fits a group seafood dinner with sticky rice or jasmine rice. Je Ngor is the named start. 200 to 400 THB a fish.

Je Ngor, 68/2 Soi Sukhumvit 20, Khlong Toei

11:00 to 22:00 · 200 to 400 THB a fish

Mama Tom Yum มาม่าต้มยำ

Mama tom yum is instant noodles recooked as a seafood pot. Jeh O Chula on Banthat Thong is the named late bowl. Jeh O Chula is the named start. 150 to 300 THB.

Jeh O Chula, 113 Soi Charat Mueang, Rong Mueang, Pathum Wan

15:50 to 00:00 daily · 150 to 300 THB

Nam Prik Kapi น้ำพริกกะปิ

Nam prik kapi is shrimp-paste chili relish with fried fish and raw veg. It is a rice-table plate, not a dip for chips. Krua Apsorn, Thewet is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Krua Apsorn, Thewet, Samsen Rd near the National Library, Dusit

10:30 to 19:30 Mon to Sat; closed Sunday · 80 to 150 THB

Or Tor Kor fruit อ.ต.ก.

Graded fruit and northern sausages in cool air. Morning only at MRT Kamphaeng Phet. This is not a dinner market.

Or Tor Kor Market, Kamphaeng Phet Rd, Chatuchak, Bangkok 10900

06:00 to 18:00 · Fruit by weight

Wang Lang lunch วังหลัง

Siriraj-side ferry lunch across from Tha Chang. Pots empty by 14:00. Plates 40 to 80 THB.

Wang Lang Market, Arun Amarin Rd, Bangkok Noi

07:00 to 14:00 · 40 to 80 THB

Banthat Thong late บรรทัดทอง

Chula night strip from MRT Sam Yan. Jeh O Chula (Michelin Bib Gourmand) is the Mama tom yum pot at 150 to 300 THB, not a separate shop called Jeh O Mama.

Jeh O Chula, 113 Soi Charat Mueang, Rong Mueang, Pathum Wan, Bangkok 10330

15:50 to 00:00 daily · 150 to 300 THB

Common questions

Which Bangkok food night should you book first?
Yaowarat after 18:00 from Wat Mangkon, or Banthat Thong if you already sleep near Sam Yan. Do not do both.
Do you need a reservation?
Almost never for stalls. Jay Fai is walk-in only, Wednesday to Saturday. Queue early or skip it.
