Krabi and Andaman food guide

Krabi food is known for a town lunch clock and Ao Nang night grills. It fits Andaman evenings after Railay, not a Chiang Mai khao soi hunt.

Ao NangKrabi TownRailay Thai food hub

Photo: Krzysztof Golik (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Town lunchKanom jeen 40 to 70 THB; pots empty by 13:00
Ao NangNight grills 200 to 400 THB if you price the fish
Railay150 to 300 THB; every crate came by longtail
Park daysHong Island box lunch; 300 THB park fee extra

Eat lunch in Krabi Town if you can. Kanom jeen nam ya poo at the morning market is 40 to 70 THB and gone by 13:00. Ao Nang is the night grill strip. Price seafood by weight. Railay dinners cost more because every crate crossed by longtail at 100 THB a person.

Khao soi is not a Krabi dish. If a beach menu lists it, order gaeng som instead. Hong Island boats include a box and a 300 THB park fee. Named dish pages cover town kanom jeen, Ao Nang pla pao, and Railay surcharge pots. For the stay map use Krabi. For Railay logistics use Railay and Phra Nang.

Kanom Jeen Nam Ya Poo ขนมจีนน้ำยาปู

Kanom jeen nam ya poo is rice noodles in crab curry. It fits a Krabi Town morning market, and pots empty by 13:00. Krabi Town morning market is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

Krabi Town morning market, Maharat Rd, Pak Nam, Krabi 81000

07:00 to 13:00 · 40 to 70 THB

Gaeng Som แกงส้ม

Gaeng som is sour curry with local fish. It fits a Krabi Town lunch off Maharat Road, not a Railay longtail dinner. Krabi Town curry rooms off Maharat Rd is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Krabi Town curry rooms off Maharat Rd, Maharat Rd, Pak Nam, Krabi 81000

10:00 to 20:00 · 80 to 140 THB

Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่

Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits a Krabi Town lunch at 50 to 70 THB, cheaper than an Ao Nang beach plate. Maharat Road lunch shophouses is the named start. 50 to 70 THB.

Maharat Road lunch shophouses, Maharat Rd, Pak Nam, Krabi 81000

08:00 to 15:00 · 50 to 70 THB

Pla Pao ปลาเผา

Pla pao is salt-crust grilled fish. It fits an Ao Nang night after 17:00 if you price the fish by weight first. Ao Nang Walking Street grills is the named start. 200 to 400 THB a fair plate.

Ao Nang Walking Street grills, Ao Nang Walking Street, Mueang Krabi, Krabi 81000

17:00 to 23:00 · 200 to 400 THB a fair plate

Hoi Tod หอยทอด

Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette. It fits an Ao Nang walking-street share plate after 17:00. Ao Nang Walking Street stalls is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Ao Nang Walking Street stalls, Ao Nang Walking Street, Mueang Krabi, Krabi 81000

17:00 to 23:00 · 80 to 150 THB

Railay Seafood Curry แกงส้มไร่เลย์

Railay seafood curry is a sour pot that paid a longtail surcharge. It fits diners already on the peninsula, not a first Krabi lunch. Railay West beach restaurants is the named start. 150 to 300 THB.

Railay West beach restaurants, Railay West beach restaurants, Krabi 81000

11:00 to 21:00 · 150 to 300 THB

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. It fits an Ao Nang cart when mango is in, at 80 to 120 THB. Ao Nang Beach road carts is the named start. 80 to 120 THB.

Ao Nang Beach road carts, Ao Nang Beach road carts, Mueang Krabi

12:00 to 20:00 · 80 to 120 THB

Grilled Squid ปลาหมึกย่าง

Grilled squid is a walking-street share plate. It fits Ao Nang after 17:00 if you confirm the THB price first. Ao Nang Walking Street grills is the named start. 80 to 150 THB is the fair band.

Ao Nang Walking Street grills, Ao Nang Walking Street, Mueang Krabi, Krabi 81000

17:00 to 23:00 · 80 to 150 THB is the fair band

Klong Jilad pier snack คลองจิหลาด

Phi Phi boats leave from Klong Jilad Pier. Eat a proper lunch in Krabi Town first, because pier snacks run 60 to 100 THB and do not fill you.

Klong Jilad Pier, Krabi 81000

Boat mornings 08:00 to 13:00 · 60 to 100 THB

Hong Island boxed lunch เกาะห้อง

Park longtail days include a box. The 300 THB park fee is extra. Leave by 08:00.

Noppharat Thara / Ao Nang longtail desks

Boats 08:00 · Tour plus 300 THB

Common questions

Town or Ao Nang for lunch?
Town for kanom jeen and khao man gai at lunch prices. Ao Nang for the night grill. Railay if you already paid the longtail.
Should you eat khao soi in Krabi?
No. Save it for a Chiang Mai lunch clock. Order gaeng som or nam ya poo here.
