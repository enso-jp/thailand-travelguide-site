Koh Chang food guide

Koh Chang food is known for a pier seafood bill and a 40 to 70 THB roadside curry. It fits a west-coast week, not a Bangkok stall crawl.

Bang BaoKlong PraoWhite Sand Thai food hub

Photo: kallerna (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Curry riceOften 40 to 70 THB on the roadside
Bang BaoFish often 200 to 350 THB; Ruan Thai 09:00 to 21:00
Kai BaeGoong Ka Ta, 239 THB a person
Shakes40 to 60 THB; coconut often 40 to 50 THB

Eat curry and noodles on the main road when you want a 40 to 70 THB plate. Nong Anat at the north end of Klong Prao village is the curry-rice room. Kin Dee, inland of White Sand, keeps a Thai menu.

Bang Bao is the expensive pier. A seafood sitting is often 300 to 400 THB a person. For the island map use Koh Chang. For the quieter Trat island use Koh Kood food.

Bang Bao Seafood อาหารทะเลบางเบ้า

Bang Bao is known for live seafood tanks on a fishing-village pier. It fits a south-coast lunch if you agree the per-kilo price before the kitchen starts. Ruan Thai Seafood is the named start. 200 to 350 THB a fish; 300 to 400 THB a person.

Ruan Thai Seafood, Bang Bao pier, Koh Chang, Trat
09:00 to 21:00 · 200 to 350 THB a fish; 300 to 400 THB a person

Khao Gaeng ข้าวแกง

Khao gaeng is curry over rice from pots that are already cooked. Nong Anat, at the north end of Klong Prao village, is the island room locals use for this plate. Nong Anat is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

Nong Anat, North end of Klong Prao village, roadside, Koh Chang, Trat
40 to 70 THB

Kin Dee Thai Plates กินดี

Kin Dee is known for Thai plates on the inland side of White Sand Beach. It fits a dinner that is not a beachfront menu of pizza and mild curry. Kin Dee is the named start. 40 to 70 THB a simple plate; shared dishes 100 to 200 THB.

Kin Dee, Inland side of the main road, White Sand Beach, Koh Chang, Trat
40 to 70 THB a simple plate; shared dishes 100 to 200 THB

Goong Ka Ta กุ้งกระทะ

Goong Ka Ta is the Kai Bae cook-it-yourself table at 239 THB. It fits a group dinner in the centre of the beach, and the room fills in the evening. Goong Ka Ta is the named start. 239 THB a person.

Goong Ka Ta, Just south of AWA Resort, Kai Bae, Koh Chang, Trat
239 THB a person

White Sand Night Stalls แผงอาหารหาดทรายขาว

White Sand night stalls are the roadside grills behind Apple Resort and Bamboo Resort. They fit a snack walk, not a Bang Bao seafood sitting. White Sand night stalls is the named start. 40 to 70 THB a simple plate.

White Sand night stalls, Roadside behind Apple Resort and Bamboo Resort, White Sand Beach, Koh Chang
40 to 70 THB a simple plate

Jae Eiw Seafood เจ๊เอียว

Jae Eiw is known as one of the older seafood rooms on Koh Chang, on the main road by the Klong Prao estuary. It fits a Thai dinner that is not a beach menu. Jae Eiw Seafood is the named start. 300 to 400 THB a person.

Jae Eiw Seafood, Main road near the Klong Prao estuary, Koh Chang, Trat
300 to 400 THB a person

Roadside Fruit Shakes น้ำผลไม้ปั่น

Roadside fruit shakes are known as the cold drink between Koh Chang beaches. They fit a pause on the main road: a shake is often 40 to 60 THB, a coconut often 40 to 50 THB. Roadside shake stalls is the named start. 40 to 60 THB a shake; coconut 40 to 50 THB.

Roadside shake stalls, Main road in White Sand, Klong Prao, and Kai Bae, Koh Chang
40 to 60 THB a shake; coconut 40 to 50 THB

Crab Curry ปูผัดผงกะหรี่

Stir-fried crab with curry powder is a rich, eggy crab wok, the order for a pier seafood meal rather than a cheap lunch. Ruan Thai on Bang Bao pier cooks it from 09:00 to 21:00. Ruan Thai Seafood is the named start. 300 to 400 THB a person.

Ruan Thai Seafood, Bang Bao pier, Koh Chang, Trat
09:00 to 21:00 · 300 to 400 THB a person

White Sand Pad Thai ผัดไทย

Pad thai is a roadside tamarind-noodle wok, the cheap plate when a Bang Bao seafood sitting is too much. The stall row in the centre of White Sand on Koh Chang fries it at night. White Sand night stalls is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

White Sand night stalls, Roadside behind Apple Resort and Bamboo Resort, White Sand Beach, Koh Chang
40 to 70 THB

Kin Dee Curry แกงเขียวหวาน

Coconut milk and Thai basil make a curry for a dinner that is not a beach pizza menu. Kin Dee, inland of White Sand on Koh Chang, cooks it as a shared dish. Kin Dee is the named start. 100 to 200 THB a shared dish.

Kin Dee, Inland side of the main road, White Sand Beach, Koh Chang, Trat
100 to 200 THB a shared dish

Steamed Fish ปลานึ่งมะนาว

Steamed fish with lime and chili is a whole fish in a sour herb dressing, ordered only after you hear the kilo price. Jae Eiw, by the Klong Prao estuary, has been that seafood room for years. Jae Eiw Seafood is the named start. 300 to 400 THB a person.

Jae Eiw Seafood, Main road near the Klong Prao estuary, Koh Chang, Trat
300 to 400 THB a person

Cashew Chicken ไก่ผัดเม็ดมะม่วง

Stir-fried chicken with cashews is a sweet-salty wok of chicken, dried chilies, and nuts. Nature Rocks, the kitchen at Nature Beach Resort on Lonely Beach, has cooked that plate since the room opened. Nature Rocks is the named start. 100 to 200 THB.

Nature Rocks, Nature Beach Resort, Lonely Beach, Koh Chang, Trat 23170
100 to 200 THB
