  1. Thai food

Thai food: street stalls, Bib Gourmand, Lanna, and the south

Thai food is known for regional cooking, not one national plate. Eat Bangkok by rail. Eat Chiang Mai at lunch. Eat Phuket Town in a heritage house, not on a beach grill.

City guides Signature dishes A-Z directory

City guides

Bangkok Bangkok eating is rail-fed stalls, Yaowarat after 18:00, and Bib rooms. Chiang Mai Khao soi is a lunch bowl at 50 to 80 THB. Markets start after 17:00. Phuket Hokkien and Baba-Nyonya in Old Town, not a beach grill. Koh Samui & Gulf Samui food is Chaweng nights and a few island plates worth a Grab. Krabi & Andaman Krabi food is Ao Nang seafood after 17:00 and a cheaper lunch clock in Town. Isan & Regional Markets Som tum, larb, and grilled chicken are the Isan plates. Ask for less heat on a first bowl. Chinatown Yaowarat Yaowarat is the peppery guay jub and Texas Lane walk after 18:00. Night Markets Night markets that matter are Ratchada, Train Night Market, and city walking streets. Michelin Street Food Bib Gourmand rooms are the value badge. Jay Fai is the one-star exception. Rooftop Dining Dress-code nights over the river, not a stall crawl.

Signature dishes

Khao Soi ข้าวซอย Khao soi is the northern coconut curry noodle with a fried-noodle nest. Eat it at lunch in Chiang Mai for 50 to 80 THB. Pad Kra Pao ผัดกะเพรา Holy-basil wok with minced pork or chicken and a fried egg. A 2026 plate is often 60 to 90 THB. Tom Yum Goong ต้มยำกุ้ง Hot and sour prawn soup. Ask for nam khon if you want the creamy coconut version. Som Tum ส้มตำ Green papaya salad. Thai hot is a different heat from Western spicy. Order Thai less spicy on a first plate. Pad Thai ผัดไทย Thip Samai on Maha Chai is the late classic plate, usually 80 to 150 THB. Massaman Curry แกงมัสมั่น Muslim-root curry with beef or chicken, milder than a southern sour curry. Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่ Hainan chicken rice from Pratunam lunch shophouses, usually 50 to 70 THB. Moo Hong หมูฮ้อง Phuket stewed pork with star anise. Order it in Old Town, not on a Patong beach grill. Kanom Jeen Nam Ya Poo ขนมจีนน้ำยาปู Rice noodles in crab curry. A southern lunch pot that empties by early afternoon. Guay Tiew Reua ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ Dark-broth boat noodles in the Victory Monument alleys, sold as 20 THB cups. Hoi Tod หอยทอด Crisp oyster omelette from Talat Phlu or Yaowarat. Share one plate. Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง Seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. Mae Varee on Thong Lo is the named box at 80 to 150 THB.

A-Z directory

B

  • Bangkok

    Bangkok eating is rail-fed stalls, Yaowarat after 18:00, and Bib rooms.
  • Bangkok Bib Gourmand

    Michelin lists 44 Bib rooms in the city and nearby provinces.
  • Bangkok street food

    Bangkok street food is rail-fed stalls and lunch shophouses.
  • Banthat Thong

    Banthat Thong is the Chula night strip for Jeh O Chula Mama pots.

C

  • Chiang Mai

    Khao soi is a lunch bowl at 50 to 80 THB. Markets start after 17:00.
  • Chinatown Yaowarat

    Yaowarat is the peppery guay jub and Texas Lane walk after 18:00.
  • Coffee guide

    Pour-over in Bangkok and Chiang Mai runs 80 to 150 THB.
  • Cooking classes

    A cooking class is a wok station in Bangkok or Chiang Mai.

D

  • Dishes to order

    This is the first-trip decoder for 12 plates.

H

    Crisp oyster omelette from Talat Phlu or Yaowarat. Share one plate. · Bangkok
