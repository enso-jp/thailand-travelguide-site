  1. Thai food

Thai food: street stalls, Bib Gourmand, Lanna, and the south

Thai food is known for regional cooking, not one national plate. Eat Bangkok by rail. Eat Chiang Mai at lunch. Eat Phuket Town in a heritage house, not on a beach grill.

A-Z directory

A

  • Aep (Chiang Mai) แอ็บ

    Aep is minced meat or fish grilled in a banana leaf. It is a Lanna snack plate from a Northern table, not a wrap to go.
  • Ang Thong Boat Lunch (Koh Samui) อาหารเรืออ่างทอง

    Ang Thong day boats are known for a boxed lunch, not a restaurant. It fits a marine-park day if you carry the 300 THB fee and accept a closed window from 1 November to 15 December.
  • Ao Nang Night Grills (Krabi) อ่าวนาง

    Ao Nang is known for night grills on the seafront road. It fits a beach dinner if you price the fish, often 200 to 400 THB, and you already sleep here.
  • Ao Salad Seafood (Koh Kood) อาหารทะเลอ่าวสลัด

    Ao Salad is the eastern fishing village of Koh Kood, and Captain Nhong serves the catch right by the pier.
  • Ari food

    Ari food is Soi Ari shophouses and cafes off BTS Ari.
  • Ayutthaya

    Boat noodle cups, roti sai mai, and river prawns between the ruins.
  • Ayutthaya River Prawns (Ayutthaya) กุ้งแม่น้ำอยุธยา

    River prawns at Phae Krung Kao are known as the overnight plate, 400 to 900 THB by size. A day trip fits Pa Lek cups at about 20 THB instead.

B

  • Ba Mee Ped (Koh Tao) บะหมี่เป็ด

    Ba mee ped is egg noodle with roast duck. Duck 995 in Sairee is the lunch almost everyone on the island recommends.
  • Bamee Moo Daeng (Bangkok) บะหมี่หมูแดง

    Bamee moo daeng is egg noodles with red roast pork and wonton. It is a Chinatown and Pratunam lunch.
  • Ban Ta Khun Day Market (Khao Sok) ตลาดบ้านตาขุน

    Ban Ta Khun day market is known for roast duck, fruit, and curries at the Highway 401 turnoff for Ratchaprapha Pier. It fits a stop on the way to the lake, from 06:00 to 16:00.
  • Banana Roti (Kanchanaburi) โรตีกล้วย

    Folded griddle bread, banana, and condensed milk is the sweet people eat once the noodles are done. Stalls beside the Kanchanaburi bus terminal sell it through the night, 17:00 to 04:00.
  • Banana Roti (Khao Lak) โรตีกล้วย

    A hot griddle, banana, and condensed milk is the dessert after the seafood grills. Bang Niang Market is the stall for a sweet close on four afternoons, 14:00 to 22:00.
  • Banana Roti (Koh Kood) โรตีกล้วย

    The village sweet after curry rice is a hot fold of dough, banana, and condensed milk. Stalls on Koh Kood sell it as a small bill, not a seafood dinner.
  • Banana Roti (Koh Lipe) โรตีกล้วย

    Banana roti, crisp flatbread folded around banana and egg, is the late sweet of Koh Lipe Walking Street, 40 to 80 THB from the dessert stalls.
  • Banana Roti (Koh Phangan) โรตีกล้วย

    The last sweet before a night boat is a hot fold of dough, banana, and condensed milk. Stalls near Thong Sala pier sell it after a Phantip dinner.
  • Banana Roti (Koh Phi Phi) โรตีกล้วย

    The sweet after a wok plate, not the dinner itself, is a crisp fold of dough, banana, and condensed milk. Dessert stalls in Tonsai sell it in the evening.
  • Banana Roti (Pai) โรตีกล้วย

    Dough, banana, and condensed milk on a hot griddle is the crisp sweet after larb. Dessert carts on Pai Walking Street sell it nightly.
  • Bang Bao Seafood (Koh Chang) อาหารทะเลบางเบ้า

    Bang Bao is known for live seafood tanks on a fishing-village pier. It fits a south-coast lunch if you agree the per-kilo price before the kitchen starts.
  • Bang Bao Seafood (Koh Kood) อาหารทะเลบางเบ้า

    Bang Bao Home is a family table at Ao Bang Bao on Koh Kood, cooking village-style Thai seafood rather than a resort buffet.
  • Bang Rak food

    Bang Rak food is Somtum Der on Sala Daeng Road.
  • Bangkok

    Bangkok eating is rail-fed stalls, Yaowarat after 18:00, and Bib rooms.
  • Bangkok Bib Gourmand

    Michelin lists 44 Bib rooms in the city and nearby provinces.
  • Bangkok street food

    Bangkok street food is rail-fed stalls and lunch shophouses.
  • Banglamphu food

    Banglamphu food is Roti Mataba and a short Khao San graze.
  • Banthat Thong

    Banthat Thong is the Chula night strip for Jeh O Chula Mama pots.
  • Beef Noodles (Khon Kaen) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเนื้อ

    Beef noodles in a light broth with brisket, tendon, and meatballs are the bowl to eat before the pot runs low. Here Joi in Sila, Khon Kaen, is the shop, daily from 09:15 to 15:00.
  • Boat Noodles (Ayutthaya) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Guay tiew reua is dark boat noodles sold in small cups. Pa Lek opposite Wat Mahathat is the named Ayutthaya bowl at about 20 THB a cup.
  • Boat Noodles (Kanchanaburi) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Boat noodles, small bowls of spiced pork or beef broth, are a daytime meal in Kanchanaburi town. Order several at a noodle shop before the evening markets take over.
  • Bophut Friday Walking Street (Koh Samui) บ่อผุด

    Fisherman Village is known for a Friday walking street from 17:00. It fits that one night if you already sleep in Bophut. Other nights, Chaweng is 20 to 40 minutes by car.
  • Bua Dang Pad Thai (Udon Thani) ผัดไทย

    Prawn pad thai that stays savoury, not candy-sweet, is the wok this shop is known for. Pad Thai Bua Dang on Posri Road in Udon Thani cooks it daily from 11:30 to 21:00.
  • Bua Loi (Chiang Mai) บัวลอย

    Bua loi is rice balls in warm coconut milk, known as a gentle night-market dessert. It fits the end of a Chiang Mai grill dinner.

C

  • Cashew Chicken (Koh Chang) ไก่ผัดเม็ดมะม่วง

    Stir-fried chicken with cashews is a sweet-salty wok of chicken, dried chilies, and nuts. Nature Rocks, the kitchen at Nature Beach Resort on Lonely Beach, has cooked that plate since the room opened.
  • Chang Phueak food

    Mae Sai khao soi north of the moat, 55 to 90 THB, closed Sunday.
  • Chao Fah Night Market (Krabi) ตลาดเจ้าฟ้า

    Chao Fah night market is the daily Krabi Town row by the river. TAT lists it 17:00 to 00:30, which fits a midweek night when Maharat is dark.
  • Chao Phrom Jok (Ayutthaya) โจ๊กเจ้าพรหม

    Chao Phrom jok is morning rice porridge at the island market. It fits a 07:00 bowl before the paid wats, not a dinner plan.
  • Chao Phrom Market (Ayutthaya) เจ้าพรหม

    Chao Phrom is the Ayutthaya market that rests on Monday. It fits a non-Monday morning. Pa Lek and Abeedeen still work that day.
  • Chaweng Grilled Fish (Koh Samui) ปลาเผาเฉวง

    Chaweng grilled fish is a walking-street seafood plate. It fits night one after a USM landing if you price the fish first.
  • Cheow Lan Raft Kitchens (Khao Sok) อาหารแพเชี่ยวหลาน

    Cheow Lan raft houses are known for their own kitchens on the water. It fits an overnight on the lake, after a longtail from Ratchaprapha Pier.
  • Chiang Mai

    Khao soi is a lunch bowl at 50 to 80 THB. Markets start after 17:00.
  • Chiang Rai

    Khao soi at lunch and night-bazaar sausage after the White Temple.
  • Chicken Feet Soup (Khao Lak) ซุปเปอร์ไก่

    Super kai, a fiery chicken-feet noodle soup, is the reason to find Go Pong, the street kitchen on the Bang Niang side of Khao Lak. It is a local favourite, not a resort plate.
  • Chinatown Yaowarat

    Yaowarat is the peppery guay jub and Texas Lane walk after 18:00.
  • Cicada Market Food (Hua Hin) ตลาดจั๊กจั่น

    Cicada Market is known for street food inside a weekend craft ground at Khao Takiab. It fits Friday to Sunday, 16:00 to 23:00, with snack plates often 40 to 100 THB.
  • Cicada Snacks (Hua Hin) ตลาดจั๊กจั่น

    Cicada Market is the Friday to Sunday craft night toward Khao Takiab. It fits snack cups at 40 to 100 THB, not a full seafood dinner.
  • Clock Tower Plates (Chiang Rai) หอนาฬิกาเชียงราย

    The clock-tower grid is known for town rooms such as Phor Jai, not for a meal at the White Temple. It fits a 50 to 80 THB khao soi before 15:00, then bazaar sticks after 18:00.
  • Coco Bakery (Khao Sok) โคโค่เบเกอรี่

    Coco Bakery is the early village cafe at 389/1 Moo 6. It fits a 07:30 start if the morning market pots are not the breakfast you want.
  • Coffee guide

    Pour-over in Bangkok and Chiang Mai runs 80 to 150 THB.
  • Cooking classes

    A cooking class is a wok station in Bangkok or Chiang Mai.
  • Crab Curry (Koh Chang) ปูผัดผงกะหรี่

    Stir-fried crab with curry powder is a rich, eggy crab wok, the order for a pier seafood meal rather than a cheap lunch. Ruan Thai on Bang Bao pier cooks it from 09:00 to 21:00.
  • Crab Omelette (Bangkok) ไข่เจียวปู

    The crab omelette at Raan Jay Fai is the one-star wok that still cooks like a street stall. It is a queue, not a reservation.
  • Crab Yellow Curry (Koh Phangan) แกงปู

    Crab yellow curry, the south's coconut curry with crab, is the order at Restaurant Sunshine on the road from Thong Sala toward Chaloklum.

D

  • Dishes to order

    This is the first-trip decoder for 12 plates.
  • Duck Noodles (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเป็ด

    Kuay teow ped is braised duck noodle soup. Plaeng Nam is the quieter Chinatown lane for a stew bowl.

E

  • Ekkamai food

    Ekkamai food is Soi 63 shophouses off BTS Ekkamai.

F

  • Fish Maw Soup (Khao Yai) ซุปกระเพาะปลา

    Tong Tong fish-maw soup is the thick bowl at Pak Chong Night Market. It fits a cool evening, often 50 to 80 THB, and the pot runs out.
  • Fisherman Village Grill (Koh Samui) บ่อผุดย่าง

    Fisherman Village grill is the Friday walking-street plate in Bophut. It fits if you already sleep on that bay. The car from Chaweng is 20 to 40 minutes.
  • Five Boat-Noodle Cups (Ayutthaya) ห้าถ้วย

    Five boat-noodle cups are known as the Ayutthaya lunch portion. They fit a ruin day at Pa Lek: about 20 THB a cup, about 100 THB a person, opposite Wat Mahathat.
  • Fruit Smoothie (Koh Kood) น้ำผลไม้ปั่น

    View Point Cafe is a wooden deck on Khlong Chao facing the sea, where the fruit smoothies follow whatever is in season.

G

  • Gaeng Hang Lay (Chiang Mai) แกงฮังเล

    Gaeng hang lay is a Burmese-root pork curry with ginger and tamarind. It is a Chiang Mai sit-down plate, not a street cup.
  • Gaeng Hang Lay (Pai) แกงฮังเล

    Gaeng hang lay, the north's slow pork curry with ginger, is a lunch dish in Pai. Look for it in the Wiang Tai shophouses rather than the evening dessert stalls.
  • Gaeng Ho (Chiang Mai) แกงโฮะ

    Gaeng ho is a Lanna mix of leftover curry, glass noodle, and veg. It is a house plate, not a tourist first order.
  • Gaeng Khanun (Chiang Mai) แกงขนุน

    Gaeng khanun is a Northern young-jackfruit curry. It is savoury and a little sour, not a sweet fruit pot.
  • Gaeng Liang (Bangkok) แกงเลียง

    Gaeng liang is a peppery vegetable soup with shrimp and a ground-pepper paste, not a coconut curry. Thai curry-rice counters are the place for that light bowl.
  • Gaeng Om (Chiang Mai) แกงอ่อม

    Gaeng om is a dill-and-herb Northern soup with pork or fish. It is lighter than hang lay and not a coconut gaeng.
  • Gaeng Pa (Chiang Mai) แกงป่า

    Gaeng pa is jungle curry: hot, herbal, and without coconut milk. It is a sharp pot beside the sweeter hang lay.
  • Gaeng Poo Bai Chaplu (Phuket) แกงปูใบชะพลู

    Gaeng poo bai chaplu is Phuket crab curry with wild betel leaf. It fits a mansion table on Krabi Road when you want a sit-down pot.
  • Gaeng Som (Koh Lanta) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is hot and sour fish curry. Fresh in Lanta Old Town is the room travel guides single out for it.
  • Gaeng Som (Koh Samui) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour curry still cooked in island kitchens around Bang Po and Maenam. It fits a north-coast lunch, not a Chaweng cocktail menu.
  • Gaeng Som (Krabi) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour curry with local fish. It fits a Krabi Town lunch off Maharat Road, not a Railay longtail dinner.
  • Gaeng Som (Phuket) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour southern curry with fish or prawn. It fits a Phuket Town mansion table, hotter than a Bangkok gaeng.
  • Gaeng Som Pla (Hat Yai) แกงส้มปลากะพง

    Gaeng som pla kapong is sour curry with snapper. Suan Chuen Sook is the named Hat Yai southern room at 70 to 400 THB.
  • Gaeng Tai Pla (Bangkok) แกงไตปลา

    Gaeng tai pla is a southern curry of fish and fermented fish innards, turmeric-hot and pungent, not a coconut massaman. Baan Ice on Thong Lo is known for that pot, daily from 11:00 to 22:00.
  • Gai Yang (Bangkok) ไก่ย่าง

    Gai yang is charcoal grilled chicken with sticky rice and jaew. Sabai Jai on Ekkamai is the named Bangkok room.
  • Gai Yang (Khao Sok) ไก่ย่าง

    Chicken grilled over charcoal, with sticky rice and a spicy dip, is the Isan plate near the park. Lab Roi Et, by the Khao Sok gate, is the grill for it daily from 10:00 to 22:00.
  • Gai Yang (Udon Thani) ไก่ย่าง

    Gai yang is charcoal grilled chicken. It fits a UD Town night plate if you are not driving to Khao Suan Kwang.
  • Gai Yang Khao Suan Kwang (Khon Kaen) ไก่ย่างเขาสวนกวาง

    Gai yang Khao Suan Kwang is known for butterflied charcoal chicken on Mittraphap. It fits a highway lunch north of Khon Kaen, not a city night grill.
  • Garlic Squid (Koh Lanta) ปลาหมึกทอดกระเทียม

    Fried squid under a heap of crisp garlic is the plate at Krua Lanta Yai, also called Beautiful, at the north end of Lanta Old Town.
  • Go Benz Jok (Phuket) โจ๊กโกเบนซ์

    Go Benz jok is dry rice porridge with crispy pork. It fits a Phuket Town evening that is not Monday.
  • Gookchai Dim Sum (Hat Yai) กุ๊กชัยติ่มซำ

    Gookchai dim sum is a Hat Yai morning cart for siu mai, rad na pla, and fried dough. It fits breakfast, and the room closes by 13:00.
  • Goong Ka Ta (Koh Chang) กุ้งกระทะ

    Goong Ka Ta is the Kai Bae cook-it-yourself table at 239 THB. It fits a group dinner in the centre of the beach, and the room fills in the evening.
  • Green Curry (Ayutthaya) แกงเขียวหวานลูกชิ้นปลา

    Green curry with fish balls is the house pot at Phae Krung Kao. It fits a river table if you already ordered prawns.
  • Green Curry (Bangkok) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Gaeng keow wan is green curry known for its sweet-hot coconut sauce, Thai eggplant, and basil. Ask how sweet before you order.
  • Green Curry (Khao Lak) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Go Pong's coconut and basil green curry is the familiar plate on a street-kitchen table famous for chicken-feet soup. It is the easy order for anyone not ready for super kai.
  • Green Curry (Khao Sok) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Thai basil in a coconut curry is the plate for a dinner at a jungle lodge. Our Jungle Camp in Klong Sok serves it from 07:30 to 20:30.
  • Green Curry (Koh Lanta) แกงเขียวหวาน

    A coconut-milk curry with Thai basil, milder than a dry southern pot, is the order when you want the heat down. Yawee in Saladan cooks it daily from 09:30 to 22:00.
  • Green Curry (Koh Phi Phi) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Green curry is the coconut curry with Thai basil. Garlic 1992 in Tonsai is the busy local Thai room where it sits on a short menu.
  • Green Curry (Pai) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Na's Kitchen on Ratsadon Road is a small Thai room that cooks every green curry to order, coconut-rich and scented with Thai basil. It is a calm sit-down dinner away from the Walking Street crush.
  • Grilled Fish (Koh Tao) ปลาเผา

    Pla pao is a whole fish grilled over charcoal and priced by weight, with charred skin, lime, and chili. Barracuda Restaurant in Sairee cooks it daily from 16:00 to 23:00.
  • Grilled Market Fish (Khao Yai) ปลาย่างปากช่อง

    A whole grilled fish at Pak Chong Night Market is the larger plate, around 160 THB. It fits two people who already bought skewers at about 10 THB.
  • Grilled Seafood (Koh Phangan) อาหารทะเลย่าง

    Chaloklum is a north-coast fishing village whose Sunday Walking Street turns into a seafood grill from about 17:00, with the day's fish over coals.
  • Grilled Seafood (Koh Phi Phi) อาหารทะเลย่าง

    Tonsai Seafood grills the day's fish over coals on the beach road in front of Phi Phi Banyan Villa. It is the classic Phi Phi seafood dinner.
  • Grilled Seafood (Koh Tao) อาหารทะเลย่าง

    Barracuda is a rooftop on the Sairee main road that grills the day's catch over coals from 16:00 to 23:00, Koh Tao's sunset seafood dinner.
  • Grilled Squid (Koh Kood) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a charcoal piece with a chili dip, the small seafood order when fish and crab are sold by weight. Ao Yai on Koh Kood grills it by the piece.
  • Grilled Squid (Koh Phangan) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Charcoal squid with lime and chili sauce, priced by the piece you point at, is the grill order. Seafood rooms in Chaloklum, on Koh Phangan's north coast, cook it for lunch and dinner.
  • Grilled Squid (Koh Phi Phi) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a charcoal snack with chili sauce, sold by the piece rather than as a whole fish by the kilo. Tonsai's seafood row on Koh Phi Phi grills it in the evening.
  • Grilled Squid (Koh Samui) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a Chaweng dusk cart. It fits a share plate at 80 to 150 THB if you confirm the price first.
  • Grilled Squid (Krabi) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a walking-street share plate. It fits Ao Nang after 17:00 if you confirm the THB price first.
  • Guay Jub (Bangkok) ก๋วยจั๊บ

    Guay jub is peppery rolled rice noodles with pork offal. Nai Ek on Yaowarat is the named sit-down bowl.
  • Guay Tiew Reua ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Dark-broth boat noodles in the Victory Monument alleys, sold as 20 THB cups. · Bangkok
  • Guay Tiew Reua (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Guay tiew reua is boat noodles known for a dark, rich broth served in small cups. It fits first-timers who want to try a few cups in one sitting.

H

  • Halal and Thai Muslim food

    Sai ua is northern herb sausage known for lemongrass, makrut lime, and chili. Buy it at Warorot and pair it with sticky rice.
  • Sai Ua (Chiang Rai) ไส้อั่ว

    Sai ua is northern herb sausage. It fits a Chiang Rai night-bazaar grill after the White Temple day, with sticky rice.
  • Sai Ua (Pai) ไส้อั่ว

    Sai ua is the north's herb-packed pork sausage, sizzling over charcoal on Pai Walking Street. Buy it by the skewer while you graze the stalls.
  • Sai Ua (Sukhothai) ไส้อั่ว

    Sai ua is herb sausage that still sells in Old Sukhothai markets. It fits a snack with sticky rice after the park, not a hotel breakfast slice.
  • Sampheng Lane food

    Sampheng is the daytime snack lane off Yaowarat.
  • Santitham food

    Akha Ama Living Factory and a short ride to Chang Phueak khao soi.
  • Satay (Hat Yai) สะเต๊ะ

    Grilled meat on a stick with peanut sauce is the snack for a walk through the dry-goods lanes. Kim Yong Market in Hat Yai is the stall for that satay through the afternoon and evening.
  • Saturday Gai Yang (Nong Khai) ไก่ย่าง

    Charcoal chicken with sticky rice is the bird for a Mekong evening. Nong Khai's Saturday walking street is the grill for it, 16:00 to 22:00, between the ferry landings.
  • Saturday Larb (Nong Khai) ลาบ

    Minced meat with lime, toasted rice, and herbs is the salad that shares a table with grilled chicken. Nong Khai's Saturday Mekong street is the stall for it, 16:00 to 22:00, and only that night.
  • Saturday Pad Thai (Nong Khai) ผัดไทย

    Tamarind rice noodles are a milder wok if larb is more heat than you want. Nong Khai's Saturday river street is the stall for that pad thai, 16:00 to 22:00.
  • Seafood Barbecue (Koh Kood) บาร์บีคิวอาหารทะเล

    Fisherman Hut in Khlong Chao grills prawns and fish at the water's edge, the Koh Kood dinner people book for a long evening.
  • Seafood Buffet (Koh Lipe) บุฟเฟ่ต์อาหารทะเล

    Progress Restaurant's pick-your-seafood grill is Koh Lipe Walking Street's group dinner, at about 490 THB a person.
  • Seafood Prices by the Kilo (Hua Hin) ราคาอาหารทะเลหัวหิน

    Hua Hin seafood is known for two prices: Soi 72 plates at 50 to 120 THB, and fish by weight on Naresdamri or at Khao Takiab. It fits a dinner only after you hear the number.
  • Shellfish Platter (Koh Kood) หอยและปู

    Tidkoh, north of Khlong Chao, is the shellfish table of Koh Kood, piling crab, cockles and oysters onto a platter to share.
  • Si Satchanalai Day Food (Sukhothai) ศรีสัชนาลัย

    Si Satchanalai is a second ruin day, not a restaurant town. It fits a trip that already ate the 40 to 70 THB Sukhothai bowl in Muang Kao from 08:00 to 15:00.
  • Soi 72 Night Market (Hua Hin) ซอย 72

    Soi 72 is the Hua Hin night market on Dechanuchit Road. It fits the first town dinner after the train, 17:00 to 23:00, at 50 to 120 THB.
  • Som Tum ส้มตำ

    Green papaya salad. Thai hot is a different heat from Western spicy. Order Thai less spicy on a first plate. · Isan
  • Som Tum (Kanchanaburi) ส้มตำ

    Pounded green papaya salad is the cheap, fiery plate at JJ Night Market, the stalls beside Kanchanaburi railway station. Come for this market, not the later one by the bus terminal.
  • Som Tum (Khao Lak) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is pounded green papaya salad. It fits a non-market night at Krua Mae Dang by the Bang Niang junction.
  • Som Tum (Khon Kaen) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. At Khao Suan Kwang, order Thai first, then poo plara if you already know the heat.
  • Som Tum (Koh Lipe) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is pounded green papaya salad. It fits a Walking Street lunch at Papaya Mom.
  • Som Tum (Koh Phangan) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is pounded green papaya salad. It fits a Phantip grill dinner, and you set the heat.
  • Som Tum (Koh Phi Phi) ส้มตำ

    Anna's in Tonsai, a family room next to Moskito Diving, pounds green papaya salad to order. It is the island's honest local-price som tum.
  • Som Tum (Koh Tao) ส้มตำ

    Pounded green papaya salad is the cheap thrill of the Sairee night stalls from about 17:00, priced well under the beachfront menus.
  • Som Tum (Nong Khai) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. In Nong Khai, order Thai first at a river or town Isan room.
  • Som Tum (Pai) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya pounded with lime, fish sauce, palm sugar, and chili while you wait. The mortar on Pai Walking Street is the place for that salad nightly.
  • Som Tum (Pattaya) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. It fits a Jomtien lunch with grilled chicken, not a Walking Street cocktail.
  • Som Tum (Udon Thani) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. At UD Town, order Thai first. Udon heat is not a Bangkok tom yum.
  • Som Tum And Gai Yang (Khao Yai) ส้มตำไก่ย่าง

    Som tum with grilled chicken is the Isan set Pak Chong is known for. It fits a market dinner or a roadside table, often 40 to 80 THB a plate.
  • Som Tum Poo Plara (Bangkok) ส้มตำปูปลาร้า

    Som tum poo pla ra is papaya salad with field crab and fermented fish. It is the Isan plate, not a first som tum.
  • Som Tum Set (Khon Kaen) ส้มตำ

    A som tum set is green papaya pounded with lime, peanut, and chili, the mortar beside noodles rather than a highway trip. Stalls near Glang Muang in Khon Kaen are a lunch stop for that salad.
  • Som Tum Thai (Bangkok) ส้มตำไทย

    Som tum Thai is green papaya salad with peanut and dried shrimp. Order this first. Thai hot is a different heat.
  • Sour Fish Curry (Koh Lipe) แกงส้มปลา

    Gaeng som pla, the south's sour fish curry, is the order at Rak Lay, a long-running seafood room on Koh Lipe Walking Street.
  • South and Baba-Nyonya

    Southern rooms cook sour curry and mansion Baba plates.
  • Southern Seafood (Koh Lipe) อาหารทะเลปักษ์ใต้

    Daya on Pattaya Beach is the southern seafood room locals recommend to each other, with bold curries and fish off the grill.
  • Southern Yam (Hat Yai) ยำใต้

    Southern yam is a sour seafood salad. Tai Khoi Soi Ton in Khuan Lang is the named Hat Yai yam room, closed Monday.
  • Spice levels

    Say mai pet or pet nit noi before a Thai wok starts.
  • Sriruen Pad Thai (Khon Kaen) ผัดไทย

    Duck-egg pad thai is tamarind noodles fried less sweet than a tourist wok, with rice noodles or glass noodles. Sriruen on Ruenchit Road in Khon Kaen is the shop for a daily 15:00 to 21:30 sitting.
  • Steamed Fish (Koh Chang) ปลานึ่งมะนาว

    Steamed fish with lime and chili is a whole fish in a sour herb dressing, ordered only after you hear the kilo price. Jae Eiw, by the Klong Prao estuary, has been that seafood room for years.
  • Sticky Rice (Chiang Mai) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice, the utensil for sausage, dips, and grilled pork. Ask for a basket, not jasmine, on a Lanna table.
  • Sukhothai

    Sukhothai noodles after the dawn bicycle in Old Town.
  • Sukhothai Noodles (Sukhothai) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวสุโขทัย

    Kuay tiew Sukhothai is thin rice noodles with pork, green beans, lime, and crushed peanut. It fits an Old Sukhothai lunch after the dawn bicycle.

T

  • Tam Khanun (Chiang Mai) ตำขนุน

    Tam khanun is a Northern shredded jackfruit salad. It is a Lanna som tum cousin without green papaya.
  • Tam Mua (Khon Kaen) ตำมั่ว

    Tam mua is a mixed Isan pound, often papaya with noodles, pork, and fermented fish. It fits eaters who already finished a Thai som tum.
  • Tha Sadet Snacks (Nong Khai) ของกินท่าเสด็จ

    Grilled pork on a stick is the snack between dry-goods shops at a daytime river market. Tha Sadet Market on Meechai Road in Nong Khai is the hall for it, daily from 08:00 to 18:30.
  • Thai breakfast

    Thai breakfast is jok and pa tong go from about 06:00.
  • Thai curries

    Thai curries are a family of pots. Market sets are often 40 to 80 THB.
  • Thai desserts

    Thai desserts are mango sticky rice and coconut sweets.
  • Thai festival food

    Festival food is khao chae in April and jay food in the ninth lunar month.
  • Thai food etiquette

    Use a spoon, share the dishes, and say how hot you want it.
  • Thai fruit by season

    Mango peaks March to May. Durian follows from April.
  • Thai iced drinks

    Cha yen and oliang are often 25 to 45 THB. Pour-over is separate.
  • Thai noodles

    Boat noodles are 15 to 20 THB. Pad thai is only one of the bowls.
  • Thai rice plates

    Rice plates are chicken rice, pork leg, and curry over rice.
  • Thai street snacks

    Moo ping is 10 to 15 THB a stick. It is a snack, not dinner.
  • Thai Suki (Bangkok) สุกี้

    Thai suki is glass noodles, greens, egg, and meat known for a sweet-hot chili dipping sauce. Elvis Suki on Soi Yotse is the Bib Gourmand pick for a wok-fried plate.
  • Thai yam salads

    Yam salads are som tum and sour plates. Say the heat first.
  • Thanarat Road Cafes (Khao Yai) ถนนธนะรัชต์

    Thanarat Road is known for view cafes on the way to the park, at tourist prices. Coffee is often 90 to 160 THB. Mains often start at 150 to 350 THB.
  • Thepprasit Weekend Market (Pattaya) เทพประสิทธิ์

    Thepprasit is known as Pattaya weekend stall street for cheap plates and clothes. It fits Friday to Sunday. A named seafood room is Nang Nual in Jomtien, 11:00 to 22:00.
  • Thonglor and Soi 38 food

    Soi 38 is the night stall lane between Thong Lo and Ekkamai.
  • Tod Mun Khao Pod (Chiang Mai) ทอดมันข้าวโพด

    Tod mun khao pod is fried sweetcorn cakes. Walking-street and market bags are the Chiang Mai snack, not a fish cake.
  • Tod Mun Pla (Bangkok) ทอดมันปลา

    Tod mun pla is fried fish cakes known for a bouncy bite and cucumber relish. Order a small plate to share before a curry.
  • Tofu Green Curry (Koh Tao) แกงเขียวหวานเต้าหู้

    Tofu green curry is the easy plant-based plate at Pranee's Kitchen in Mae Haad. Ask for no egg and no fish sauce.
  • Tom Kha Gai (Bangkok) ต้มข่าไก่

    Tom kha gai is coconut chicken soup known for its galangal scent. It suits anyone who finds tom yum too sour or too hot.
  • Tom Kha Gai (Koh Phangan) ต้มข่าไก่

    Tom kha gai is coconut chicken soup with galangal. At Phantip it is the creamy bowl to order beside the grills.
  • Tom Klong (Bangkok) ต้มโคล้ง

    Tom klong is a sour herb soup, lighter than a coconut curry, and Somtum Der grills the catfish for it. The room on Sala Daeng Road is known for that Isan pot, daily from 11:00 to 23:00.
  • Tom Lued Moo (Hat Yai) ต้มเลือดหมู

    Tom lued moo is a clear pork-blood soup. Siri on Sri Phadung Withee is the named Hat Yai bowl at 60 to 90 THB.
  • Tom Saep (Bangkok) ต้มแซ่บ

    Tom saep is a clear, fiery Isan soup, often poured over soft bone. Somtum Der on Sala Daeng Road is the room for a sit-down bowl, daily from 11:00 to 23:00, last order 22:15.
  • Tom Yum (Khao Lak) ต้มยำ

    Tom yum goong is a hot-sour prawn soup built on lemongrass, lime leaves, and chili. Bang Niang Market ladles it on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday, 14:00 to 22:00.
  • Tom Yum (Koh Lipe) ต้มยำ

    Tom yum is hot and sour soup. It fits an early Thai Derm dinner if you like heat.
  • Tom Yum (Koh Phi Phi) ต้มยำ

    Tom yum is the hot and sour soup. At Garlic 1992 it is a cheap bowl in a village where most menus charge resort prices.
  • Tom Yum (Pai) ต้มยำ

    Hot and sour tom yum is the other reason to sit down at Na's Kitchen, made fresh when you order. Pair it with the green curry for a full Pai dinner.
  • Tom Yum Goong ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Hot and sour prawn soup. Ask for nam khon if you want the creamy coconut version. · Central
  • Tom Yum Goong (Bangkok) ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Tom yum goong is hot-and-sour prawn soup. Nam khon is the creamy coconut bowl. Nam sai is the clear, sharper one.
  • Tom Yum Goong (Kanchanaburi) ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Tom yum goong is hot and sour prawn soup. It fits a river lunch at Keeree Tara, about 100 m from the River Kwai bridge.
  • Tom Yum Noodles (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวต้มยำ

    Guay tiew tom yum is a hot-and-sour noodle bowl, creamier than clear tom yum soup. It is a lunch stall, not a hotel pot.
  • Tom Yum Talay (Koh Kood) ต้มยำทะเล

    Tom yum talay is hot and sour seafood soup. It fits a Klong Chao dinner at Nuch Leuang near the hotels.
  • Town Pad Thai (Khao Yai) ผัดไทย

    Tamarind rice noodles are a one-plate town dinner after a day in the park. Pak Chong Night Market, near the railway station, is the wok for a 17:00 to 22:00 plate.
  • Tub Tim Grob (Bangkok) ทับทิมกรอบ

    Tub tim grob is water-chestnut cubes in coconut milk over ice. It is a Bangkok heat-break cup, not a bakery cake.

U

  • U Thong Food Walk (Ayutthaya) ถนนอู่ทอง

    U Thong Road is known for candy-floss roti at Abeedeen and river prawns at Phae Krung Kao. Roti is 20 to 40 THB from 05:30 to 19:00. Prawns are 400 to 900 THB if you overnight.
  • UD Town Grills (Udon Thani) ยูดีทาวน์

    UD Town grills are the Udon Thani night-food yard. It fits a first city dinner for som tum, sai krok, and iced drinks at 40 to 120 THB.
  • UD Town Mango (Udon Thani) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    The sweet after the grills is coconut rice and ripe mango. UD Town in Udon Thani is the stall for a March to May box, 17:00 to 22:30.

V

  • Vegetarian and jay food

    Jay food follows the yellow เจ flag and skips fish sauce.
  • Victory Monument food

    Victory Monument is boat noodles at 15 to 20 THB a bowl.
  • Village Morning Market (Khao Sok) ตลาดเช้าเขาสก

    The Khao Sok village morning market is known for rice soup and southern curry over rice. It fits a 06:30 to 09:00 breakfast at 40 to 80 THB, before the restaurant strip wakes up.

W

  • Walking Street (Nong Khai) ถนนคนเดินหนองคาย

    Nong Khai walking street is the Saturday snack walk by the Mekong. It fits cups at 30 to 80 THB, not a nam nuang substitute.
  • Walking Street Food (Pattaya) ถนนคนเดินพัทยา

    Pattaya Walking Street is known for a late strip, not a market price. Beach Road closes to cars from 19:00 to 06:00. Eat fish in Jomtien first if the bill matters.
  • Walking Street Grills (Pattaya) วอล์คกิ้งสตรีท

    Walking Street grills are late central-Pattaya stalls. They fit only if that night is already the trip, not a first-week Andaman substitute.
  • Wang Lang and Talat Noi

    Wang Lang is the ferry lunch; Talat Noi is the morning lane.
  • White Sand Night Stalls (Koh Chang) แผงอาหารหาดทรายขาว

    White Sand night stalls are the roadside grills behind Apple Resort and Bamboo Resort. They fit a snack walk, not a Bang Bao seafood sitting.
  • White Sand Pad Thai (Koh Chang) ผัดไทย

    Pad thai is a roadside tamarind-noodle wok, the cheap plate when a Bang Bao seafood sitting is too much. The stall row in the centre of White Sand on Koh Chang fries it at night.
  • White Temple Day Khao Soi (Chiang Rai) อาหารวันวัดร่องขุ่น

    A White Temple day is known for the temple, not a food court. It fits travelers who eat khao soi in town first, 50 to 80 THB, and keep the night bazaar for after 18:00.
  • Winery Pad Thai (Khao Yai) ผัดไทย

    Tamarind rice noodles with peanut and lime are a sit-down wok on a vineyard menu. Great Hornbill at PB Valley in Pak Chong serves that pad thai daily from 11:00 to 20:00.
  • Wualai food

    Saturday walking street near Chiang Mai Gate, about 17:00 to 22:00.

Y

  • Yam Hua Plee (Khao Lak) ยำหัวปลี

    Yam hua plee is banana blossom salad. It fits a sit-down dinner at Takiang if you want more than the classics.
  • Yam Nang Moo (Chiang Mai) ยำหนังหมู

    Yam nang moo is a pork-skin salad with herbs and chili. It is a beer plate on a Northern table, not a diet bowl.
  • Yaowarat walk

    The Yaowarat walk starts after 18:00 from MRT Wat Mangkon.
  • Yen Ta Fo (Bangkok) เย็นตาโฟ

    Yen ta fo is a pink, slightly sweet-sour noodle soup with fish balls and morning glory. It is a Bangkok lunch bowl.
  • Yen Ta Fo (Kanchanaburi) เย็นตาโฟ

    Yen ta fo is pink noodle soup coloured with fermented red tofu. In Kanchanaburi it is a Saeng Chuto Road cart bowl near the bus terminal.
  • Yen Ta Fo (Khon Kaen) เย็นตาโฟ

    Yen ta fo is a noodle bowl in a tangy pink broth made with fermented bean curd, morning glory, and seafood or pork. Leng on Glang Muang Road in Khon Kaen serves it daily from 07:00 to 03:00.
