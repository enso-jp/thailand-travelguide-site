  1. Thai food

Thai food: street stalls, Bib Gourmand, Lanna, and the south

Thai food is known for regional cooking, not one national plate. Eat Bangkok by rail. Eat Chiang Mai at lunch. Eat Phuket Town in a heritage house, not on a beach grill.

A-Z directory

A

  • Aep (Chiang Mai) แอ็บ

    Aep is minced meat or fish grilled in a banana leaf. It is a Lanna snack plate from a Northern table, not a wrap to go.
  • Ayutthaya

    Boat noodle cups, roti sai mai, and river prawns between the ruins.

B

  • Ba Mee Ped (Koh Tao) บะหมี่เป็ด

    Ba mee ped is egg noodle with roast duck. Duck 995 in Sairee is the lunch almost everyone on the island recommends.
  • Bamee Moo Daeng (Bangkok) บะหมี่หมูแดง

    Bamee moo daeng is egg noodles with red roast pork and wonton. It is a Chinatown and Pratunam lunch.
  • Bangkok

    Bangkok eating is rail-fed stalls, Yaowarat after 18:00, and Bib rooms.
  • Bangkok Bib Gourmand

    Michelin lists 44 Bib rooms in the city and nearby provinces.
  • Bangkok street food

    Bangkok street food is rail-fed stalls and lunch shophouses.
  • Banthat Thong

    Banthat Thong is the Chula night strip for Jeh O Chula Mama pots.
  • Boat Noodles (Ayutthaya) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Guay tiew reua is dark boat noodles sold in small cups. Pa Lek opposite Wat Mahathat is the named Ayutthaya bowl at about 20 THB a cup.
  • Bua Loi (Chiang Mai) บัวลอย

    Bua loi is rice balls in warm coconut milk, known as a gentle night-market dessert. It fits the end of a Chiang Mai grill dinner.

C

  • Chao Phrom Jok (Ayutthaya) โจ๊กเจ้าพรหม

    Chao Phrom jok is morning rice porridge at the island market. It fits a 07:00 bowl before the paid wats, not a dinner plan.
  • Chaweng Grilled Fish (Koh Samui) ปลาเผาเฉวง

    Chaweng grilled fish is a walking-street seafood plate. It fits night one after a USM landing if you price the fish first.
  • Chiang Mai

    Khao soi is a lunch bowl at 50 to 80 THB. Markets start after 17:00.
  • Chiang Rai

    Khao soi at lunch and night-bazaar sausage after the White Temple.
  • Chinatown Yaowarat

    Yaowarat is the peppery guay jub and Texas Lane walk after 18:00.
  • Cicada Snacks (Hua Hin) ตลาดจั๊กจั่น

    Cicada Market is the Friday to Sunday craft night toward Khao Takiab. It fits snack cups at 40 to 100 THB, not a full seafood dinner.
  • Coffee guide

    Pour-over in Bangkok and Chiang Mai runs 80 to 150 THB.
  • Cooking classes

    A cooking class is a wok station in Bangkok or Chiang Mai.
  • Crab Omelette (Bangkok) ไข่เจียวปู

    The crab omelette at Raan Jay Fai is the one-star wok that still cooks like a street stall. It is a queue, not a reservation.

D

  • Dishes to order

    This is the first-trip decoder for 12 plates.
  • Duck Noodles (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเป็ด

    Kuay teow ped is braised duck noodle soup. Plaeng Nam is the quieter Chinatown lane for a stew bowl.

F

  • Fisherman Village Grill (Koh Samui) บ่อผุดย่าง

    Fisherman Village grill is the Friday walking-street plate in Bophut. It fits if you already sleep on that bay. The car from Chaweng is 20 to 40 minutes.

G

  • Gaeng Hang Lay (Chiang Mai) แกงฮังเล

    Gaeng hang lay is a Burmese-root pork curry with ginger and tamarind. It is a Chiang Mai sit-down plate, not a street cup.
  • Gaeng Ho (Chiang Mai) แกงโฮะ

    Gaeng ho is a Lanna mix of leftover curry, glass noodle, and veg. It is a house plate, not a tourist first order.
  • Gaeng Khanun (Chiang Mai) แกงขนุน

    Gaeng khanun is a Northern young-jackfruit curry. It is savoury and a little sour, not a sweet fruit pot.
  • Gaeng Om (Chiang Mai) แกงอ่อม

    Gaeng om is a dill-and-herb Northern soup with pork or fish. It is lighter than hang lay and not a coconut gaeng.
  • Gaeng Pa (Chiang Mai) แกงป่า

    Gaeng pa is jungle curry: hot, herbal, and without coconut milk. It is a sharp pot beside the sweeter hang lay.
  • Gaeng Poo Bai Chaplu (Phuket) แกงปูใบชะพลู

    Gaeng poo bai chaplu is Phuket crab curry with wild betel leaf. It fits a mansion table on Krabi Road when you want a sit-down pot.
  • Gaeng Som (Koh Lanta) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is hot and sour fish curry. Fresh in Lanta Old Town is the room travel guides single out for it.
  • Gaeng Som (Koh Samui) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour curry still cooked in island kitchens around Bang Po and Maenam. It fits a north-coast lunch, not a Chaweng cocktail menu.
  • Gaeng Som (Krabi) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour curry with local fish. It fits a Krabi Town lunch off Maharat Road, not a Railay longtail dinner.
  • Gaeng Som (Phuket) แกงส้ม

    Gaeng som is sour southern curry with fish or prawn. It fits a Phuket Town mansion table, hotter than a Bangkok gaeng.
  • Gaeng Som Pla (Hat Yai) แกงส้มปลากะพง

    Gaeng som pla kapong is sour curry with snapper. Suan Chuen Sook is the named Hat Yai southern room at 70 to 400 THB.
  • Gai Yang (Bangkok) ไก่ย่าง

    Gai yang is charcoal grilled chicken with sticky rice and jaew. Sabai Jai on Ekkamai is the named Bangkok room.
  • Gai Yang (Udon Thani) ไก่ย่าง

    Gai yang is charcoal grilled chicken. It fits a UD Town night plate if you are not driving to Khao Suan Kwang.
  • Gai Yang Khao Suan Kwang (Khon Kaen) ไก่ย่างเขาสวนกวาง

    Gai yang Khao Suan Kwang is known for butterflied charcoal chicken on Mittraphap. It fits a highway lunch north of Khon Kaen, not a city night grill.
  • Go Benz Jok (Phuket) โจ๊กโกเบนซ์

    Go Benz jok is dry rice porridge with crispy pork. It fits a Phuket Town evening that is not Monday.
  • Gookchai Dim Sum (Hat Yai) กุ๊กชัยติ่มซำ

    Gookchai dim sum is a Hat Yai morning cart for siu mai, rad na pla, and fried dough. It fits breakfast, and the room closes by 13:00.
  • Green Curry (Ayutthaya) แกงเขียวหวานลูกชิ้นปลา

    Green curry with fish balls is the house pot at Phae Krung Kao. It fits a river table if you already ordered prawns.
  • Green Curry (Bangkok) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Gaeng keow wan is green curry known for its sweet-hot coconut sauce, Thai eggplant, and basil. Ask how sweet before you order.
  • Green Curry (Koh Phi Phi) แกงเขียวหวาน

    Green curry is the coconut curry with Thai basil. Garlic 1992 in Tonsai is the busy local Thai room where it sits on a short menu.
  • Grilled Squid (Koh Samui) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a Chaweng dusk cart. It fits a share plate at 80 to 150 THB if you confirm the price first.
  • Grilled Squid (Krabi) ปลาหมึกย่าง

    Grilled squid is a walking-street share plate. It fits Ao Nang after 17:00 if you confirm the THB price first.
  • Guay Jub (Bangkok) ก๋วยจั๊บ

    Guay jub is peppery rolled rice noodles with pork offal. Nai Ek on Yaowarat is the named sit-down bowl.
  • Guay Tiew Reua ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Dark-broth boat noodles in the Victory Monument alleys, sold as 20 THB cups. · Bangkok
  • Guay Tiew Reua (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ

    Guay tiew reua is boat noodles known for a dark, rich broth served in small cups. It fits first-timers who want to try a few cups in one sitting.

H

  • Hat Yai

    Turmeric fried chicken at Kim Yong and morning dim sum.
  • Hat Yai Fried Chicken (Hat Yai) ไก่ทอดหาดใหญ่

    Hat Yai fried chicken is turmeric-garlic fried bird with sticky rice. It fits a Kim Yong Market lunch, not a beach grill.
  • Hoi Tod หอยทอด

    Crisp oyster omelette from Talat Phlu or Yaowarat. Share one plate. · Bangkok
  • Hoi Tod (Bangkok) หอยทอด

    Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette meant for sharing. Nai Mong in Phraeng Phuton is the named room.
  • Hoi Tod (Hua Hin) หอยทอด

    Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette. It fits a Hua Hin night-market share plate on Soi 72.
  • Hoi Tod (Krabi) หอยทอด

    Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette. It fits an Ao Nang walking-street share plate after 17:00.
  • Hua Hin

    Soi 72 night market and Naresdamri fish after the train.
  • Hua Hin Night Market (Hua Hin) ตลาดโต้รุ่งหัวหิน

    Hua Hin night market is the Soi 72 grill street. It fits a first town dinner after the train, with plates at 50 to 120 THB.

I

  • Isan & Regional Markets

    Som tum, larb, and grilled chicken are the Isan plates. Ask for less heat on a first bowl.

J

  • Jim Jum (Khon Kaen) จิ้มจุ่ม

    Jim jum is an Isan herb hot pot. It fits a Khon Kaen evening table when the highway bird is already done.
  • Jin Som (Chiang Mai) จิ๊นส้ม

    Jin som is Northern sour fermented pork. It is a market snack related to naem, eaten with chili, ginger, and peanuts.
  • Jok (Bangkok) โจ๊ก

    Jok is rice porridge with pork ball and ginger. Jok Prince on Charoen Krung is the named Bang Rak bowl.
  • Jok (Chiang Mai) โจ๊ก

    Jok is rice porridge with pork ball and ginger. Warorot morning halls are the Chiang Mai bowl, not a hotel buffet cup.
  • Jok (Sukhothai) โจ๊ก

    Jok is rice porridge. It fits a 06:30 bowl beside the Old Sukhothai park gate before the bicycle.
  • Jomtien Grilled Fish (Pattaya) ปลาเผาจอมเทียน

    Jomtien grilled fish is a calmer gulf plate than Walking Street. It fits if you already sleep on that sand.

K

  • Kaeng Khae (Chiang Mai) แกงแค

    Kaeng khae is a Northern herb curry with little coconut and a lot of local veg. It is a garden plate, not a sweet gaeng.
  • Kanchanaburi

    Pink yen ta fo on Saeng Chuto Road and a night market open to 04:00.
  • Kanom Jeen (Sukhothai) ขนมจีน

    Kanom jeen is rice noodles with a curry pot and raw veg. It fits an Old Sukhothai morning before the park heat.
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ngiao (Chiang Rai) ขนมจีนน้ำเงี้ยว

    Kanom jeen nam ngiao is rice noodles in a northern tomato-pork broth. It fits a Chiang Rai lunch, sharper than khao soi.
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ngiao (Pai) ขนมจีนน้ำเงี้ยว

    Nam ngiao is rice noodle in a tomato and pork-rib broth. It fits a cheap lunch at a Thai-menu shop on Route 1095.
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ya (Bangkok) ขนมจีนน้ำยา

    Kanom jeen nam ya is thin rice noodles in a fish-and-herb curry. It is a Bangkok lunch pot that empties early.
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ya (Phuket) ขนมจีนน้ำยา

    Kanom jeen nam ya is rice noodles in a southern fish curry. It fits a Phuket Town morning market, and pots empty by early afternoon.
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ya Poo ขนมจีนน้ำยาปู

    Rice noodles in crab curry. A southern lunch pot that empties by early afternoon. · South
  • Kanom Jeen Nam Ya Poo (Krabi) ขนมจีนน้ำยาปู

    Kanom jeen nam ya poo is rice noodles in crab curry. It fits a Krabi Town morning market, and pots empty by 13:00.
  • Khanom Buang (Bangkok) ขนมเบื้อง

    Khanom buang is a crisp Thai crepe with merengue and a sweet or savoury shred. Buy a few, not a box.
  • Khanom Jeen Sao Nam (Chiang Mai) ขนมจีนซาวน้ำ

    Khanom jeen sao nam is cool rice noodles with pineapple, garlic, chili, and minced pork. It is a heat-break lunch, not a soup.
  • Khanom Krok (Bangkok) ขนมครก

    Khanom krok is small coconut pancakes known for crisp edges and a soft centre. Buy a set, not one piece.
  • Khanom Krok (Chiang Rai) ขนมครก

    Khanom krok is coconut pancake cups from a night-bazaar griddle. It fits a Chiang Rai dessert after the grill.
  • Khanom Tuay (Chiang Mai) ขนมถ้วย

    Khanom tuay is a two-layer coconut cup: salty bottom, sweet cream top. Market trays in Chiang Mai sell a set of small cups.
  • Khao Gaeng (Bangkok) ข้าวแกง

    Khao gaeng is curry-over-rice from a row of pots. Point at two curries. It is the Bangkok office lunch.
  • Khao Gaeng (Koh Phangan) ข้าวแกง

    Khao gaeng is curry over rice from a tray counter. It fits a cheap Phantip Market dinner in Thong Sala.
  • Khao Kan Jin (Chiang Mai) ข้าวกั๊นจิ๊น

    Khao kan jin is Tai Yai blood rice steamed in a banana leaf. It is a market breakfast, not a hotel buffet item.
  • Khao Kha Moo (Bangkok) ข้าวขาหมู

    Khao kha moo is stewed pork leg on rice, known for the soft jelly around the bone. It fits a cheap, filling lunch.
  • Khao Kha Moo (Chiang Mai) ข้าวขาหมู

    Khao kha moo is stewed pork leg on rice. Chang Phuak (North Gate) is the evening cluster in Chiang Mai.
  • Khao Kha Moo (Hat Yai) ขาหมู

    Khao kha moo is stewed pork leg on rice. Ar Restaurant on Suphasan Rangsan is the named Hat Yai Thai-Chinese room.
  • Khao Kluk Kapi (Bangkok) ข้าวคลุกกะปิ

    Khao kluk kapi is shrimp-paste fried rice with sweet pork, egg, and raw veg. It is a Bangkok lunch plate with many toppings.
  • Khao Lak

    Bang Niang Market four days a week, plus Isan heat at the junction.
  • Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่

    Hainan chicken rice from Pratunam lunch shophouses, usually 50 to 70 THB. · Bangkok
  • Khao Man Gai (Ayutthaya) ข้าวมันไก่

    Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits an Ayutthaya island lunch between paid wats at 50 to 70 THB.
  • Khao Man Gai (Bangkok) ข้าวมันไก่

    Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice with a fermented-bean dip. It is a Bangkok lunch, not a dinner plate.
  • Khao Man Gai (Hua Hin) ข้าวมันไก่

    Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits a Hua Hin town lunch at 50 to 70 THB before the beach hour.
  • Khao Man Gai (Koh Samui) ข้าวมันไก่

    Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits a Chaweng lunch shophouse near the hospital soi at 50 to 70 THB.
  • Khao Man Gai (Krabi) ข้าวมันไก่

    Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits a Krabi Town lunch at 50 to 70 THB, cheaper than an Ao Nang beach plate.
  • Khao Mok Gai (Hat Yai) ข้าวหมกไก่

    Khao mok gai is Thai-Muslim spiced chicken rice. It fits a Hat Yai lunch near Kim Yong if you want a halal plate beside the fried bird.
  • Khao Mok Gai (Khao Lak) ข้าวหมกไก่

    Khao mok gai is southern Thai-Muslim chicken biryani. It fits a Bang Niang Market night, four days a week.
  • Khao Niew (Chiang Rai) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a bamboo basket. It is the utensil for sai ua, larb, and nam prik in Chiang Rai.
  • Khao Niew (Khon Kaen) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for gai yang, larb, and som tum in Khon Kaen.
  • Khao Niew (Nong Khai) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for som tum in Nong Khai.
  • Khao Niew (Pattaya) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for som tum and grilled chicken in Pattaya Isan rooms.
  • Khao Niew (Sukhothai) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for sai ua and nam prik in Old Sukhothai.
  • Khao Niew (Udon Thani) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is known as the UD Town utensil, and it fits every grill plate you order.
  • Khao Pad (Bangkok) ข้าวผัด

    Khao pad is Thai fried rice with egg, onion, and a lime-chili fish-sauce dip. It is the safe lunch when the wok list is long.
  • Khao Soi ข้าวซอย

    Khao soi is the northern coconut curry noodle with a fried-noodle nest. Eat it at lunch in Chiang Mai for 50 to 80 THB. · North
  • Khao Soi (Chiang Mai) ข้าวซอย

    Khao soi is the northern coconut curry noodle with a fried-noodle nest. Eat it at lunch. Evening tourist bowls are a different dish.
  • Khao Soi (Chiang Rai) ข้าวซอย

    Khao soi is northern coconut curry noodles. Khao Soi Phor Jai is the named Chiang Rai lunch bowl, not a 21:00 tourist pot.
  • Khao Soi (Pai) ข้าวซอย

    Khao soi is the northern coconut curry noodle. In Pai, Khao Soi Zisters is the bowl people walk 15 minutes out of town for.
  • Khao Soi Islam (Chiang Mai) ข้าวซอยอิสลาม

    Khao Soi Islam is the Muslim beef curry noodle on Charoen Prathet. It is a different kitchen from the moat-yard chicken bowl.
  • Khao Soi Nam Na (Chiang Mai) ข้าวซอยน้ำหน้า

    Khao soi nam na is the Tai Yai wide-noodle curry, darker and less coconut-sweet than the Chiang Mai egg-noodle bowl.
  • Khao Takiab Seafood (Hua Hin) อาหารทะเลเขาตะเกียบ

    Khao Takiab seafood is the hill-end grill strip south of town. It fits if you already sleep toward Cicada, not as a first-night taxi from the station.
  • Khao Tom (Bangkok) ข้าวต้ม

    Khao tom is rice soup with a tray of small sides. It is the late, gentle Bangkok bowl when a wok is too much.
  • Koh Kood

    Ao Yai crab and fish by weight, Klong Chao tom yum at night.
  • Koh Lanta

    Fiery kua kling in Saladan and sour gaeng som in Old Town.
  • Koh Lipe

    Walking Street som tum and spicy Thai Derm plates before 19:30.
  • Koh Phangan

    Phantip Market in Thong Sala: curry over rice for 60 to 70 THB.
  • Koh Phi Phi

    Garlic 1992 in Tonsai keeps village plates near town prices.
  • Koh Samui & Gulf

    Samui food is Chaweng nights and a few island plates worth a Grab.
  • Koh Tao

    An 80 THB massaman in Sairee and roast duck noodles at 995.
  • Krabi & Andaman

    Krabi food is Ao Nang seafood after 17:00 and a cheaper lunch clock in Town.
  • Kua Kling (Koh Lanta) คั่วกลิ้ง

    Kua kling is a dry turmeric and chili curry from the south. Krua Dan Tai in Saladan cooks it with no toning down.

L

  • Lam (Chiang Mai) หลาม

    Lam is a Northern soup cooked in a bamboo tube. You see it at festival tables and a few Lanna rooms, not at every lunch stall.
  • Lamai Night Market (Koh Samui) ตลาดกลางคืนละไม

    Lamai night market is a smaller grill strip than Chaweng. It fits if Lamai is already your sand. Plates run 60 to 150 THB.
  • Larb Kua (Chiang Mai) ลาบคั่ว

    Larb kua is Northern cooked mince known for blood and dry spice. It is not the lime Isan salad on Sukhumvit.
  • Larb Kua (Chiang Rai) ลาบคั่ว

    Larb kua is northern minced-meat salad, drier and less minty than Isan larb. It fits a Chiang Rai table with sticky rice.
  • Larb Moo (Bangkok) ลาบหมู

    Larb moo is minced pork salad known for toasted rice, lime, and chili. Isan larb is hotter and sourer than the northern cooked mince.
  • Larb Moo (Khon Kaen) ลาบหมู

    Larb moo is minced pork salad with toasted rice and mint. It fits a Khao Suan Kwang table beside the grilled bird, hotter than northern larb.
  • Larb Moo (Udon Thani) ลาบหมู

    Larb moo is minced pork salad with toasted rice. It fits a UD Town table with sticky rice.

M

  • Mama Tom Yum (Bangkok) มาม่าต้มยำ

    Mama tom yum is instant noodles recooked as a seafood pot. Jeh O Chula on Banthat Thong is the named late bowl.
  • Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. Mae Varee on Thong Lo is the named box at 80 to 150 THB. · Bangkok
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Bangkok) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is ripe nam dok mai mango with coconut sticky rice. It is a season plate, not a July hunt.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Chiang Mai) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is a season dessert here too. Buy it when nam dok mai is in, not as a July hunt.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Hua Hin) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Hua Hin night-market box at 80 to 120 THB is the check.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Koh Samui) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Lamai town shop at 80 to 120 THB is the check against a beach cart.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Krabi) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. It fits an Ao Nang cart when mango is in, at 80 to 120 THB.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Nong Khai) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Nong Khai town box at 80 to 120 THB is the check.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Pattaya) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Jomtien town box at 80 to 120 THB is the check against Beach Road carts.
  • Mango Sticky Rice (Sukhothai) ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

    Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. An Old Sukhothai fruit shop at 80 to 120 THB is the afternoon check.
  • Massaman Curry แกงมัสมั่น

    Muslim-root curry with beef or chicken, milder than a southern sour curry. · South
  • Massaman Curry (Bangkok) แกงมัสมั่น

    Massaman is a Muslim-root curry with potato and peanut. It is milder than a southern sour curry.
  • Massaman Curry (Koh Lanta) แกงมัสมั่น

    Massaman is a mild curry with potato and peanut. Family-run Yawee in Saladan is the Lanta table people return to for it.
  • Massaman Curry (Koh Phi Phi) แกงมัสมั่น

    Massaman is the mild potato and peanut curry. Papaya Restaurant in Tonsai serves big Thai and Indian plates at budget prices.
  • Massaman Curry (Koh Tao) แกงมัสมั่น

    Massaman is the mild potato and peanut curry. Blue Chair in Sairee is known for it, and the pot often runs out after 19:00.
  • Mee Hokkien (Phuket) หมี่ฮกเกี้ยน

    Mee hokkien is Phuket Town yellow noodles in a dark wok gravy. It fits a weekday lunch on Thalang, not a Patong beach grill.
  • Mekong Fish (Nong Khai) ปลาย่างโขง

    Mekong grilled fish is a Nong Khai river plate. It fits a rimkhong dinner after nam nuang, if you price the fish first.
  • Michelin Street Food

    Bib Gourmand rooms are the value badge. Jay Fai is the one-star exception.
  • Moo Hong หมูฮ้อง

    Phuket stewed pork with star anise. Order it in Old Town, not on a Patong beach grill. · Phuket
  • Moo Hong (Phuket) หมูฮ้อง

    Moo hong is Phuket stewed pork with star anise and soy. It fits a sit-down Town lunch in a shophouse, not a Kata grill.
  • Moo Ping (Bangkok) หมูปิ้ง

    Moo ping is grilled pork on a stick with sticky rice. It is a morning market snack, not a sit-down dinner.
  • Moo Ping (Chiang Mai) หมูปิ้ง

    Moo ping is grilled pork on a stick, known for its sweet garlic marinade. Warorot morning grills are the Chiang Mai buy.
  • Moo Ping (Chiang Rai) หมูปิ้ง

    Moo ping is grilled pork on a stick. It fits a Chiang Rai night-bazaar snack after 18:00.
  • Moo Ping (Kanchanaburi) หมูปิ้ง

    Moo ping is sweet grilled pork on a stick with sticky rice. It fits a late dinner at Kanchanaburi Night Market by the bus terminal.
  • Moo Tod (Koh Lipe) หมูทอด

    Moo tod is garlic deep-fried pork. Thai Derm on Walking Street is the busy family room where locals eat it with rice.

N

  • Naem (Chiang Mai) แหนม

    Naem is sour fermented pork eaten with ginger, peanut, and raw chili. It is a beer snack and a market bite.
  • Naklua Fish Market (Pattaya) ตลาดปลานาเกลือ

    Naklua fish market is the north-Pattaya morning market. It fits a grill after you pick the fish. A 2026 fish plus grill fee is often 200 to 400 THB.
  • Nam Ngiao (Chiang Mai) ขนมจีนน้ำเงี้ยว

    Kanom jeen nam ngiao is Tai Yai rice-noodle soup known for its tomato and pork broth. It is not southern nam ya.
  • Nam Nuang (Nong Khai) แหนมเนือง

    Nam nuang is Vietnamese-style grilled pork wraps with herbs and a sweet sauce. Daeng Namnuang on Meechai is the named Nong Khai table.
  • Nam Prik Kapi (Bangkok) น้ำพริกกะปิ

    Nam prik kapi is shrimp-paste chili relish with fried fish and raw veg. It is a rice-table plate, not a dip for chips.
  • Nam Prik Kha (Chiang Mai) น้ำพริกข่า

    Nam prik kha is a galangal chili dip served with steamed mushrooms. Huen Muan Jai is the named room for it.
  • Nam Prik Kung Siap (Phuket) น้ำพริกกุ้งเสียบ

    Nam prik kung siap is a Phuket dried-shrimp relish with raw vegetables. It fits a Baba table, not a first som tum.
  • Nam Prik Noom (Chiang Mai) น้ำพริกหนุ่ม

    Nam prik noom is a smoky green chili dip. Buy it at Warorot and eat it with sticky rice and boiled veg or pork crackling.
  • Nam Prik Ong (Chiang Mai) น้ำพริกอ่อง

    Nam prik ong is a pork-and-tomato dip with boiled veg and pork crackling. It is a Lanna table plate, not a snack cup.
  • Nam Prik Ong (Chiang Rai) น้ำพริกอ่อง

    Nam prik ong is a northern pork-and-tomato dip with raw vegetables. It fits a Chiang Rai sit-down Lanna table, not a night-bazaar stick.
  • Nam Prik Ta Daeng (Chiang Mai) น้ำพริกตาแดง

    Nam prik ta daeng is a sharp red chili relish. It is a Lanna heat plate for rice and boiled veg, not a first dip.
  • Nam Tok (Bangkok) น้ำตก

    Nam tok is grilled-beef salad known for the same lime, chili, and toasted-rice dressing as larb. Pick it if you want chunkier meat, with sticky rice on the side.
  • Nam Tok (Khon Kaen) น้ำตก

    Nam tok is grilled-beef salad in the same herb family as larb. It fits the Khao Suan Kwang table with sticky rice.
  • Nang Nual Seafood (Pattaya) นางนวล

    Nang Nual is the long-run Jomtien seafood house. It fits a gulf dinner if you want a named room, not a Walking Street grill.
  • Night Markets

    Night markets that matter are Ratchada, Train Night Market, and city walking streets.
  • Northern Lanna

    Northern Lanna food is khao soi, larb, and sai ua at lunch.

O

  • Oh Aew (Phuket) โอวเอ๋ว

    Oh aew is Phuket fig-seed jelly with shaved ice and syrup. It fits an afternoon after a Thalang curry, not a dinner plan.
  • Or Tor Kor

    Or Tor Kor is graded fruit at MRT Kamphaeng Phet.

P

  • Pa Tong Go (Bangkok) ปาท่องโก๋

    Pa tong go is Chinese-style fried dough eaten with condensed milk or savory dips. Pa Tong Go Savoey is the named Yaowarat shop.
  • Pad Kee Mao (Bangkok) ผัดขี้เมา

    Pad kee mao is the wide-noodle drunken wok with chili and holy basil. It is a late plate, hotter than pad see ew.
  • Pad Kra Pao ผัดกะเพรา

    Holy-basil wok with minced pork or chicken and a fried egg. A 2026 plate is often 60 to 90 THB. · Bangkok
  • Pad Kra Pao (Bangkok) ผัดกะเพรา

    Pad kra pao is the holy-basil wok with minced pork or chicken and a fried egg. It is the usual first Bangkok lunch.
  • Pad Kra Pao (Koh Tao) ผัดกะเพรา

    Pad kra pao is holy basil stir-fry over rice. It fits a quick plate at Long Thai Food near Mae Haad pier.
  • Pad See Ew (Bangkok) ผัดซีอิ๊ว

    Pad see ew is wide rice noodles in dark soy with Chinese broccoli and egg. It is the mild wok if kra pao is too hot.
  • Pad Thai ผัดไทย

    Thip Samai on Maha Chai is the late classic plate, usually 80 to 150 THB. · Bangkok
  • Pad Thai (Bangkok) ผัดไทย

  • Pad Thai (Pai) ผัดไทย

  • Pattaya

    Jomtien seafood and Naklua fish, not a Walking Street default.

    Hokkien and Baba-Nyonya in Old Town, not a beach grill.

  • Phuket Old Town food

    Old Town lunch is Hokkien mee and moo hong, not a beach grill.
  • Pla Pao (Bangkok) ปลาเผา

  • Pla Pao (Hua Hin) ปลาเผา

    Pla pao is salt-crust grilled fish. It fits an Ao Nang night after 17:00 if you price the fish by weight first.

  • Pla Som (Chiang Mai) ปลาส้ม

    Pla som is sour fermented fish, usually fried. It is a Northern and Isan sour plate, eaten with sticky rice and raw veg.
  • Poo Pad Pong Karee (Koh Kood) ปูผัดผงกะหรี่

    Poo pad pong karee is crab stir-fried with curry powder and egg. It fits an Ao Yai lunch at Chonthicha, priced by weight.
  • Pork Satay (Bangkok) สะเต๊ะหมู

    Thai satay is grilled pork or chicken with peanut sauce and cucumber relish. It is a snack row, not a main.
  • Pu Ob Woon Sen (Bangkok) ปูอบวุ้นเส้น

R

  • Rad Na (Bangkok) ราดหน้า

  • Railay Seafood Curry (Krabi) แกงส้มไร่เลย์

  • Rooftops and river

    Rooftops are dress-code nights over the river.
  • Rotee Thaew Nam (Phuket) โรตีแถวน้ำ

  • Roti Mataba (Bangkok) โรตีมะตะบะ

  • Roti Sai Mai (Ayutthaya) โรตีสายไหม

    Roti sai mai is a thin roti wrapped around candy floss. Abeedeen-Pranom Sangaroon opposite Ayutthaya Hospital is the named 70-year stall.

    Sai ua is northern herb sausage. It fits a Chiang Rai night-bazaar grill after the White Temple day, with sticky rice.

  • Som Tum (Khao Lak) ส้มตำ

  • Som Tum (Khon Kaen) ส้มตำ

  • Som Tum (Koh Lipe) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is pounded green papaya salad. It fits a Walking Street lunch at Papaya Mom.
  • Som Tum (Koh Phangan) ส้มตำ

  • Som Tum (Nong Khai) ส้มตำ

  • Som Tum (Pattaya) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. It fits a Jomtien lunch with grilled chicken, not a Walking Street cocktail.
  • Som Tum (Udon Thani) ส้มตำ

    Som tum is green papaya salad. At UD Town, order Thai first. Udon heat is not a Bangkok tom yum.
  • Som Tum Poo Plara (Bangkok) ส้มตำปูปลาร้า

    Som tum poo pla ra is papaya salad with field crab and fermented fish. It is the Isan plate, not a first som tum.
  • Som Tum Thai (Bangkok) ส้มตำไทย

    Som tum Thai is green papaya salad with peanut and dried shrimp. Order this first. Thai hot is a different heat.
  • South and Baba-Nyonya

    Southern rooms cook sour curry and mansion Baba plates.
  • Southern Yam (Hat Yai) ยำใต้

    Southern yam is a sour seafood salad. Tai Khoi Soi Ton in Khuan Lang is the named Hat Yai yam room, closed Monday.
  • Sticky Rice (Chiang Mai) ข้าวเหนียว

    Khao niew is sticky rice, the utensil for sausage, dips, and grilled pork. Ask for a basket, not jasmine, on a Lanna table.
  • Sukhothai

    Sukhothai noodles after the dawn bicycle in Old Town.
  • Sukhothai Noodles (Sukhothai) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวสุโขทัย

    Kuay tiew Sukhothai is thin rice noodles with pork, green beans, lime, and crushed peanut. It fits an Old Sukhothai lunch after the dawn bicycle.

T

  • Tam Khanun (Chiang Mai) ตำขนุน

    Tam khanun is a Northern shredded jackfruit salad. It is a Lanna som tum cousin without green papaya.
  • Tam Mua (Khon Kaen) ตำมั่ว

    Tam mua is a mixed Isan pound, often papaya with noodles, pork, and fermented fish. It fits eaters who already finished a Thai som tum.
  • Thai Suki (Bangkok) สุกี้

    Thai suki is glass noodles, greens, egg, and meat known for a sweet-hot chili dipping sauce. Elvis Suki on Soi Yotse is the Bib Gourmand pick for a wok-fried plate.
  • Tod Mun Khao Pod (Chiang Mai) ทอดมันข้าวโพด

    Tod mun khao pod is fried sweetcorn cakes. Walking-street and market bags are the Chiang Mai snack, not a fish cake.
  • Tod Mun Pla (Bangkok) ทอดมันปลา

    Tod mun pla is fried fish cakes known for a bouncy bite and cucumber relish. Order a small plate to share before a curry.
  • Tom Kha Gai (Bangkok) ต้มข่าไก่

    Tom kha gai is coconut chicken soup known for its galangal scent. It suits anyone who finds tom yum too sour or too hot.
  • Tom Kha Gai (Koh Phangan) ต้มข่าไก่

    Tom kha gai is coconut chicken soup with galangal. At Phantip it is the creamy bowl to order beside the grills.
  • Tom Lued Moo (Hat Yai) ต้มเลือดหมู

    Tom lued moo is a clear pork-blood soup. Siri on Sri Phadung Withee is the named Hat Yai bowl at 60 to 90 THB.
  • Tom Yum (Koh Lipe) ต้มยำ

    Tom yum is hot and sour soup. It fits an early Thai Derm dinner if you like heat.
  • Tom Yum (Koh Phi Phi) ต้มยำ

    Tom yum is the hot and sour soup. At Garlic 1992 it is a cheap bowl in a village where most menus charge resort prices.
  • Tom Yum Goong ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Hot and sour prawn soup. Ask for nam khon if you want the creamy coconut version. · Central
  • Tom Yum Goong (Bangkok) ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Tom yum goong is hot-and-sour prawn soup. Nam khon is the creamy coconut bowl. Nam sai is the clear, sharper one.
  • Tom Yum Goong (Kanchanaburi) ต้มยำกุ้ง

    Tom yum goong is hot and sour prawn soup. It fits a river lunch at Keeree Tara, about 100 m from the River Kwai bridge.
  • Tom Yum Noodles (Bangkok) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวต้มยำ

    Guay tiew tom yum is a hot-and-sour noodle bowl, creamier than clear tom yum soup. It is a lunch stall, not a hotel pot.
  • Tom Yum Talay (Koh Kood) ต้มยำทะเล

    Tom yum talay is hot and sour seafood soup. It fits a Klong Chao dinner at Nuch Leuang near the hotels.
  • Tub Tim Grob (Bangkok) ทับทิมกรอบ

    Tub tim grob is water-chestnut cubes in coconut milk over ice. It is a Bangkok heat-break cup, not a bakery cake.

U

  • UD Town Grills (Udon Thani) ยูดีทาวน์

    UD Town grills are the Udon Thani night-food yard. It fits a first city dinner for som tum, sai krok, and iced drinks at 40 to 120 THB.

W

  • Walking Street (Nong Khai) ถนนคนเดินหนองคาย

    Nong Khai walking street is the Saturday snack walk by the Mekong. It fits cups at 30 to 80 THB, not a nam nuang substitute.
  • Walking Street Grills (Pattaya) วอล์คกิ้งสตรีท

    Walking Street grills are late central-Pattaya stalls. They fit only if that night is already the trip, not a first-week Andaman substitute.
  • Wang Lang and Talat Noi

    Wang Lang is the ferry lunch; Talat Noi is the morning lane.

Y

  • Yam Hua Plee (Khao Lak) ยำหัวปลี

    Yam hua plee is banana blossom salad. It fits a sit-down dinner at Takiang if you want more than the classics.
  • Yam Nang Moo (Chiang Mai) ยำหนังหมู

    Yam nang moo is a pork-skin salad with herbs and chili. It is a beer plate on a Northern table, not a diet bowl.
  • Yaowarat walk

    The Yaowarat walk starts after 18:00 from MRT Wat Mangkon.
  • Yen Ta Fo (Bangkok) เย็นตาโฟ

    Yen ta fo is a pink, slightly sweet-sour noodle soup with fish balls and morning glory. It is a Bangkok lunch bowl.
  • Yen Ta Fo (Kanchanaburi) เย็นตาโฟ

    Yen ta fo is pink noodle soup coloured with fermented red tofu. In Kanchanaburi it is a Saeng Chuto Road cart bowl near the bus terminal.
