Koh Lanta food guide

Koh Lanta food is southern heat in Saladan and a sour curry lunch in Old Town. Ask for less chili on a first plate.

Kua klingMassamanGaeng som Thai food hub

Photo: T.Tseng (CC BY 2.0)

Southern heatKrua Dan Tai, Saladan; 11:00 to 22:00
MassamanYawee; 50 to 100 THB, cash only
Old TownFresh; dinner for two under 400 THB
ClockSaladan at night, Old Town by day

Saladan: Krua Dan Tai and Yawee sit on Highway 4245 near the pier.

Old Town: Fresh is on Sri Raya Road on the east coast. For the island map use Koh Lanta.

Specialties

  • Kua Kling
  • Massaman Curry
  • Gaeng Som

Kua Kling คั่วกลิ้ง

Kua kling is a dry turmeric and chili curry from the south. Krua Dan Tai in Saladan cooks it with no toning down. Krua Dan Tai is the named start. Budget roadside prices.

Krua Dan Tai, 616 Moo 3, Highway 4245, Saladan, Koh Lanta, Krabi 81150

11:00 to 22:00 daily · Budget roadside prices

Massaman Curry แกงมัสมั่น

Massaman is a mild curry with potato and peanut. Family-run Yawee in Saladan is the Lanta table people return to for it. Yawee Restaurant is the named start. 50 to 100 THB.

Yawee Restaurant, Highway 4245, Saladan, Koh Lanta, Krabi 81150

09:30 to 22:00 daily · 50 to 100 THB

Gaeng Som แกงส้ม

Gaeng som is hot and sour fish curry. Fresh in Lanta Old Town is the room travel guides single out for it. Fresh Restaurant is the named start. Dinner for two under 400 THB.

Fresh Restaurant, 33 Moo 2, Sri Raya Rd, Lanta Old Town, Koh Lanta, Krabi 81150

09:00 to 22:00 daily · Dinner for two under 400 THB
