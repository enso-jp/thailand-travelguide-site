Pattaya food guide

Pattaya food is known for Jomtien seafood if you ask the weight price. It fits an east-gulf night, not a first-week Andaman substitute.

JomtienNakluaSom tum Thai food hub

Photo: iMahesh (CC BY-SA 4.0)

JomtienNang Nual and beach grills; price by weight
NakluaMorning fish market, then a grill fee
Walking StreetLate mark-up; eat a proper lunch first
Som tum60 to 100 THB with sticky rice

Eat in Jomtien if you came to swim. Nang Nual near the pier is the named seafood house. Naklua is the north market if you sleep Wongamat. Walking Street is a late snack strip, not the best fish.

For the stay split use Pattaya. Hua Hin is the train-town gulf, a different weekend.

Nang Nual Seafood นางนวล

Nang Nual is the long-run Jomtien seafood house. It fits a gulf dinner if you want a named room, not a Walking Street grill. Nang Nual Jomtien is the named start. 300 to 800 THB a table.

Nang Nual Jomtien, Jomtien Beach Rd near the pier, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
11:00 to 22:00 · 300 to 800 THB a table

Jomtien Grilled Fish ปลาเผาจอมเทียน

Jomtien grilled fish is a calmer gulf plate than Walking Street. It fits if you already sleep on that sand. Jomtien Beach road grills is the named start. 200 to 400 THB.

Jomtien Beach road grills, Jomtien Beach Rd, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
16:00 to 22:30 · 200 to 400 THB

Naklua Fish Market ตลาดปลานาเกลือ

Naklua fish market is the north-Pattaya morning market. It fits a grill after you pick the fish. A 2026 fish plus grill fee is often 200 to 400 THB. Naklua fish market is the named start. 200 to 400 THB a fish plus grill.

Naklua fish market, Naklua, north Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
Morning market; grill rooms through lunch · 200 to 400 THB a fish plus grill

Walking Street Grills วอล์คกิ้งสตรีท

Walking Street grills are late central-Pattaya stalls. They fit only if that night is already the trip, not a first-week Andaman substitute. Walking Street stalls is the named start. 150 to 400 THB.

Walking Street stalls, Walking Street, Central Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
19:00 to late · 150 to 400 THB

Som Tum ส้มตำ

Som tum is green papaya salad. It fits a Jomtien lunch with grilled chicken, not a Walking Street cocktail. Jomtien Isan lunch rooms is the named start. 60 to 100 THB.

Jomtien Isan lunch rooms, Jomtien Beach Rd side sois, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
10:00 to 20:00 · 60 to 100 THB

Khao Niew ข้าวเหนียว

Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for som tum and grilled chicken in Pattaya Isan rooms. Jomtien Isan lunch rooms is the named start. 10 to 22 THB.

Jomtien Isan lunch rooms, Jomtien Beach Rd side sois, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
With the som tum · 10 to 22 THB

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Jomtien town box at 80 to 120 THB is the check against Beach Road carts. Jomtien fruit shops is the named start. 80 to 120 THB.

Jomtien fruit shops, Jomtien town fruit shops, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
10:00 to 20:00 · 80 to 120 THB

Thepprasit Weekend Market เทพประสิทธิ์

Thepprasit is known as Pattaya weekend stall street for cheap plates and clothes. It fits Friday to Sunday. A named seafood room is Nang Nual in Jomtien, 11:00 to 22:00. Thepprasit Road market is the named start. Stall plates; Nang Nual 300 to 800 THB a table.

Thepprasit Road market, Thepprasit Rd, Pattaya, Chonburi
Stall plates; Nang Nual 300 to 800 THB a table

Jomtien Seafood Table จอมเทียน

Jomtien seafood is known for Nang Nual near the pier. It fits a gulf dinner from 11:00 to 22:00, often 300 to 800 THB a table if you price the fish. Nang Nual Jomtien is the named start. 300 to 800 THB a table.

Nang Nual Jomtien, Jomtien Beach Rd near the pier, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150
11:00 to 22:00 · 300 to 800 THB a table

Naklua and Jomtien Grilled Fish นาเกลือจอมเทียน

Naklua and Jomtien are the two Pattaya fish clocks. Jomtien has Nang Nual, 11:00 to 22:00. Naklua is the north market if that is the sand you slept on. Naklua fish market or Nang Nual is the named start. Jomtien tables 300 to 800 THB.

Naklua fish market or Nang Nual, Naklua, North Pattaya, or Jomtien Beach Rd
Nang Nual 11:00 to 22:00 · Jomtien tables 300 to 800 THB

Walking Street Food ถนนคนเดินพัทยา

Pattaya Walking Street is known for a late strip, not a market price. Beach Road closes to cars from 19:00 to 06:00. Eat fish in Jomtien first if the bill matters. Pattaya Walking Street is the named start. Marked up; Jomtien tables 300 to 800 THB.

Pattaya Walking Street, Beach Rd to Bali Hai Pier, South Pattaya
Closed to traffic 19:00 to 06:00 · Marked up; Jomtien tables 300 to 800 THB

Koh Larn Day Food อาหารเกาะล้าน

Koh Larn food is a day-trip plate after a ferry from Bali Hai. The ferry is 30 to 40 THB and about 45 minutes. It fits a beach lunch, not a Pattaya dinner plan. Koh Larn beach restaurants is the named start. Ferry 30 to 40 THB; Jomtien dinner 300 to 800 THB.

Koh Larn beach restaurants, Koh Larn, boats from Bali Hai Pier, Pattaya
Ferry 30 to 40 THB; Jomtien dinner 300 to 800 THB

Beef Noodle Soup ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเนื้อ

Beef noodle soup here is a long-simmered beef broth, known for a clear bowl and an amber bowl with several cuts in one order. La Voi on Chaiyaphruek Road is open Tuesday to Sunday and closed Monday. La Voi is the named start. 150 THB for the mixed bowl.

La Voi, 324/61 Chaiyaphruek Rd, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chon Buri 20150
Tue to Fri 09:00 to 18:00; Sat and Sun 09:00 to 16:30; closed Monday · 150 THB for the mixed bowl
