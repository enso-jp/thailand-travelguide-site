Phuket food guide

Phuket food is known for Hokkien and Baba-Nyonya in Old Town. It fits a weekday lunch inland, not a Patong beach grill after a boat day.

Old TownHokkienBaba-Nyonya Thai food hub

Eat Phuket in Town. Hokkien mee, moo hong, and oh aew live on Thalang, Dibuk, and Krabi Roads. A 2026 noodle plate is 60 to 90 THB. Mansion rooms such as The Charm take a phone booking for a Baba table. Sunday walking street on Thalang is snacks, not a full dinner.

Patong and Rawai grills are a different bill. Confirm seafood by weight. Named dish pages start at Kopitiam mee Hokkien, Raya moo hong, and Tu Kab Khao oh aew. For the shophouse map use Phuket Old Town. For sour curry and the wider south use South and Baba-Nyonya.

Mee Hokkien หมี่ฮกเกี้ยน

Mee hokkien is Phuket Town yellow noodles in a dark wok gravy. It fits a weekday lunch on Thalang, not a Patong beach grill. Kopitiam by Wilai is the named start. 105 to 125 THB.

Kopitiam by Wilai, 18 Thalang Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
11:00 to 20:00 Mon to Sat; Sunday rest · 105 to 125 THB

Moo Hong หมูฮ้อง

Moo hong is Phuket stewed pork with star anise and soy. It fits a sit-down Town lunch in a shophouse, not a Kata grill. Raya Restaurant is the named start. 120 to 180 THB.

Raya Restaurant, 48 Dibuk Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
10:00 to 22:00 · 120 to 180 THB

Oh Aew โอวเอ๋ว

Oh aew is Phuket fig-seed jelly with shaved ice and syrup. It fits an afternoon after a Thalang curry, not a dinner plan. Lock Tien food court is the named start. 40 to 60 THB.

Lock Tien food court, Thalang Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
Food-court hours; afternoon is the useful clock · 40 to 60 THB

Kanom Jeen Nam Ya ขนมจีนน้ำยา

Kanom jeen nam ya is rice noodles in a southern fish curry. It fits a Phuket Town morning market, and pots empty by early afternoon. Ranong Road wet market kanom jeen shops is the named start. 40 to 60 THB.

Ranong Road wet market kanom jeen shops, Ranong Rd wet market, Phuket Town
07:00 to 13:00 · 40 to 60 THB

Gaeng Som แกงส้ม

Gaeng som is sour southern curry with fish or prawn. It fits a Phuket Town mansion table, hotter than a Bangkok gaeng. Tu Kab Khao is the named start. 80 to 150 THB.

Tu Kab Khao, 20 Krabi Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
11:00 to 21:00 · 80 to 150 THB

Gaeng Poo Bai Chaplu แกงปูใบชะพลู

Gaeng poo bai chaplu is Phuket crab curry with wild betel leaf. It fits a mansion table on Krabi Road when you want a sit-down pot. Tu Kab Khao is the named start. 180 to 320 THB.

Tu Kab Khao, 20 Krabi Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
11:00 to 21:00 · 180 to 320 THB

Nam Prik Kung Siap น้ำพริกกุ้งเสียบ

Nam prik kung siap is a Phuket dried-shrimp relish with raw vegetables. It fits a Baba table, not a first som tum. The Charm Dining Gallery is the named start. 150 to 300 THB.

The Charm Dining Gallery, 32 Krabi Rd, Talat Nuea, Phuket
11:30 to 21:00; phone ahead · 150 to 300 THB

Rotee Thaew Nam โรตีแถวน้ำ

Rotee Thaew Nam is charcoal roti with massaman or fish curry. It fits a Phuket Town morning before the heat. Rotee Thaew Nam is the named start. 30 to 60 THB.

Rotee Thaew Nam, Phuket Town morning roti row near the Old Town grid
Morning charcoal; go before 10:00 · 30 to 60 THB

Go Benz Jok โจ๊กโกเบนซ์

Go Benz jok is dry rice porridge with crispy pork. It fits a Phuket Town evening that is not Monday. Go Benz is the named start. 70 to 100 THB.

Go Benz, Phuket Town; evenings except Monday
Evenings except Monday · 70 to 100 THB

Phuket Kopi กาแฟภูเก็ต

Phuket kopi is Old Town drip or iced coffee with condensed milk. It fits a morning before a Thalang lunch. Kopi de Phuket is the named start. 40 to 70 THB.

Kopi de Phuket, 61 Phuket Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
08:00 to 18:00 · 40 to 70 THB

Sunday Walking Street ถนนคนเดินภูเก็ต

Thalang Road on Sunday late afternoon. Snacks, not a full dinner plan. 30 to 80 THB.

Thalang Rd, Talat Yai, Phuket 83000
Sunday from 16:00 · 30 to 80 THB

Lobster grill (only if priced) กุ้งมังกร

Rawai and Chalong desks quote by weight. Confirm the THB number before the grill and walk away from a shrug.

Rawai seafood promenade, Viset Rd, Rawai, Phuket 83130
16:00 to 22:00 · By weight; ask first

Common questions

Should you eat on the beach?
For a sunset drink, yes. For Hokkien mee and moo hong, no. Those plates live in Town at lunch prices.
How do you avoid a seafood surprise?
Ask the weight price in THB before the grill. Walk away from a shrug. 2026 fair plates are often 200 to 400 THB, not a silent 2,000.
