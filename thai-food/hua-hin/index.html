Hua Hin food guide

Hua Hin food is known for the Soi 72 night market after the train. It fits a town dinner, or a Khao Takiab grill if you already sleep south.

Soi 72SeafoodCicada Thai food hub

Photo: Elmschrat (CC0)

Night marketSoi 72, 17:00 to 23:00, 50 to 120 THB
FishNaresdamri rooms; price by weight
CicadaFriday to Sunday, 16:00 to 23:00
TrainSpecial Express 43 is the useful morning set

Soi 72 is the every-night grill if you sleep in town. Plates 50 to 120 THB. Naresdamri rooms do sit-down fish if you ask the weight price. Cicada is Friday to Sunday toward Khao Takiab, snacks not a whole dinner.

For the stay split use Hua Hin. Pattaya is a different gulf. Do not treat them as the same weekend.

Hua Hin Night Market ตลาดโต้รุ่งหัวหิน

Hua Hin night market is the Soi 72 grill street. It fits a first town dinner after the train, with plates at 50 to 120 THB. Hua Hin Night Market is the named start. 50 to 120 THB.

Hua Hin Night Market, Soi 72 / Dechanuchit Rd, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
17:00 to 23:00 · 50 to 120 THB

Hoi Tod หอยทอด

Hoi tod is a crisp oyster omelette. It fits a Hua Hin night-market share plate on Soi 72. Hua Hin Night Market stalls is the named start. 80 to 140 THB.

Hua Hin Night Market stalls, Soi 72 / Dechanuchit Rd, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
17:00 to 23:00 · 80 to 140 THB

Pla Pao ปลาเผา

Pla pao is salt-crust grilled fish. It fits a Hua Hin town dinner if you price the fish by weight first. Hua Hin town seafood rooms on Naresdamri is the named start. 200 to 400 THB a fair plate.

Hua Hin town seafood rooms on Naresdamri, Naresdamri Rd, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
11:00 to 22:00 · 200 to 400 THB a fair plate

Khao Takiab Seafood อาหารทะเลเขาตะเกียบ

Khao Takiab seafood is the hill-end grill strip south of town. It fits if you already sleep toward Cicada, not as a first-night taxi from the station. Khao Takiab beach restaurants is the named start. 200 to 400 THB.

Khao Takiab beach restaurants, Khao Takiab beach road, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
11:00 to 22:00 · 200 to 400 THB

Cicada Snacks ตลาดจั๊กจั่น

Cicada Market is the Friday to Sunday craft night toward Khao Takiab. It fits snack cups at 40 to 100 THB, not a full seafood dinner. Cicada Market is the named start. 40 to 100 THB.

Cicada Market, Toward Khao Takiab, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
Friday to Sunday, 16:00 to 23:00 · 40 to 100 THB

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Hua Hin night-market box at 80 to 120 THB is the check. Hua Hin Night Market dessert stalls is the named start. 80 to 120 THB.

Hua Hin Night Market dessert stalls, Soi 72 / Dechanuchit Rd, Hua Hin, Prachuap Khiri Khan 77110
17:00 to 23:00 · 80 to 120 THB

Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่

Khao man gai is Hainan chicken rice. It fits a Hua Hin town lunch at 50 to 70 THB before the beach hour. Town lunch shophouses near the station is the named start. 50 to 70 THB.

Town lunch shophouses near the station, Near Hua Hin railway station, Hua Hin 77110
08:00 to 15:00 · 50 to 70 THB
