Koh Lipe food guide

Koh Lipe food is known for Walking Street. It fits an early dinner, since Thai Derm fills after 19:30.

Walking StreetSom tumThai Derm Thai food hub

Photo: Vyacheslav Argenberg (CC BY 4.0)

Thai DermWalking Street; 16:00 to 02:00
Papaya Mom09:00 to 22:00; dishes 80 to 150 THB
QueueArrive before 19:30
CashCarry small notes

Walking Street: Papaya Mom and Thai Derm sit on the same strip.

Heat: Thai Derm cooks spicy. Say pet nit noi. For the island map use Koh Lipe.

Som Tum ส้มตำ

Som tum is pounded green papaya salad. It fits a Walking Street lunch at Papaya Mom. Papaya Mom is the named start. 80 to 150 THB a dish.

Papaya Mom, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
09:00 to 22:00 daily · 80 to 150 THB a dish

Moo Tod หมูทอด

Moo tod is garlic deep-fried pork. Thai Derm on Walking Street is the busy family room where locals eat it with rice. Thai Derm Restaurant is the named start. Budget Walking Street prices.

Thai Derm Restaurant, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
16:00 to 02:00 daily · Budget Walking Street prices

Tom Yum ต้มยำ

Tom yum is hot and sour soup. It fits an early Thai Derm dinner if you like heat. Thai Derm Restaurant is the named start. Budget Walking Street prices.

Thai Derm Restaurant, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
16:00 to 02:00 daily · Budget Walking Street prices

Seafood Buffet บุฟเฟ่ต์อาหารทะเล

Progress Restaurant's pick-your-seafood grill is Koh Lipe Walking Street's group dinner, at about 490 THB a person. Progress Restaurant is the named start. About 490 THB a person.

Progress Restaurant, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
About 490 THB a person

Southern Seafood อาหารทะเลปักษ์ใต้

Daya on Pattaya Beach is the southern seafood room locals recommend to each other, with bold curries and fish off the grill. Daya Restaurant is the named start. 120 to 250 THB a dish; fish by weight.

Daya Restaurant, Pattaya Beach, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
120 to 250 THB a dish; fish by weight

Sour Fish Curry แกงส้มปลา

Gaeng som pla, the south's sour fish curry, is the order at Rak Lay, a long-running seafood room on Koh Lipe Walking Street. Rak Lay is the named start. 150 to 350 THB a dish.

Rak Lay, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
150 to 350 THB a dish

Banana Roti โรตีกล้วย

Banana roti, crisp flatbread folded around banana and egg, is the late sweet of Koh Lipe Walking Street, 40 to 80 THB from the dessert stalls. Walking Street roti stalls is the named start. 40 to 80 THB.

Walking Street roti stalls, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
40 to 80 THB

Halal Thai Plates อาหารฮาลาล

Sanae Lipe Halal Cafe on Walking Street is a halal kitchen serving Thai plates with no pork, an easy choice for Muslim travellers on Koh Lipe. Sanae Lipe Halal Cafe is the named start. 80 to 150 THB is the street band.

Sanae Lipe Halal Cafe, Walking Street, Koh Lipe, Satun 91000
80 to 150 THB is the street band
