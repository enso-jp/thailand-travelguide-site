Nong Khai food guide

Nong Khai food is known for nam nuang wraps on Meechai. It fits a Mekong day, not a same-night UD Town crawl.

Nam nuangMekongWalking street Thai food hub

Photo: sirinthorn phaopongthai (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Nam nuangDaeng Namnuang, 689/1 Meechai; 120 to 220 THB
River fish200 to 400 THB if you price it
Walking streetSaturday from 16:00; snacks 30 to 80 THB
From UdonA separate Mekong day

Daeng Namnuang at 689/1 Meechai is the named wrap table at 120 to 220 THB a set. Rimkhong rooms grill river fish if you ask the weight price. Saturday walking street is snacks, not a substitute for the wrap.

Udon is UD Town. Khon Kaen is the highway bird. Use those pages if that is where you actually are.

Nam Nuang แหนมเนือง

Nam nuang is Vietnamese-style grilled pork wraps with herbs and a sweet sauce. Daeng Namnuang on Meechai is the named Nong Khai table. Daeng Namnuang is the named start. 120 to 220 THB a set.

Daeng Namnuang, 689/1 Meechai Rd, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
10:00 to 20:00; confirm the rest day the morning you go · 120 to 220 THB a set

Mekong Fish ปลาย่างโขง

Mekong grilled fish is a Nong Khai river plate. It fits a rimkhong dinner after nam nuang, if you price the fish first. Rimkhong river restaurants is the named start. 200 to 400 THB a fair plate.

Rimkhong river restaurants, Rimkhong Rd, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
11:00 to 21:00 · 200 to 400 THB a fair plate

Walking Street ถนนคนเดินหนองคาย

Nong Khai walking street is the Saturday snack walk by the Mekong. It fits cups at 30 to 80 THB, not a nam nuang substitute. Nong Khai Walking Street is the named start. 30 to 80 THB.

Nong Khai Walking Street, Meechai and river grid, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
Saturday from 16:00 · 30 to 80 THB

Sai Krok Isan ไส้กรอกอีสาน

Sai krok Isan is sour fermented pork sausage. It fits a Nong Khai walking-street stick after nam nuang. Nong Khai Walking Street grills is the named start. 30 to 60 THB a set.

Nong Khai Walking Street grills, Meechai and river grid, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
Saturday from 16:00; night stalls on other days · 30 to 60 THB a set

Som Tum ส้มตำ

Som tum is green papaya salad. In Nong Khai, order Thai first at a river or town Isan room. Town Isan rooms off Meechai is the named start. 50 to 90 THB.

Town Isan rooms off Meechai, Meechai Rd side sois, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
10:00 to 20:00 · 50 to 90 THB

Khao Niew ข้าวเหนียว

Khao niew is sticky rice in a basket. It is the utensil for som tum in Nong Khai. Town Isan rooms off Meechai is the named start. 10 to 22 THB.

Town Isan rooms off Meechai, Meechai Rd side sois, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
With the som tum · 10 to 22 THB

Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง

Mango sticky rice is seasonal mango with coconut sticky rice. A Nong Khai town box at 80 to 120 THB is the check. Town fruit shops near Meechai is the named start. 80 to 120 THB.

Town fruit shops near Meechai, Meechai Rd, Mueang Nong Khai 43000
10:00 to 20:00 · 80 to 120 THB

Nam nuang or sai krok first?

From Udon the same evening?
Only if you already dropped the UD Town night. They are different dinners.
