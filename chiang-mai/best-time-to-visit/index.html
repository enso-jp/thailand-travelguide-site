Best time to visit Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai is known for a cooler November to February city week that fits temple walks, while March haze can sit in the valley. Songkran in mid-April is a water fight, not a calm wat morning.

November to February is the cooler dry window, and March can hold farm-burn haze. Mid-April is Songkran: water, not silk, and May to October is green and wet on the ridges. Kew Mae Pan on Doi Inthanon runs November to May.

Temple dress still applies in rain, and Wat Chedi Luang is 50 THB. Doi Suthep is 30 THB plus a 60 to 100 THB songthaew; leave at 07:00, and pair this page with when to go. A coast week is a separate monsoon choice, not a same-week stack.

Month notes

  • Nov to Feb: Cooler walks. Book Sunday-market weekends early.
  • Mar to Apr: Check air quality. Songkran is mid-April.
  • May to Oct: Green hills. Inthanon trails go slick.

Common questions

Is March a bad Chiang Mai month?
It can be if haze sits. Check air quality before you lock the hotel.
Should you skip April?
Skip a calm temple plan in Songkran week. Keep April if the water festival is why you came.
