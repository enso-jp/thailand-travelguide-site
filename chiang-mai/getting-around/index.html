Getting around Chiang Mai

Chiang Mai is known for red songthaews and short Grabs rather than a BTS grid, so sleep in the Old City or Nimman and use a hired truck for Doi Suthep. Skip a first-week scooter on the moat corners.

CNX to the Old City is 15 to 25 minutes. Book a Trip.com car if you land with bags. In town, red songthaews run shared routes; agree a charter fare before you climb in. A 2026 Grab from Nimman to the moat is often 80 to 120 THB and four to seven minutes.

Doi Suthep is 30 THB at the wat plus a songthaew at 60 to 100 THB each way, and leave at 07:00. Do not scooter the ridge in rain on a first week, and pair this page with where to stay. Route 1095 to Pai is a different day.

Which mode for which hop

  • CNX car: 15 to 25 minutes to the moat.
  • Grab: Nimman, Wat Ket, late nights.
  • Red songthaew: Shared city hops; charter for Suthep.
  • Suthep: 30 THB plus 60 to 100 THB each way. Leave 07:00.

Common questions

Grab or songthaew to Doi Suthep?
Shared red trucks from Chang Phuak or CMU are 60 to 100 THB each way. Grab costs more and leaves when you do.
Can you walk Nimman to the moat?
You can. It is hot. Grab is four to seven minutes.
