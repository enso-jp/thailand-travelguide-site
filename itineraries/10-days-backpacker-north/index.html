10 days backpacker north: Chiang Mai loop itinerary

Sleep inside the Chiang Mai moat, pay 210 to 250 THB for the Pai van, then ride the Green Bus (about 301 THB coach or 422 THB VIP). White Temple is 200 THB since 1 January 2026.

Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

Buses, not scooters, carry this north loop for travellers who want wats and ridges instead of a beach. Sleep four nights in the Old City. Doi Suthep at 07:00 is 30 THB plus songthaew 60 to 100 THB. Where to stay lists guest houses inside the moat.

Van to Pai on Route 1095 in 3 to 4.5 hours. Sleep three nights using Pai where to stay. Return to Chiang Mai, then Green Bus about 3.5 hours to Chiang Rai. White Temple day is 200 THB since 1 January 2026. Blue Temple is free. Baan Dam is a separate ticket.

Common questions

White Temple fee in 2026?
Foreign adults pay 200 THB since 1 January 2026. Confirm on the White Temple day page before you go.
Scooter the Route 1095?
Not on a first loop. The van is 3 to 4.5 hours and skips 762 bends on a hired bike.
Add Sukhothai?
Not on ten north days. Use the Sukhothai and Chiang Mai week if ruins are the point.
