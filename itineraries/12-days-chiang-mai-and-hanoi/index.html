12 days in Chiang Mai and Hanoi: hills then lakes itinerary

Walk the moat, leave at 07:00 for Doi Suthep, then fly CNX to HAN. Add one Ha Long or Ninh Binh day, not both rushed, and file the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) for the Thai half.

Chiang Mai hills and Hanoi lakes share one trip for people who skip Thai beaches. Start in Chiang Mai for six nights using where to stay or Mae Rim. Doi Suthep at 07:00 is 30 THB plus songthaew 60 to 100 THB. File the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC).

The hop to Hanoi is a separate international ticket. Book the CNX to HAN hop on Trip.com or with your airline. City and bay notes sit on Hanoi on Vietnam TravelGuide. Keep Phuket off this pair.
