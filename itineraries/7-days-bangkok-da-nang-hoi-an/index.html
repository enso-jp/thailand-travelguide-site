7 days in Bangkok, Da Nang, and Hoi An itinerary

Ticket the palace, fly BKK to DAD, and sleep Hoi An if lantern streets are the point. File the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) for Thailand and use the Vietnam e-visa portal for the second stamp.

Short hopHoi An nightsNo Thai island
Split3 nights Bangkok; 3 nights Hoi An or Da Nang
Bangkok ticketsPalace 500 THB, Wat Pho 300 THB, Wat Arun 200 THB
HopBKK to DAD on Trip.com or your airline
EntryTDAC; official Vietnam pages
Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

Three Bangkok nights and Hoi An lantern streets make a short week that skips Thai islands. Sleep three nights using Bangkok where to stay near a BTS exit, walk the old-city river temples on the first full morning (BTS 17 to 62 THB), then fly from Suvarnabhumi to Da Nang. File the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC).

Fly to Da Nang on an international ticket. Sleep Hoi An old town or a Da Nang beach. Notes sit on Da Nang on Vietnam TravelGuide. Do not add Samui or Phuket on this week.

Day by day

1

Bangkok tickets

Palace 500 THB, Wat Pho 300 THB, Wat Arun 200 THB plus 5 THB ferry.

2

Market night

Yaowarat after 18:00 or Banthat Thong.

3

City buffer

Pack. Confirm official Vietnam entry and the Da Nang ticket.

4

Hop to Da Nang

International flight. Car to Hoi An if that is the sleep.

5

Hoi An old town

Walk Hoi An Ancient Town after the heat. Hoi An lantern streets.

6

Beach or marble hour

Da Nang sand or a short hill. One add-on, not three.

7

Exit Da Nang

Same-day long-haul is tight. File TDAC before you re-enter Thailand, and read the Vietnam e-visa portal for the exit stamp.

Three Bangkok nights, then Hoi An

This is not a Thai-island week. For a longer dual-country coast see 16 days Thailand Vietnam coasts.

Common questions

Where do I check a short Vietnam stay?
Read the Vietnam e-visa portal for your passport and dates. File TDAC and check Thai Immigration for Thailand.
Thai islands on the same week?
No. Seven days already use an international hop.
Da Nang or Hoi An sleep?
Hoi An if old-town nights are the point. Da Nang if you want a beach and a shorter airport run.
