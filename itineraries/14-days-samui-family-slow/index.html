14 days on Koh Samui: slow family itinerary

Land USM and stay put. One island is the point, with a hotel pool as the daily default and one park boat if the kids handle the swell.

Sleep14 nights one island; Bophut, Choeng Mon, or a Lamai pool

Two slow weeks stay on Koh Samui so kids are not on landing boats. Fly Bangkok to Koh Samui; Samui Airport is a premium ticket, often 3,500 to 9,000 THB one way in 2026, and a 2026 Grab to Chaweng is often 200 to 400 THB. Sleep the fortnight using where to stay: Bophut, Choeng Mon, or a Lamai pool. Chaweng is louder. Bangkok Hospital Samui sits on the island. Family notes sit on family travel.

Book Ang Thong once if the park is open (300 THB). Skip a default Phangan hop with small kids; Lomprayah is about 30 minutes but landing days still cost energy. January to September is the Gulf window. Do not add Phuket. Official Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) steps are on visas and TDAC.

Common questions

Do you need two hotels?
Only if the first bay is wrong. A mid-stay move from Chaweng to Bophut is the usual fix.
Full Moon with kids?
No. Haad Rin is the Full Moon Party beach. Stay on Samui.
When is the Gulf calmer?
January to September. November and December can still work, but October swell is common.
