10 days in Bangkok, Khao Sok Lake, and Krabi

Known for a city-to-karst-to-cliff line: Bangkok, Cheow Lan Lake, then Railay or Ao Nang. Fits 10-night trips that fly to Surat Thani (URT) and exit Krabi (KBV).

Ten days are known for a Bangkok start, a Khao Sok lake night, and a Krabi finish. Sleep three nights in the city (Sukhumvit or riverside). Take a morning flight from BKK or DMK to Surat Thani (URT), then a 90-minute van to the park. See Surat Thani to Khao Sok. Split the two park nights: one in the Highway 401 jungle valley, one on a floating raft house on Cheow Lan Lake via Ratchaprapha Pier. Foreign adults pay 300 THB at the park gate.

A private van south to Krabi takes about 2.5 hours. Sleep four nights split between Railay and Ao Nang, or move the last nights to Koh Lanta if you want a slower island. Fly out of KBV. November to April is the Andaman window for the Krabi block. Do not add Samui on this spine. For a classic city-north-beach ten days, use classic 10 days.

Day by day

1

Bangkok arrival

Land BKK or DMK. Sukhumvit or riverside. Walk a soi. Sleep.

2

Rattanakosin morning

Palace 500 THB at 08:30, Wat Pho 300 THB, Wat Arun 200 THB. Dinner back on Sukhumvit or Yaowarat.

3

City buffer

Chinatown, a river boat, or Siam if the heat wins. Pack for a morning URT flight.

4

Fly URT, van to Khao Sok

Morning flight, then about 90 minutes by van to the 401 village. Sleep the jungle valley. No lake boat after dark.

5

Ratchaprapha raft house

Transfer to Ratchaprapha Pier. Park fee 300 THB. One night on a Cheow Lan floating raft house.

6

Van south to Krabi

About 2.5 hours private van. Sleep Ao Nang or cross to Railay (100 THB daytime longtail).

7

Railay and Phra Nang

Phra Nang Cave and the west sand. Climb only with a named shop (1,200 to 2,000 THB half-day).

8

Ao Nang or Lanta move

Stay on the Krabi mainland, or boat to Koh Lanta if you want four quieter nights split 2 and 2.

9

Island or cliff buffer

Hong Islands or a Lanta beach day. Weather buffer lives here.

10

KBV out

Fly Krabi Airport. Same-day long-haul via BKK needs a wide connection. Do not book a dawn boat on exit day.

City, lake, then one Krabi coast

Ratchaprapha Pier is not the 401 village. Budget transfer time both ways. Lanta boats and vans sit on Krabi to Koh Lanta. Keep the four south nights on one coast name.

Common questions

Why fly URT instead of KBV first?
URT puts you 90 minutes from Khao Sok. KBV first means a longer backtrack to the lake. Leave from KBV after the Krabi nights.
Is the raft house required?
One valley night plus one raft-house night is the point of this spine. Two valley nights skip the karst reservoir.
Railay, Ao Nang, or Lanta for the last four nights?
Split Railay and Ao Nang if you want cliffs and a road. Use Lanta if you want a slower island. Do not book all three.
