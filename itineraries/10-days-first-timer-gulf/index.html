10 days in Thailand: first-timer Gulf itinerary

Fly BKK to USM after the palace morning, sleep Bophut or Chaweng, and take one 300 THB Ang Thong day if the park is open. Skip Phuket on this spine.

Bangkok and Koh Samui make the first Gulf island week. Land at Suvarnabhumi, sleep Sukhumvit, and fly the premium hop into Samui Airport so landing day stays boat-free. January to September is the sea that works; Phuket is the wrong add.

Give the Grand Palace and river wats their own morning (BTS 17 to 62 THB). Fly Bangkok to Koh Samui. Samui Airport is a premium ticket. Sleep six nights using Samui where to stay: Chaweng, Lamai, or Bophut. Book Ang Thong (300 THB). A Lomprayah hop to Phangan is about 30 minutes if you want one bay night, not a Full Moon default. Season notes sit on when to go.

Day by day

1

Bangkok tickets

Palace 500 THB, Wat Pho 300 THB, Wat Arun 200 THB plus 5 THB ferry.

2

Market night

Chatuchak or Or Tor Kor, then Yaowarat.

3

City buffer

Pack for USM. Jim Thompson or a Siam hour.

4

Land USM

No boat. Sleep Bophut, Choeng Mon, or Chaweng.

5

Beach and village

Bophut Fisherman Village if you sleep north. Learn Grab and songthaew fares.

6

Ang Thong day

Park fee 300 THB. No public ferry. Back before dark.

7

Second Samui coast

Swap to Lamai or a north bay if the first beach is wrong.

8

Optional Phangan day

Lomprayah about 30 minutes Bangrak or Maenam to Thong Sala. Back the same day unless you booked a bay night.

9

Buffer swim

Hotel day. Confirm the USM exit car.

10

Exit USM

Airport car. Same-day long-haul from Samui is tight.

Bangkok, then one Gulf island

Keep the Andaman off this ten-day page. May to September swell hits Phuket first. For three islands, use Gulf islands 10 days.

Common questions

Why not add Phuket?
The Andaman and Gulf do not share one dry season. This page is a Gulf first trip.
Ang Thong fee?
300 THB for foreign adults. There is no public ferry. Tours leave from Samui piers.
Is USM cheaper via Surat Thani?
URT plus a boat is the budget path. USM is the short hop and the premium fare.
