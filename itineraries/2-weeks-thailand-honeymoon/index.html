2 weeks in Thailand honeymoon itinerary: river, mountains, and private pool villas

Known for a river, mountain, and villa split with private pools and 06:30 longtails. Fits couples who want a Chao Phraya hotel, a Lanna spa, then one dry-season coast.

A honeymoon fortnight is known for three named river nights, four cooler mountain nights, and six villa nights on one sea. Sleep on the Chao Phraya at Capella, Mandarin Oriental, or The Siam. Book a private longtail canal tour and one riverfront tasting dinner. Dress-code roofs sit on rooftop bars. See honeymoon and couples for the shorter 3-4-5 shape.

Take four nights in Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai. Four Seasons Chiang Mai in Mae Rim is the valley pool. A riverside boutique in town works if you want dinner without a long car. Book a Lanna herbal spa at Fah Lanna or Oasis Spa. Finish with six nights on the Andaman or Gulf: Rayavadee or Phulay Bay in Krabi, Six Senses Yao Noi, Keemala or Trisara in Phuket, or Four Seasons Koh Samui. Charter private 06:30 longtails. Match the coast to the monsoon. Do not stack two seas.

Day by day

1

Chao Phraya arrival

Capella, Mandarin Oriental, or The Siam. River room. No temple circuit on landing day.

2

Private canal longtail

Charter a longtail for canals and a quiet wat. Tasting dinner on the river at night.

3

One roof or one terrace

One dress-code skyline drink or a Wat Arun terrace. Not both. Pack for the north flight.

4

Fly the north

BKK to CNX or CEI, about 70 minutes. Four Seasons Mae Rim or a riverside boutique.

5

Lanna spa day

Fah Lanna or Oasis Spa herbal compress. Slow Old City walk if you sleep in town.

6

Valley or Chiang Rai temples

Mae Rim rice fields, or a White Temple morning if you chose Chiang Rai (100 THB).

7

North buffer

Second spa hour or a cooking class. Fly south tomorrow. One villa coast only.

8

Villa arrival

Krabi, Phuket, Yao Noi, or Samui. Private transfer. No shared tour boat on day one.

9

Pool and bay

Private pool test. Learn the resort boat desk if a 06:30 charter is on the list.

10

06:30 longtail

Private charter before tour boats. Park fees of 200 to 400 THB still apply.

11

Villa day

Stay put. A second hotel on this block wastes the pool you booked.

12

Second boat or spa

Hong Islands, a quiet Phang Nga dawn, or a resort spa. One outing.

13

Buffer

Weather hold. Confirm the airport car. Same-day long-haul from a south airport is tight.

14

Out KBV, HKT, or USM

Named airport car. Do not add a dawn boat on exit day.

River, Lanna, then one villa coast

Named villa pages live under resort living. Andaman villas (Krabi, Phuket, Yao Noi) from November to April. Samui from January to August. A shared resort beach is a different trip from a private pool.

Common questions

How many nights in each place?
3 on the Bangkok river, 4 in Chiang Mai or Chiang Rai, 6 at one villa coast. Two hotel changes after the city is enough.
Krabi, Phuket, Yao Noi, or Samui?
Andaman villas from November to April. Samui from January to August. Pick one sea.
When do you charter the longtail?
06:30. Tour boats reach the same bays after 09:00. Park fees of 200 to 400 THB still apply.
