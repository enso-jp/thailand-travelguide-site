Common questions

Can you see Phuket and Samui in one trip?
Not in a first week. In 14 days you still pick one coast unless you enjoy airport days. The 10-day south plan stays on one sea.
Which plan fits 3 days versus 3 weeks?
Use the Bangkok stopover for 72 hours. Use 7 or 10 days for a first trip. Use the 21-day grand route only if you can fly south once and keep Andaman and Gulf islands on one sea.
