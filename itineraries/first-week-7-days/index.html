7-Day Thailand Itinerary: Bangkok plus one fork

A first week suits planners who want Bangkok plus one other place. North is temples and food. South is one beach. Pick with the monsoon, not with a map that shows both seas.

Duration7 days
ForkChiang Mai or one coast
Do notTwo island groups

Three Bangkok nights are enough. The remaining four nights belong to Chiang Mai, Phuket, Samui, or Krabi, not two of them. The fork flight is about 70 minutes to CNX, HKT, USM, or KBV.

Pick the fork before you book day 4

Do not hold both a Chiang Mai sleeper and a Phuket beach. Book the first Sukhumvit nights, then one exit. Bangkok to Chiang Mai is the north hop. Bangkok to Phuket or Bangkok to Koh Samui is the coast hop. File TDAC before you fly in.

  • North: temples and food. Fly BKK or DMK to CNX, or take SRT 9 from Krung Thep Aphiwat at 18:40, due Chiang Mai 07:15 (SRT 2026 timetable).
  • Andaman: west-coast beach. Fly to HKT, then 45 to 90 minutes to Kata, Kamala, or Surin. Easier November to April.
  • Gulf: Samui strip or a quieter north coast. Fly to USM. Easier January to September.

Day by day

1

Bangkok arrival

Airport car or ARL. Sukhumvit sleep.

2

Palace morning

Rattanakosin early, river boat, dinner in Thonglor.

3

City buffer

Siam, Chinatown, or a rest day. If you take SRT 9, leave for Krung Thep Aphiwat (not Hua Lamphong) in the late afternoon. The 18:40 sleeper is due Chiang Mai at 07:15.

4

Fork day

Chiang Mai Old City, or a HKT, USM, or KBV landing and a named-beach transfer. Do not boat to Phi Phi the same afternoon you land HKT.

5

Deepen the fork

Nimman or a boat day. Not a new city.

6

Second full day away

This is the day the trip needed.

7

Return and out

Fly back to BKK only if the long-haul needs it. Same-day international is a gamble.

Common questions

North or south on a first week?
North if you care about food and wats. South if you need a beach. The 14-day plan is how you do both.
Flight or SRT 9 to Chiang Mai?
Fly if day 4 is a temple morning you want intact. Take SRT 9 if you want the overnight from Krung Thep Aphiwat (18:40 to 07:15 on the 2026 SRT board). Book the berth, not a day seat, if you need sleep.
Can you add Phuket and Samui on a first week?
No. That is two seas and a wasted transfer day. Pick one coast, or stay north.
