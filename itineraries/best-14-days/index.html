14-Day Thailand Itinerary: Bangkok, Chiang Mai, one coast

This is the 14-day spine: three nights Bangkok, three Chiang Mai, then one south coast. Do not add a second sea.

Duration14 days
PaceSteady, one domestic flight plus one south flight
South flipAndaman Nov-Apr, Gulf Jan-Sep
Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

The spine is city, north, then one island group. Fly the long legs: Bangkok to Chiang Mai is about 70 minutes. Drop Chiang Rai and a second sea before you drop rest days.

Day by day

1

Bangkok arrival

Land BKK or DMK. Airport car or ARL to Sukhumvit. Walk a soi, sleep.

2

Old City temples

Grand Palace at opening, Wat Pho, Wat Arun ferry. Dinner back on Sukhumvit.

3

River or Chinatown

Riverside walk and a Charoenkrung evening. Nightlife is optional and on its own pages.

4

Bangkok to Chiang Mai

Morning flight to CNX, or the previous night on SRT 9/10. Sleep in the Old City.

5

Old City circuit

Dawn wats, cooking class or Doi Suthep. Sunday market if the calendar lines up.

6

Nimman and a half-day

Cafes west of the moat, or a Chiang Rai day if you accept a long van.

7

Fly south

CNX to HKT or USM. If you must connect in Bangkok, stay at one airport and allow at least 1 hour 15 minutes on one ticket.

8

Beach day

Do not boat on arrival day. Swim and reset the clock.

9

Island hop or Railay

Andaman: Phi Phi or Railay. Gulf: Phangan day boat or a second Samui coast.

10

Quiet bay

Move hotels if the first beach is wrong. This is the day people usually should have started on.

11

Optional Khao Sok

Andaman itinerary only. One night on the lake, then back to the airport coast.

12

Buffer

Weather buffer. Ferry leftovers go here.

13

Back to Bangkok

Fly to BKK. One night in Siam or riverside if you have a morning long-haul.

14

Out

ARL or a booked car. Do not plan a palace morning before a noon flight.

Common questions

What do you actually see in 14 days?
Bangkok, Chiang Mai, and one south coast. That is the spine. Khao Sok or Phangan is a swap, not an add.
What should you drop?
Do not stack Phuket and Samui. Do not add Pai and Chiang Rai on the same north block.
