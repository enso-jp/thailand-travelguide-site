21 days backpacker Thailand: north then one sea itinerary

Do the north on vans and the Green Bus first. Lock the beach to the month: Andaman November to April, Gulf January to September.

Split3 Bangkok, 8 north, 9 one coast, 1 exit

Three weeks of vans and one monsoon coast fit backpackers who want the north before sand. Sleep three nights in Bangkok so the old-city river temples sit on a full morning (BTS 17 to 62 THB) before the 70 to 75 minute Bangkok to Chiang Mai hop.

Run Pai on Route 1095 (3 to 4.5 hours) and Chiang Rai on Green Bus (about 3.5 hours). White Temple is 200 THB since 1 January 2026. Then pick one coast with when to go. Keep Phuket and Samui off the same first week. Read visas and TDAC and file the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) before you fly.

Day by day

1

Bangkok tickets

Palace 500 THB, Wat Pho 300 THB, Wat Arun 200 THB plus 5 THB ferry.

2

Market night

Chatuchak or Or Tor Kor, then Yaowarat.

3

City buffer

Pack for CNX. Jim Thompson if it rains.

4

Fly Chiang Mai

About 70 to 75 minutes. Old City guest house.

5

Doi Suthep 07:00

30 THB plus songthaew 60 to 100 THB.

6

Sanctuary or class

Named ethical half-day or a cooking class.

7

Van to Pai

Route 1095, 3 to 4.5 hours.

8

Pai canyon

Viewpoint and springs. No night scooter.

9

Back to CM

Laundry and a bus ticket to Chiang Rai.

10

Green Bus to CR

About 3.5 hours. Town guest house.

11

White Temple day

200 THB since 1 January 2026. Blue Temple free.

12

Fly or bus to one coast

Andaman via HKT or Gulf via USM/URT. Not both.

13

Coast landing

No boat. Cheap room near the sand you booked.

14

First beach day

West-coast Phuket or a Samui bay.

15

One park boat

Maya Bay 400 THB no swim, or Ang Thong 300 THB.

16

Second stop

Railay/Lanta or Phangan. One sea only.

17

Island night

Long Beach, Srithanu, or Klong Dao.

18

Third stop or buffer

Lipe or Tao if energy holds. Otherwise stay put.

19

Slow day

Snorkel or a viewpoint. Confirm the exit boat.

20

Move to an airport island

HKT, KBV, USM, or URT. Sleep near the hop.

21

Exit

Domestic hop to BKK if the long-haul is there. Buffer beats a missed boat.

Common questions

Can week three do Phuket and Samui?
No. Pick one sea. The monsoons do not match.
Where do I check stay length?
File TDAC and read Thai Immigration. The visas and TDAC page lists the Thai steps.
White Temple fee?
200 THB for foreigners since 1 January 2026.
