Bangkok to Koh Samui: 70-min USM flight vs Donsak ferry

Fly to USM if the day matters. The van-plus-ferry via Donsak is the cheaper half-day.

FlightAbout 70 minutes to USM. Often 3,500-9,000 THB ($100-260) in 2026
Ferry pathVan to Donsak plus boat, 8-10 hours. Often 700-1,500 THB ($20-43)
SeasonEasier January to September
Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

Samui is the Gulf island with its own airport. USM transfers are short. Chaweng is close. The opposite coast still deserves a booked car at night. USM is a private-concession airport, so 2026 air fares often sit at 3,500 to 9,000 THB ($100 to $260) one way. The Donsak van-plus-ferry is often 700 to 1,500 THB ($20 to $43) and takes 8 to 10 hours.
