Get there: Thailand routes

Fly the regional hops from Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK) when the day matters. The overnight Chiang Mai sleeper from Krung Thep Aphiwat is the useful night if you want the ride. In the south, morning ferries beat late arrivals.

NorthFlight about 70 minutes, sleeper about 12 hours
AndamanBKK or DMK to HKT or KBV
GulfBKK to USM, or ferry from Donsak
