Plan a trip to Thailand

Thailand runs two separate island monsoon calendars, not one winter. If you land on a US, UK, or most EU passport, the tourist visa-free stamp is 30 days from 15 September 2026, and you submit TDAC before the flight.

Entry30-day visa-free plus TDAC from 15 Sep 2026
SeasonTwo coasts, two calendars
City moveBTS, MRT, ARL, Grab, Bolt
Search stays on Trip.com Check live 2026 rates and free-cancellation options. Book tickets on Klook Book tickets, tours, or transfers ahead for peak dates.

Season decides the south coast. Visa and TDAC decide the flight. Money, a SIM, and the monsoon decide the pack list. A 2026 BTS tap is 17 to 62 THB; keep that as the city baseline.
