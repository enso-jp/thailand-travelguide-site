Thailand Trains and Ferries 2026: sleeper and boats

The useful train is the Bangkok to Chiang Mai sleeper on SRT 9 and 10. The useful boats are Lomprayah on the Gulf and morning Andaman ferries to Phi Phi.

North trainSRT 9 and 10, Krung Thep Aphiwat to Chiang Mai
Gulf boatsLomprayah and Seatran
Andaman boatsMorning only in the wet months

The useful Thai train is the Bangkok to Chiang Mai sleeper from Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal, not Hua Lamphong. The ride is about 12 hours. A 2026 lower berth is often 800 to 1,400 THB. Budget a Grab from your hotel; the station is north of the old core.

Book trains on SRT or via Trip.com when inventory is mirrored. Book Gulf ferries the afternoon before. Book Andaman boats when the weather desk is awake, not a week out in September.

Hua Lamphong or Krung Thep Aphiwat?
For Chiang Mai sleepers 9 and 10, use Krung Thep Aphiwat. Hua Lamphong is still a station; it is not your sleeper.
