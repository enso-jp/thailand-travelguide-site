Thailand Visa and TDAC 2026: 30-day visa-free

This page is for first-time visitors on US, UK, or most EU passports. From 15 September 2026 the tourist visa-free stamp is 30 days, and you file a free TDAC on the official Immigration site before you fly.

Visa-free30 days for US, UK, and 58 other listed passports from 15 Sep 2026
TDACFree form at tdac.immigration.go.th, within 3 days before arrival
Longer stayTourist e-visa is still a 60-day stay if you apply before you fly

The 60-day visa-exemption scheme ended on 15 September 2026 (Government PRD, 4 September 2026, citing MFA Consular Affairs). Ordinary US, UK, and most EU passports now sit on the 30-day tourist list (MFA ผ.30, 15 September 2026). A tourist visa applied through thaievisa.go.th is a different product and still grants up to 60 days on arrival.

TDAC replaced the paper TM6. File it only at tdac.immigration.go.th. The Immigration Bureau guide says to submit within 3 days before arrival. It is free. Sites that charge to file it are not the Bureau. Keep the emailed QR offline.

File TDAC, then lock the first two nights

TDAC asks for your first Thai address. Book the Bangkok hotel before you open the form. Do not plan a same-day island hop after a long-haul. Grab, BTS, and the airport car is the landing move. First week is the route after you sleep.

  • Who files: every non-Thai national, including visa-free and visa holders (tdac.immigration.go.th/manual).
  • When: within 3 days before the arrival date. You can update the card before you travel.
  • What you need: passport, flight number, and the first hotel name. That is why the stay search sits here, not on a generic homepage.
  • Stamp check: the officer still decides entry. The London Thai Embassy notes proof of onward travel within 30 days and, if asked, funds of 20,000 THB per person.

Common questions

When do you file TDAC?
Within 3 days before arrival, once the flight number is stable. Use tdac.immigration.go.th only. Keep the QR offline.
Is visa-free still 60 days?
No. From 15 September 2026 the tourist exemption is 30 days for the 60 listed passports. A tourist visa is still up to 60 days if you apply before you fly.
Can you extend the 30-day stamp?
Immigration offices can extend at their discretion. The London Thai Embassy describes an extension of up to 30 days. Do not plan that on day 2 of a first trip.
