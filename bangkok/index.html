Central Thailand

Bangkok กรุงเทพฯ

Bangkok is the usual first stop. Sleep on Sukhumvit or in Siam for the BTS. Sleep riverside for the Chao Phraya. The Old City is for temples, not late nights.

Overview

Bangkok is the capital you land in: Grand Palace and river wats by day, BTS hotel strips at night. Give it two or three nights. Eat in Thonglor or Chinatown. Nightlife lives on its own pages.

From BKK, the Airport Rail Link, Grab, and BTS tap is the cheap, predictable move. Book a Trip.com airport car if you land late with luggage. The usual next hops are Bangkok to Chiang Mai, Bangkok to Phuket, or Bangkok to Koh Samui. Use the 7-day or 14-day itinerary once you pick a fork.

Top attractions in Bangkok

1

Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew พระบรมมหาราชวัง

The royal compound and Wat Phra Kaew open at Mani Noppharat Gate from 08:30 to 15:30. Foreign tickets are 500 THB. Pair Wat Pho after this visit, not Chatuchak.

5.0 ★★★★★ Editor

2 to 3 hours · 500 THB
2

Wat Pho วัดโพธิ์

Wat Pho is known for the Reclining Buddha and the massage school next to the Grand Palace. The temple ticket is 300 THB, and a 30-minute table at the school desk is 340 THB.

5.0 ★★★★★ Editor

90 minutes · 300 THB
3

Wat Arun วัดอรุณ

Wat Arun is the riverside prang you reach from Tha Tien on the 5 THB orange ferry. Climbing the prang costs 200 THB. Go before 09:00 or at dusk.

5.0 ★★★★★ Editor

1 hour · 200 THB
4

Chatuchak Weekend Market ตลาดนัดจตุจักร

Chatuchak is the Saturday and Sunday market at BTS Mo Chit or MRT Chatuchak Park. Arrive between 09:00 and 12:00 if you want to walk before the heat.

4.0 ★★★★☆ Editor

3 to 4 hours · Free entry
5

Jim Thompson House บ้านจิม ทอมป์สัน

Jim Thompson House is the teak-house museum off BTS National Stadium. Book a timed slot. A 2026 ticket is about 200 THB.

4.0 ★★★★☆ Editor

75 minutes · 200 THB
6

Yaowarat Chinatown เยาวราช

Yaowarat is the dinner walk from MRT Wat Mangkon after 18:00. Monday stalls thin out, so bring cash or PromptPay and skip that night if you want a full street.

4.0 ★★★★☆ Editor

2 to 3 hours · Plates 60 to 100 THB
7

Rajadamnern and Lumpinee ราชดำเนิน / ลุมพินี

Rajadamnern and Lumpinee are the stadium fight cards, not a soi gym tourist bout. Book a 2026 card. Tickets start around 1,000 THB.

4.0 ★★★★☆ Editor

3 hours · 1,000 to 2,000 THB
8

Damnoen Saduak and Amphawa ดำเนินสะดวก / อัมพวา

These are the two canal days from Bangkok. Amphawa is the Friday to Sunday evening market. Pick one, and do not try both on the same trip.

3.0 ★★★☆☆ Editor

Half day · Tour 800 to 1,500 THB
9

Lumphini Park สวนลุมพินี

Lumphini Park is the free dawn walk for monitor lizards from MRT Lumphini or Silom. Pair it with a Silom lunch, not a palace morning.

3.0 ★★★☆☆ Editor

1 hour · Free
10

Talat Noi and Charoenkrung ตลาดน้อย

Talat Noi is the morning Chinese shophouse walk, and Charoenkrung turns into warehouse bars at night. Do not stack both with a Yaowarat dinner in the same 14 hours.

3.0 ★★★☆☆ Editor

2 hours · Free to walk
11

Sukhumvit and Thonglor สุขุมวิท / ทองหล่อ

Sukhumvit from BTS Nana to Ekkamai is the first-timer hotel strip. Come for dinner density, not temples. A 2026 BTS tap on this line is 17 to 47 THB.

3.0 ★★★☆☆ Editor

Evening · BTS 17 to 47 THB
12

Ari cafes อารีย์

Ari is the cafe neighbourhood fifteen minutes north of Siam. It suits a half day of coffee at 80 to 150 THB. Skip it if you only have two Bangkok nights.

3.0 ★★★☆☆ Editor

Half day · Coffee 80 to 150 THB

Districts

Sukhumvit and Thonglor: hotels, BTS Nana to Ekkamai, and most first-timer stays.

Riverside and Charoenkrung: the river, warehouse bars, and the Chinatown edge.

  • Old City and Rattanakosin: palace, wat circuit, and heat. Go early.
  • Siam and Silom: malls, BTS interchanges, and the Sala Daeng MRT link.
  • Phrom Phong and Thong Lo: Japanese dining, EmDistrict, and Soi 55 nights.
  • Ari: cafes and neighbourhood kitchens 15 minutes north of Siam

Area guides and planning pages

  • Where to stay: BTS, river, or Old City.
  • Getting around: BTS, MRT, ARL, boats.
  • Best time to visit: Cool dry November to February.
  • Day trips: Ayutthaya, Kwai, markets.
  • Top attractions: Palace 500 THB, Chatuchak weekends.
  • Nightlife districts: One district per night.
  • Khao San: Backpacker street, not a first BTS hotel.
  • Chatuchak: Saturday and Sunday only.

Common questions

BKK or DMK?
Fly into the airport your long-haul uses. Transfers between them take 45 to 90 minutes. Do not schedule a tight same-day connection.
Where should you stay on a first trip?
Sukhumvit if you want BTS and dinner options. Riverside if you want the river. Old City only if the palace is the whole point and you accept thin evening food.
Where is nightlife?
District nightlife pages under Interests. The main Bangkok page stays districts, transport, and hotels.
