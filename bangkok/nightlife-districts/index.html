Bangkok nightlife districts

Bangkok nightlife is known for five districts rather than one crawl, so sleep where you will Grab home after the last BTS. Soi 11 is the loud strip, Thonglor is dinner first, and Charoenkrung is the shophouse pour.

Open Sukhumvit Soi 11 if you already sleep at Nana or Asok, and open Thonglor and Ekkamai if dinner is first. Charoenkrung and Soi Nana is the shophouse pour near Yaowarat, and Silom and Sathorn holds roofs and jazz. Ari and Pradipat is gardens and vinyl.

A 2026 crafted cocktail is often 400 to 650 THB, and after the last BTS, Grab home on the same side of town. Do not rent a scooter at 01:00, and the main nightlife hub lists rooms. This page only names which district to open.

Pick one district

  • Soi 11: Loud, walkable, Havana Social at 20:00. Sleep Nana.
  • Thonglor: Reservations, unmarked doors. Sleep Thong Lo or Ekkamai.
  • Charoenkrung: River and timber rooms. No BTS at the door.
  • Silom: Sky Bar walk-in, Suan Phlu jazz. Moon Bar stays closed until 2 December 2026.
  • Ari: Craft beer gardens. Last BTS still matters.

Common questions

Can you do Soi 11 and Thonglor the same night?
You can. You will spend the night in a car. Pick one.
Where do you sleep for Charoenkrung bars?
Riverside or a short Grab from Chinatown. There is no BTS at Soi Nana.
