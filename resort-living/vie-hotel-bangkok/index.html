VIE Hotel Bangkok (MGallery)

VIE Hotel Bangkok is known as the MGallery on Ratchathewi BTS. Stay here if you want a designed city night without a river compound.

Back to Bangkok

Ratchathewi puts you on the BTS between Siam and the ARL at Phaya Thai. A 2026 BTS tap from here is 17 to 47 THB.

This is a tower, not a villa. Rooms are city rooms. Entry rates in a busy 2026 week often sit from about 4,000 to 7,000 THB. Suites run 8,000 to 14,000 THB and up. Planning bands. It is the useful upgrade from a generic Sukhumvit tower when you still want the train.

River and district versus other Bangkok stays

Your Tower

Ratchathewi BTS tower

Airport Rail Link plus one hop, a city value pick rather than a river compound.

  • VIE Hotel Bangkok locks design rooms at 4,000 to 14,000+ THB beside BTS Ratchathewi.
  • Siam shopping and the ARL are the point. A hotel boat is not.
  • This is the short-stay rail lock when two river nights are not in the budget.
Manor Step-Up

Phloen Chit vertical manor

The step up if you want a quieter tower, rooftop cocktails, and embassy-row walks.

  • Rosewood Bangkok is the manor rival at 14,000 to 32,000+ THB beside BTS Ploenchit.
  • The skytrain story is the same. The room product is the difference.
  • Pick Rosewood when you still want rails but need a longer stay feel.
River Nights

Charoenkrung river nights

Low-rise waterfront courtyards if two river nights are the Bangkok plan.

  • Capella Bangkok is the river alternative at 32,000 to 85,000+ THB.
  • Four Seasons and Mandarin Oriental share that bank if you want a courtyard or the historic pier.
  • The Siam remains the upriver teak compound if you want a barge and no promenade traffic.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Phaya Thai ARL is one BTS stop plus a walk or a short extra hop depending on your bags. Suvarnabhumi to Phaya Thai is about 30 minutes on the ARL, 45 THB in recent years for the full line. Pay with cash or token at the station machine. DMK is a different airport and a different car.
  • Siam malls are two BTS stops. The palace is a Grab, 150 to 250 THB in the morning. Soi 11 is a few BTS stops east. Use VIE as a Bangkok night that is nicer than Nana and cheaper than Capella. Two nights is the usual lock before you pick a coast.
  • VIE sits on Ratchathewi BTS, between Siam and the Airport Rail Link at Phaya Thai. From BKK, ride the ARL about 30 minutes (about 45 THB for the full line in recent years), then one BTS hop. A 2026 private BKK car is often 700 to 1,400 THB and 40 to 70 minutes. DMK is a different airport and a different car, often 600 to 1,200 THB.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is a city tower. Coast monsoons do not apply. May to October rain still slows the ARL last mile and the expressway. Land, ride the train, leave the first afternoon empty. The palace is a morning Grab, 150 to 250 THB.
  • VIE spa or outsourced treatment hours in 2026 often sit from about 1,800 to 3,500 THB, well below a river-compound spa. Book it if you have a buffer night. Do not treat it as a resort week.
  • A 2026 BTS tap from Ratchathewi is 17 to 47 THB along the core board. Soi 11 is a few stops east. Capella is a Grab to the river, not a walk. Ask the desk for a 2026 airport quote in THB if you refuse the ARL with bags.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Entry rooms often sit from about 4,000 to 7,000 THB. Use VIE as a nicer Bangkok night than Nana and a cheaper one than Capella.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
VIE or a Sukhumvit tower?
VIE if you want ARL access and a designed lobby. Sukhumvit if dinner density is the point.
