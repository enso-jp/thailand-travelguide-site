Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve

Phulay Bay is known as a sealed Krabi beach reserve, west of Ao Nang under the headland. You come for the limestone bay, not the village strip.

Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, is a sealed Krabi beach west of Ao Nang. The published door is 111 Moo 3, Nongthalay, Mueang, Krabi 81180, +66 75 628 111. The official airport note is about 40 km from KBV. Fewer keys, a private beach, and a price that assumes you will not hunt Ao Nang nightlife.

Rayavadee is the Railay grove with no road. Phulay Bay has a road and still feels sealed. Do not book both. Pair with a Phi Phi or Hong day only in the Andaman window, and only if the marine desk agrees that morning.

Beach and zone versus other Krabi and Phang Nga coasts

Your Mainland

Tubkaek mainland sunset

Quiet mainland sand facing the thirteen limestone islands of Koh Hong, with a direct road from Krabi Airport.

  • Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve locks oversized pavilions at 28,000 to 75,000+ THB with zero boat dependency in changeable weather.
  • A KBV car is the arrival story, which matters when Andaman swell cancels longtails.
  • Hong Island boats remain a booked morning, not a required transfer to reach the bed.
Island Rival

Koh Yao Noi in Phang Nga Bay

A sleepy island halfway between Phuket and Krabi, with sunrise views over karst pinnacles.

  • Six Senses Yao Noi is the bay-villa rival at 26,000 to 70,000+ THB, reached by a 45-minute speedboat from Phuket or Krabi.
  • Book this rival when the pinnacles, not a mainland road, are the week.
  • Ao Nang dinners become a project from the island, so plan most plates on property.
Boat-Only Pocket

Railay and Phra Nang peninsula

Boat-only limestone beaches cut off from mainland roads, 15 minutes from Ao Nang by longtail.

  • Rayavadee sits in a coconut grove at 22,000 to 60,000+ THB, steps from Phra Nang cave beach.
  • Accept a stuck storm day if you pick this pocket. There is no road exit.
  • Ao Nang is the mainland fallback if you want the same cliffs with a car.

Nong Thale, not the Ao Nang strip

Nong Thale (Nongthalay) is the bay west of Ao Nang, under Khao Ngon Nak. The village is thin. Dinner off-property is a decision, not a stroll of ten menus. Ao Nang beach road is a 15 to 25 minute car when the traffic is kind. Krabi Town is farther and useful only for a market or a departure.

Southwest monsoon (May to October) hits this west-facing water. Green-season rates look clever until the beach closes and the longtail cancels. If your dates are July, read the Koh Tao page instead of forcing Krabi.
