Avista Hideaway Phuket (MGallery)

Avista Hideaway is known as the MGallery west-coast hill stay. Use it when you want a quieter Andaman week than Patong.

Back to Phuket
UseWhen the hotel is the trip
BookTrip.com rate search

Use it when you want a quieter Andaman week than Patong and Amanpuri is not the budget. Still treat it as a west-coast monsoon property. HKT is about 45 to 75 minutes by car. A 2026 private car is often 1,000 to 1,800 THB.

The hotel sits above the Kamala / west-coast hills. You will need a car for the beach and for dinner. A 2026 Grab down to Kamala is often 150 to 300 THB. Do not boat on arrival day. November to April is the window.

Beach and zone versus other Phuket coasts

This Hill

Patong and Kata ridge

A hillside MGallery between Patong and Freedom Beach, with faster dining and less cove privacy.

  • Avista Hideaway Phuket sits at 6,000 to 20,000+ THB, a value west-coast lock when flagship coves are closed.
  • Patong plates and Kata family beaches are 20 to 30 minutes, so you still need a car after dark.
  • Kamala and Surin remain the useful dinner and daybed belt from this hill.
Cove Step-Up

Pansea and Surin privacy

Gated headlands if the budget jumps and you want the sand to belong to the hotel.

  • Amanpuri is the privacy jump at 45,000 to 120,000+ THB on a private white-sand cove.
  • The HKT car is longer than the Avista hop, so book the driver with the villa.
  • This rival beats the ridge when road noise is the reason you left Patong.
Villa Alternative

Naithon villa hills

All-pool-villa northwest cliffs far from Patong bass, closer to the airport.

  • Trisara Phuket is the private-pool-villa alternative at 35,000 to 95,000+ THB.
  • Naithon sits 20 to 25 minutes from HKT, which helps a late arrival more than a Patong-ridge hotel.
  • Mai Khao north sand is the quiet airport beach if you want length, not a hillside view.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Rooms and pool access suites are the inventory: Entry rates in a busy dry-season week often sit from about 6,000 to 11,000 THB. Larger suites run 12,000 to 20,000 THB and up. Planning bands. Trisara is the northwest Phuket step up if the villa is the week.
  • Patong is 20 to 30 minutes and the wrong default dinner: Kamala and Surin are the local plates. Old Town is a morning. Beach clubs are a booked daybed, not a walk-in from the hill. See Phuket beach clubs.
  • Land at HKT, then a private car to the Kamala / west-coast hills: The ride is 45 to 75 minutes. A 2026 booked car is often 1,000 to 1,800 THB. Shared vans are cheaper and add hotel stops. A 2026 Grab down to Kamala dinner is often 150 to 300 THB.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is an Andaman west-coast hill: Wet season is roughly May to October: swell on Kamala and Surin, cancelled daybeds, and a Patong night that is not worth the wet road. Book November to April if the view is the week.
  • Avista spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 2,500 to 5,000 THB: Book on a buffer day. Amanpuri is the grove step up on the same coast, with a higher spa band and a Pansea car of 1,500 to 2,500 THB.
  • Ask the desk for the 2026 HKT quote in THB: Extra beds can add 1,500 to 4,000 THB. Patong is 20 to 30 minutes and the wrong default plate.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Beach clubs are a booked daybed on the sand, not a walk-in from the hill: Wi-Fi is fine for mail. It is not why you picked the hill.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
Avista or Amanpuri?
Amanpuri if the grove is the trip and the rate is fine. Avista if you want the same coast at an MGallery price.
