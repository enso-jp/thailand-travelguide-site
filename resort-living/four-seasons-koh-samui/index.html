Four Seasons Koh Samui

Four Seasons Koh Samui is known for northwest-coast villas. Stay here if you want a Gulf view and will Grab for most dinners.

It is a different island from Chaweng. USM is about 20 to 35 minutes by car. A 2026 private car is often 800 to 1,500 THB.

Villas face the water. Entry villas in a busy 2026 Gulf week often sit from about 25,000 to 40,000 THB. Larger pool villas run 50,000 to 100,000 THB and up. Planning bands. Trip.com shows the live number. Six Senses is the Bophut headland if you want Fisherman Village closer.

Beach and zone versus other Samui coasts

Your Cape

Laem Yai sunset amphitheater

Steep coconut-palmed hillsides facing the Gulf sunset, 35 to 45 minutes from Samui Airport.

  • Four Seasons Koh Samui locks butler-buggy hillside villas and a private cove at 36,000 to 90,000+ THB.
  • Do not ride a scooter down this hill after dark. The grade is steep and there is no shoulder. Book a car before the first pour off property.
  • Lipa Noi daybeds sit on the same west-facing coast when you want a booked afternoon off property.
Village-Walk Rivals

Choeng Mon and Bophut headland

Granite promontories 15 minutes from USM and 10 minutes from Fisherman Village.

  • Six Senses Samui is the 20-acre timber villa point at 22,000 to 55,000+ THB.
  • The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui holds a 58-acre former coconut plantation bay at 16,000 to 42,000+ THB.
  • This rival wins if a village dinner walk matters more than the sunset amphitheater.
East-Coast Strip

Chaweng walkable east coast

Powder-sand swimming and the dinner strip, a different week from a northwest villa hill.

  • Sleep on Chaweng when you want walkable dining and a short USM hop, not butler-buggy silence.
  • January to September is the easier Gulf sea on both coasts; October swell hits the east first.
  • A late Grab from Chaweng to Laem Yai often runs 300 to 700 THB, so do not treat the strip as a nightly outing from this hill.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Off-property dinners mean a car along the hills: Lipa Noi and Taling Ngam are quiet. Chaweng is 40 to 60 minutes. Do not plan a beach-club afternoon and a late Chaweng night unless you like that road twice.
  • Use the Gulf window, January to September: USM is the arrival. Pair with a Phangan boat only if you want a day off-property and you leave a buffer. Ang Thong is a day boat from the island, not from the villa lawn.
  • Land at USM, then a private car to the Lipa Noi / Taling Ngam northwest coast: The ride is 20 to 35 minutes. A 2026 booked car is often 800 to 1,500 THB. Chaweng is 40 to 60 minutes the other way. Book the car. The hill road is not a first scooter.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is a Lower Gulf villa row: The wettest window is roughly October to December. January to September is the easier sea. A west-facing sunset still works on many wet-season evenings. Boat days to Ang Thong do not.
  • Four Seasons Samui spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 5,000 to 10,000 THB: Book on a buffer day.
  • Ask the desk for the 2026 USM quote in THB: not a vague "car included." Extra beds can add 2,000 to 6,000 THB.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Phangan is a pier-to-pier boat, not a lawn-to-bay hop: Leave a buffer. Ang Thong is a day boat from the island, 300 THB at the park, no public ferry.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
Northwest or Bophut?
Northwest for the view and quiet. Bophut if you want a village walk.
