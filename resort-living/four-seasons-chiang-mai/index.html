Four Seasons Chiang Mai

Four Seasons Chiang Mai is known for rice-terrace pavilions in Mae Rim. Use it when the valley is the trip and you accept a 30 to 45 minute ride to the Old City.

It is not a moat hotel. You come for terraces, cooking school, and cool-season mornings.

CNX is about 25 to 40 minutes. A 2026 private car is often 800 to 1,500 THB. The Old City is 30 to 45 minutes the other way. Budget the car if you want Sunday Walking Street. Do not plan to walk anywhere off-property.

Valley and district versus other Chiang Mai stays

Your Valley

Mae Rim rice-terrace valley

Working terraced paddies, water buffalo, and misty foothills 30 to 40 minutes north of the moat.

  • Four Seasons Chiang Mai locks Lanna pavilions and private residences at 30,000 to 80,000+ THB.
  • November to early February is the clearest valley air. Late February to April haze can sit in every view.
  • Wat mornings need a booked car. This is not a walk to Wat Phra Singh.
River Rivals

Wat Ket and the Ping River

Walkable riverside lawns and 19th-century teak 10 minutes east of the Old City wats.

  • 137 Pillars House is the 1889 homestead at 18,000 to 40,000+ THB, with 30 suites around the original house.
  • Anantara Chiang Mai holds the contemporary Ping bank at 12,000 to 28,000+ THB.
  • These rivals win when you want a 07:00 wat without a 40-minute valley car.
Hill Contrast

Hang Dong forest slopes

Elevated valley infinity pools near Doi Suthep-Pui at a fraction of Mae Rim pavilion rates.

  • Hang Dong forest slopes hold hill pools and shorter Nimman taxis than a Mae Rim run, at a fraction of pavilion rates.
  • Nimman taxis are shorter than a Mae Rim run, so dinner in the west city is easier.
  • Old City on foot still wins if you want Wat Phra Singh at 07:00 without any hill road.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Pavilions and residences sit on the terraces: Entry pavilions in a busy cool-season week often sit from about 20,000 to 32,000 THB. Larger residences run 40,000 to 80,000 THB and up. Planning bands. Smoke season (late February to April) can sit in the valley. Check the air before you lock March.
  • Cooking school is the on-property day: Doi Suthep is a half day if you leave early. Inthanon is a full day. Elephant venues vary. Read the ethics page before you book a camp. Veranda High is the hill pool above town if you want a view and a shorter taxi to Nimman.
  • Land at Chiang Mai (CNX), then a private car to Mae Rim: The desk quotes 25 to 40 minutes. A 2026 booked car is often 800 to 1,500 THB. You will not walk off-property. Budget the car for Sunday Walking Street.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is a valley resort, not a coast: Andaman and Gulf monsoons do not apply. The local problem is haze from late February to April. Cool season (November to February) is the terrace week.
  • Four Seasons Mae Rim spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 4,500 to 9,000 THB: Book a still morning.
  • Ask for the 2026 CNX quote in THB: Extra beds in residences can add 2,000 to 6,000 THB. A pavilion rate without a transfer plan is incomplete if you land after dark.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Veranda High is the hill pool above town if you want a shorter taxi to Nimman: Old City guesthouses win if you want Wat Phra Singh at 07:00 on foot. Use Mae Rim when the terrace is the trip.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
Can you skip a town hotel?
Yes, if you accept the car for every wat. No, if you want to walk the moat at dawn.
