Veranda High Chiang Mai (MGallery)

Veranda High is known for a hill pool above the valley. Stay here if the view is the point and you will taxi to the Old City.

Back to Chiang Mai
UseWhen the hotel is the trip

It is an MGallery, not a moat guesthouse. The ride to the Old City is about 20 to 35 minutes. That is the trade.

CNX is 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. A 2026 private car is often 700 to 1,300 THB. Nimman is closer than the moat on some routes. You will not walk to dinner. Budget Grab or the hotel car every time you leave.

Valley and district versus other Chiang Mai stays

Your Hill

Hang Dong western foothills

Forested slopes near Doi Suthep-Pui, with an elevated infinity pool at an accessible luxury rate.

  • Veranda High Chiang Mai locks the MGallery hillside retreat at 5,000 to 18,000+ THB, 25 to 35 minutes from town.
  • Nimman dinner is a shorter taxi than a Mae Rim run.
  • This is a view-pool week, not a walk to Wat Phra Singh at 07:00.
Pavilion Step-Up

Mae Rim pavilion valley

Working rice terraces if the paddy budget is open and you want Lanna residences.

  • Four Seasons Chiang Mai is the 30,000 to 80,000+ THB rival, 30 to 40 minutes north of the moat.
  • November to early February is the kindest air for that terrace view.
  • Book Mae Rim when the buffalo field, not a hillside pool, is the photograph.
River Walks

Ping River and Old City on foot

Walkable teak or a contemporary river bank if you want wats without a hill road.

  • 137 Pillars House and Anantara Chiang Mai sit 10 minutes from the moat at 12,000 to 40,000+ THB.
  • Old City rooms still win if you want Wat Phra Singh at 07:00 without a taxi.
  • A 2026 moat Grab to Nimman often runs 80 to 120 THB, cheaper than a Hang Dong dinner run.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Rooms face the valley: Entry rooms in a busy cool-season week often sit from about 5,000 to 9,000 THB. Suites run 10,000 to 18,000 THB and up. Planning bands. Cool season is the point. In hot season, confirm the haze before you book a view room as the trip.
  • Four Seasons Mae Rim is the rice-terrace alternative if the hotel is the whole week and the budget is higher: Old City stays win if you want Wat Phra Singh at 07:00 on foot. Use Veranda High for two or three nights when the pool and the view are why you came.
  • Land at CNX, then a private car into the western hills above the valley: The desk quotes 25 to 40 minutes. A 2026 booked car is often 700 to 1,300 THB. The Old City is 20 to 35 minutes. Nimman is closer on some routes. Budget Grab or the hotel car every exit.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is a hill MGallery, not a beach: Andaman wet season (May to October) and Gulf rain (wettest October to December) do not cancel the pool. Haze from late February to April can sit in the view. Confirm the air before you book a view room as the trip.
  • Veranda spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 2,000 to 4,000 THB: Book a late morning. Four Seasons Mae Rim is the rice-terrace step up, 30 to 45 minutes from the moat, with a higher spa band.
  • Ask for the 2026 CNX quote in THB: Extra beds in suites can add 1,500 to 4,000 THB. A view rate without a car plan is a problem if you land after 21:00.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Doi Suthep is a songthaew or a car from the zoo road: not a walk from the lobby.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
Hill or moat?
Hill if the view is the point. Moat if you want to walk to the wats.
