Trisara Phuket

Trisara is known for private villas on a guest-only beach in Cherngtalay, north-west Phuket. Laguna is next door. Patong is a different island.

Trisara is private villas on a guest-only Cherngtalay beach. The published door is 60/1 Moo 6, Srisoonthorn Road, Cherngtalay, Thalang, Phuket 83110, +66 76 310 100. Villas, not a tower. Two Michelin Keys sit on the current brand page; treat keys as a hotel badge, not a restaurant star.

Phuket Airport is the correct landing, closer than a town transfer. The Andaman calendar still rules: November to April for settled water. In the southwest monsoon, this coast takes wind and rain. A boat day is a desk decision, not a prepaid sure thing.

Beach and zone versus other Phuket coasts

Your Headland

Naithon and Layan headland

Forested northwest privacy and sunset Andaman views, 20 to 25 minutes south of HKT.

  • Trisara Phuket locks every key as a private-pool villa at 35,000 to 95,000+ THB, with PRU as the on-property dining room.
  • The airport hop is the shortest of the ultra-prime west-coast coves, useful on a late BKK connection.
  • Bang Tao and Laguna beach clubs sit a short car south when you want a booked daybed.
Pansea Step-Up

Pansea and Surin coves

Tighter white-sand headlands and Phuket's highest villa rates, farther from the airport.

  • Amanpuri is the step up if you want a gated private cove and will pay 45,000 to 120,000+ THB.
  • The HKT car stretches to 45 to 75 minutes and 1,500 to 2,500 THB, so land with a booked driver.
  • Surin and Kamala dinner rooms stay closer to this rival than to Naithon.
Dinner Bays

Kamala hills and Patong ridge

Walkable dinner bays and faster nightlife when you do not need a private-cove villa.

  • Kamala hills give easier Cafe del Mar style days and west-coast plates without a Patong base.
  • Mai Khao is the long airport beach if you want length, not a private-cove villa.
  • Patong ridge after 22:00 is the wrong default if you booked this quiet northwest cove.

Cherngtalay, Laguna, Bang Tao

Srisoonthorn Road is the inland spine of Cherngtalay. Laguna Phuket and Bang Tao beach clubs sit a short drive south. Catch Beach Club is at 202/88 Moo 2, Cherngtalay, on Bang Tao sand. XANA is inside Angsana Laguna at 10 Moo 4 Srisoonthorn. Those are afternoon rooms, listed on the nightlife page. They are not a reason to leave Trisara at noon unless you want a DJ.

Phuket Old Town is the Baba lunch, 50 minutes when the road is kind. Do it once. Do not string Patong Walking Street onto this stay. Amanpuri is the other west-coast pavilion product, further south toward Kamala and Surin. You do not need both in one week.

Do you need a car?
The resort can keep you fed. Book a driver for Old Town or a club. A scooter on Srisoonthorn after dark is a poor idea if you do not already ride.
