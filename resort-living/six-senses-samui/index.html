Six Senses Samui

Six Senses Samui is known for hill villas above Bophut. Stay here if you want a Gulf headland, not the Chaweng strip.

Back to Koh Samui

January to September is the easier sea. USM is about 15 to 25 minutes by car. Still book the car. The last road is steep.

Bophut village is the dinner walk if you leave the hill. Fisherman Village is the evening grid. Nikki Beach is on the nightlife page, not here. A 2026 Grab down the hill is often 200 to 400 THB. Do not ride a scooter on that hill at night. The grade is steep and there is no shoulder.

Beach and zone versus other Samui coasts

Your Point

Choeng Mon northern headland

A rocky 20-acre peninsula near Fisherman Village, 15 minutes from USM.

  • Six Senses Samui locks timber villas on the point at 22,000 to 55,000+ THB.
  • Bophut village is the dinner walk if you leave the hill. A 2026 Grab down often runs 200 to 400 THB.
  • Choeng Mon swimming coves sit below the villas, so you do not need Chaweng for a beach hour.
Sunset Step-Up

Laem Yai northwest cape

The sunset amphitheater if privacy beats a village dinner walk.

  • Four Seasons Koh Samui is the butler-buggy rival at 36,000 to 90,000+ THB, 35 to 45 minutes from USM.
  • Lipa Noi daybeds pair with that cape more cleanly than with Choeng Mon.
  • Book this rival when the Gulf sunset, not Fisherman Village, is the week.
Bay or Strip

Chaweng strip versus Plai Laem bay

Walkable nightlife sand, or a wider plantation bay 15 minutes from the airport.

  • Chaweng is swim-and-nightlife, a different week from this timber headland.
  • The Ritz-Carlton, Koh Samui is the closer bay estate at 16,000 to 42,000+ THB if you want lawn and a gentler arrival.
  • January to September is the swimming window on all three coasts.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Villas are the inventory: Entry pool villas in a busy 2026 week often sit from about 22,000 to 35,000 THB. Larger residences run 40,000 to 80,000 THB and up. Planning bands only. Trip.com shows the live number. Four Seasons sits on the northwest coast if you want a different Gulf view.
  • Ang Thong is a day boat from Samui, 300 THB park fee, no public ferry: Phangan is 30 minutes from the island pier, not from the villa door. Leave a buffer before any boat. Do not land USM at 16:00 and book a 17:00 crossing.
  • Land at Samui (USM), then a private car to the Bophut / Plai Laem headland: The ride is 15 to 25 minutes. A 2026 booked car is often 600 to 1,200 THB. Book the car with the room. Touts at USM quote more.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is a Lower Gulf hill: The wettest window is roughly October to December on the east and north coasts. A prepaid sunset daybed in November can still work on a calm day. Recheck the sky.
  • Six Senses Samui spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 4,500 to 9,000 THB: Book it after a rest morning, not after Ang Thong. A 2026 Grab down to Bophut is often 200 to 400 THB.
  • Ask for the 2026 transfer in THB on the voucher: Extra beds change villa math by a few thousand baht. Fisherman Village is the dinner walk if you leave the hill. Nikki Beach is a booked afternoon, not a walk from the lobby.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • Phangan is about 30 minutes from the island pier, not from the villa door: Leave a buffer. Ang Thong is 300 THB at the park plus the boat.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
Chaweng or this hill?
Chaweng if you want the strip. This hill if the villa is the trip.
