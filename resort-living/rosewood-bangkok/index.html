Rosewood Bangkok

Rosewood Bangkok is known for a Ploenchit tower with the Skytrain in the basement. Use it for city days. Use the river hotels if you want water.

Rosewood Bangkok is a Ploenchit tower with BTS Exit 1 in the basement. It opened 31 March 2019 at 1041/38 Ploenchit Road, Lumphini, Pathumwan 10330, +66 2 080 0088. One hundred and fifty-eight rooms. Ride to Siam, Thonglor, or Saphan Taksin without standing in Ploenchit rain.

This is central Pathumwan: Central Embassy, Wireless Road embassies, and a short walk toward Lumphini Park. It is not a quiet river path. Book it when the itinerary is city. Book Four Seasons or the Oriental when the itinerary is water.

River and district versus other Bangkok stays

Your Manor

Phloen Chit skytrain manor

Direct BTS, malls, and rooftop cocktails with no river road in the way.

  • Rosewood Bangkok locks the vertical manor at 14,000 to 32,000+ THB beside BTS Ploenchit.
  • Embassy row and Central Embassy sit a short walk, which is why this lock works for mixed business weeks.
  • Airport Rail Link plus BTS beats a Chao Phraya taxi on a short stay.
River Compounds

Chao Phraya compounds

River boats and low-rise courtyards if the barge, not the skytrain, is the stay.

  • Capella Bangkok and Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok sit on the water at 25,000 to 85,000+ THB.
  • Four Seasons Bangkok shares that bank if you want a courtyard spa wing.
  • Pick the river when palace boats matter more than a mall bridge.
Garden or Rails

Ratchathewi and Dusit contrast

A cheaper ARL pocket one stop from Siam, or an upriver garden if you want teak instead of a tower.

  • Ratchathewi is the cheaper design-room pocket one stop from Siam, with ARL at the same interchange.
  • The Siam remains the upriver teak compound if you want a garden instead of a tower.
  • This alternative wins when the manor rate is closed and you still want rails, not a river road.

Ploenchit and Wireless

Breakfast, then BTS. That is the stay. Dinner can be the hotel or a Grab to Thonglor. Soi 11 is one stop plus a walk if someone in the party insists. The park is the morning reset if the air is clear.

BKK and DMK are both about 45 minutes in decent traffic and much more in rain. Use the hotel car. Ploenchit Road drop-offs clog; do not plan a tight international departure from a 09:00 checkout without a buffer.
