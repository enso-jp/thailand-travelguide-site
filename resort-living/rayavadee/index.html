Rayavadee

Rayavadee is known for a boat-only grove in the Railay cliffs. Use it when the pocket is the stay and you accept being stuck on a storm day.

Back to Krabi

There is no road. You arrive by boat in about 15 minutes from Ao Nang. That is not a bug.

KBV is 30 to 45 minutes to the Ao Nang pier, then the hotel boat. A 2026 combined transfer is often 2,000 to 4,000 THB a person or a private rate on the voucher. Lock it in the Andaman window, November to April. A storm day here is still beautiful and fully stuck.

Beach and zone versus other Krabi and Phang Nga coasts

Your Pocket

Phra Nang and Railay pocket

Boat-only limestone beaches with zero road traffic and a 15-minute longtail from Ao Nang.

  • Rayavadee locks two-storey coconut-grove pavilions at 22,000 to 60,000+ THB, steps from Phra Nang cave beach.
  • There is no car at the door. Bags move by golf cart and boat.
  • Climbing walls and three beaches are the week, not an Ao Nang night market loop.
Storm-Proof Rival

Tubkaek storm-proof road

Mainland sunset sand if you want a KBV car and an exit that does not depend on a longtail.

  • Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve is the road-access rival at 28,000 to 75,000+ THB.
  • Hong Island views sit across the water, and the car stays booked for a weather day.
  • Pick this rival when you refuse a stuck-storm story on Railay.
Bay-Villa Alternative

Koh Yao Noi bay villas

Phang Nga pinnacles as the daily view, with a 45-minute speedboat from Phuket or Krabi.

  • Six Senses Yao Noi is the island alternative at 26,000 to 70,000+ THB.
  • The bay is the week. Ao Nang dinners become a project.
  • Book this alternative when sunrise karst, not a cave beach, is the reason you came.

Why stay here and beach setting

  • Pavilions sit in the coconut grove between Railay and Phra Nang. Entry pavilions in a busy dry-season week often sit from about 20,000 to 35,000 THB. Larger pavilions and villas run 40,000 to 80,000 THB and up. Planning bands. Phra Nang beach is the walk. Ao Nang dinners are a boat.
  • Climbing and longtails are the off-property days. Phi Phi is a morning product from Ao Nang, not from the grove door. If you want the same cliffs with a car, sleep in Ao Nang. If you want a bay villa with a roadless island transfer, Yao Noi is the other Andaman hideaway.
  • Land at Krabi (KBV): then a car of 30 to 45 minutes to the Ao Nang pier, then the hotel boat of about 15 minutes into the Railay / Phra Nang grove. A 2026 combined transfer is often 2,000 to 4,000 THB a person, or a private rate on the voucher. Do not land late and cross in the dark in swell.

Rooms, villas, and 2026 rate tiers

  • This is an Andaman cliff pocket. Wet season is roughly May to October. Book November to April if you cannot accept a cancelled boat. The east Railay landing is the usual wet-season door.
  • Rayavadee spa hours in 2026 often sit from about 4,000 to 8,000 THB. Book on a still morning. Ao Nang dinners are a boat, often 100 to 200 THB a person on a shared longtail if you skip the hotel launch.
  • Ask for the 2026 pier-and-boat quote in THB. Extra beds change pavilion math.

Airport transfers, monsoon window, and spa

  • If you want a bay villa with an island transfer: Six Senses Yao Noi is the other Andaman hideaway.

Common questions

Do you need a tour desk?
Not on arrival day. Confirm the transfer on the hotel voucher and leave the first afternoon empty.
What if the sea is up?
You stay. That is the trade. Book November to April if you cannot accept a cancelled boat.
