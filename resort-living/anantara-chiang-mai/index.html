Anantara Chiang Mai Resort

Anantara Chiang Mai is known as a river resort beside the Night Bazaar. Breakfast on the Ping. The Old City after 16:00. Not the Mae Rim valley.

Anantara Chiang Mai is the Ping River resort beside the Night Bazaar, at 123-123/1 Charoen Prathet Road, Changklan, Mueang Chiang Mai 50100, +66 53 253 333. Rooms face the river or the garden. This is an in-town stay, not the Mae Rim valley.

Four Seasons Chiang Mai is a different product 30 to 45 minutes north. 137 Pillars House is the Wat Gate teak compound across the water. Book Anantara when you want a pool and a river path without giving up dinner in the Old City.

Valley and district versus other Chiang Mai stays

Your River Bank

Ping River contemporary bank

Night Bazaar side lawns, 10 minutes to the moat, with a modern river sanctuary rather than an 1889 house.

  • Anantara Chiang Mai locks the Ping bank at 12,000 to 28,000+ THB.
  • Wat mornings are a short Grab. Sunday Walking Street is a planned evening, not a surprise.
  • This is the easier river lock if teak homestead rates are closed.
Teak Rival

Wat Ket teak homestead

The 1889 house look if antique timber, not a contemporary river wing, is the stay.

  • 137 Pillars House is the homestead rival at 18,000 to 40,000+ THB.
  • Both sit about 10 minutes from the moat. The architecture is the difference.
  • Pick teak when the lawn and the original house are the photograph.
Valley or Hill

Mae Rim versus Hang Dong

A rice-terrace pavilion week, or a western hill pool at an accessible rate.

  • Four Seasons Chiang Mai is the 30,000 to 80,000+ THB valley lock if you accept a 30 to 45 minute car each way.
  • Hang Dong hill pools give a western valley view without pavilion rates, 25 to 35 minutes from town.
  • Haze from late February to April can sit in both valley and hill views.

Changklan and the eastern gates

Charoen Prathet is the river road. The Night Bazaar is behind you, tourist by design, useful for one walk. Tha Phae Gate is a short hop west. Sunday Walking Street is doable on foot if you like crowds. Wat Gate, the quieter teak neighbourhood, is across the Nawarat Bridge; that is breakfast-noodle country (Lung Khajohn) and 137 Pillars.

CNX is about 15 to 25 minutes. Book the car. Smoke season still applies. Khao soi belongs at Mae Sai or Khun Yai, not as a room-service impression.
