Kanchanaburi day trips

Kanchanaburi day trips are known for Erawan Falls or the Death Railway story, not both in one afternoon, so stay two nights. The bridge itself is a morning in town.

Erawan and Sai Yok is the waterfall day: 300 THB for a foreign adult, seven tiers, 65 to 70 km from Kanchanaburi town. Bus 8170 leaves about 08:00 and takes about 1.5 hours, and treat 15:30 as the turnaround for tier 7. Hellfire Pass is a memorial walk by road, not a train stop.

Do not add Ayutthaya to the same van, and trains in still leave Thonburi, not Krung Thep Aphiwat; see Bangkok to Kanchanaburi. Two nights if Erawan and the railway are both planned, and the bridge itself lives on River Kwai.

Common questions

Erawan and Hellfire Pass the same day?
A car can rush both. You will short-change tier 7 or the memorial walk. Pick one.
Erawan from Bangkok as a day trip?
You will sit in a van. Stay two nights in Kanchanaburi if the falls matter.
