## The season

- **Open:** **15 October 2026 to 15 May 2027**.
- **Closed:** mid May to mid October, for reef recovery and the southwest monsoon.
- **Opening day:** 1,409 visitors, 1,359 on day trips and 50 divers, per the park.

## How a trip works

- **Day trips:** speedboats leave Thap Lamu pier, just south of Khao Lak, in the morning and return in the afternoon.
- **Liveaboards:** divers stay out for several nights to reach the outer sites, including Koh Bon and Koh Tachai.
- **Life jackets:** required for every boat passenger on the way out.

## Reef rules

- **Reef-safe sunscreen only:** the park is checking and campaigning on this from day one.
- **No single-use plastic:** bring a refillable bottle.
- **Stay off the coral:** no standing, touching or feeding fish.

## Timing tips

- **Go early in the season:** late October and November are quieter than December to February.
- **Base in Khao Lak:** it is the closest coast, about 1 to 1.5 hours by road from Phuket airport.
