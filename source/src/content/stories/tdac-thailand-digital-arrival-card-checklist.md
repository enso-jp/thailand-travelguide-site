## The rules

- **Who:** every non-Thai national, including children and infants, and visa-exempt travellers.
- **When:** within **3 days before arrival**, counting the arrival day.
- **Where:** only the Immigration Bureau site, **tdac.immigration.go.th**.
- **Cost:** **free**. Sites that charge a fee are not official.
- **Once per entry:** a TDAC is single use. File a new one for every trip into Thailand.

## Have these ready

- **Passport details**
- **Flight number and arrival date**
- **Your first address in Thailand**, such as the hotel name
- **Travel purpose and a short health declaration**
- **An email address** to receive the TDAC

## Good to know

- **Transit only:** if you do not pass immigration, you do not need one.
- **Plans changed:** you can update or resubmit before you enter. The latest entry counts.
- **No reminders:** the system does not email you before your trip, so set your own.
- **Not a visa:** the TDAC does not change visa or visa exemption rules. Check those separately.
