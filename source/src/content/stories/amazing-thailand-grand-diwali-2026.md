## What Diwali is, and why Thailand is lighting up

- **The festival:** Diwali (Deepavali) marks the triumph of light over darkness and good over evil. Families light oil lamps called **diyas**, gather, pray and exchange gifts.
- **Diwali Day 2026:** Saturday **8 November 2026**, per TAT.
- **The Thailand programme:** **Amazing Thailand Grand Diwali Festival 2026** runs **3 to 29 November 2026** with **seven** celebrations in Bangkok, Pattaya (Chon Buri) and Phuket.
- **Who backs it:** Organised by the Indian Association of Thailand (IAT) and the Thai-India Trade and Tourism Association (TITTA), in collaboration with TAT, the Embassy of India in Bangkok and partners. TAT calls this its **fifth consecutive year** of support.
- **What to expect:** five signature diya-lighting ceremonies, Thai-Indian cultural performances, Bollywood music, festive markets and food. Admission to all seven celebrations is **free**.

## The seven celebrations (TAT schedule)

- **The First Light at Central Bangrak, Bangkok:** Monday **3 November**, diya ceremony **17:00 to 18:00**, then live Bollywood music; activities continue through **4 November**.
- **Lotus by the River at ICONSIAM Riverpark, Bangkok:** **6 to 8 November**. On Diwali Day **8 November**, diya lighting **17:00 to 18:00** and riverside fireworks at **18:00**.
- **Nine Nights of Candlelight at Central Festival Pattaya Beach:** **7 to 15 November**, including Diwali Day activities on **8 November**.
- **A Sunday of Flavour at Central Patong, Phuket:** **8 November**, Indian food festival and festive activities (TAT did not print a clock time for this day).
- **Lamps Along the Shore on Pattaya Beach Road:** **13 to 15 November**, with a beachfront diya ceremony on **13 November** from **18:00 to 19:00**.
- **Threads of Faith in Phahurat (Little India), Bangkok:** **20 to 24 November**, building on Diwali events along Khlong Ong Ang since 2022. On **24 November**, diya lighting **17:00 to 18:00** joins **Dev Deepavali**, **Loi Krathong** and **Gurpurab** on the same full-moon night.
- **Music on the Avenue at Quartier Avenue, EMQUARTIER, Bangkok:** **26 to 29 November**. Final diya ceremony on **29 November** from **17:00 to 18:00**.

## How to plan a night (or three)

- **Free entry:** TAT confirms admission is free at all seven venues. Participating malls may run seasonal promotions; those are separate from the ceremony tickets (there are none).
- **Best Diwali Day pick in Bangkok:** ICONSIAM's Lotus by the River on **8 November** if you want the printed fireworks time (**18:00**). Arrive before the **17:00** diya window for a riverside spot.
- **Pattaya stretch:** Candlelight nights at Central Festival Pattaya Beach (**7 to 15 November**) pair with the Beach Road lamps (**13 to 15 November**).
- **Phahurat deep dive:** Save **20 to 24 November** for Little India markets and the **24 November** triple-festival night.
- **Check before you go:** TAT Contact Centre **1672**. Re-check the venue board the day you visit if a stage time shifts.

## Pair it with November's other river nights

- **6 November:** the [Royal Barge Procession](/stories/royal-barge-procession-bangkok-6-november-2026) sails the Chao Phraya. ICONSIAM's Diwali window (**6 to 8 November**) sits right beside it, so river-area stays cover both.
- **13 November to 13 December:** [Vijit Chao Phraya](/stories/vijit-chao-phraya-2026) lights 13 riverside sites after Diwali Day, so late-November Bangkok can stack lamps, barges and projection mapping.
- **24 November:** [Loy Krathong](/stories/loy-krathong-yi-peng-2026-dates) falls on the same full moon as Phahurat's Threads of Faith diya. Expect floating krathongs plus Little India lights on one night.
- **Stay tip:** base near the river (ICONSIAM / Charoen Nakhon), Bangrak or Phahurat so you can walk or hop a short boat between ceremonies. Use the stay box on this page for Bangkok options; we are not naming individual hotels here.
