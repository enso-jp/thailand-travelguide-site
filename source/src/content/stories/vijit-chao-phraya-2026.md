## The dates that are locked in

- **When:** Friday **13 November to Sunday 13 December 2026**, about **31 nights**.
- **Who runs it:** the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT). This is the **fifth** edition.
- **Theme:** **Light of Imagination** (แสงแห่งจินตนาการ), confirmed by TAT Governor Thapanee Kiatphaibool at a partners briefing on **22 October 2026**.
- **Where on the map:** both banks of the Chao Phraya between **Rama III Bridge** and **Rama VIII Bridge**.
- **How many sites:** **13** riverside locations with light and sound, projection mapping, drone formations, fireworks and cultural performances.
- **Cost to watch:** free from the riverbanks, as with past editions. Re-check TAT closer to opening if any paid viewing platforms appear.

## Sites named so far

- **Named landmarks:** Rama VIII Bridge, Wat Arun Ratchawararam, Wichai Prasit Fort, Memorial Bridge (Phra Phuttha Yodfa), Phra Pok Klao Bridge and Krung Thep Bridge.
- **Plus more:** TAT says other major riverside locations complete the set of 13. Full site maps usually land closer to opening night.
- **Best postcard bank:** the Bangkok side opposite Wat Arun (Tha Tien area) for the lit prang across the water. Climb the temple by day; watch the river by night.
- **Bridge views:** Rama VIII for a wide cable-stayed silhouette, Memorial Bridge and Krung Thep Bridge for the denser mid-river stretch.

## What is still unpublished

- **Nightly show calendar:** as of **4 November 2026**, TAT had not posted the night-by-night list of drone nights, fireworks nights or exact start times on its main channels.
- **Treat third-party timetables as provisional:** some ticket and listing sites already print Friday or weekend fireworks around **20:45** and Wednesday drone nights at Wat Arun. Wait for TAT, the Thailand Festival listing, or on-site boards before you plan a single big night around one clock time.
- **2025 window for context only:** last year's programmes ran free from about **18:00 to 22:00**. That is useful for planning dinner and boats, not a confirmed 2026 timetable.

## How to get there and watch

- **River boats:** Chao Phraya Express (orange flag) and the local ferries are the simplest way to hop between piers. Start from **Saphan Taksin (BTS)** if you are coming from the Skytrain.
- **Useful piers:** Tha Tien for the Wat Arun view, Tha Arun on the Thonburi bank, and stops near Memorial Bridge for the mid-river bridges.
- **MRT backup:** Blue Line **Sanam Chai** for the old town bank, **Itsaraphap** for the Thonburi side near Wat Arun.
- **Crowds:** opening weekend and weekends fill first. Arrive before dusk if you want a rail-side spot without standing five people deep.
- **Safety:** stay behind barriers on bridges and piers. Marine Police and tourist police support the event corridor; follow staff directions if a stretch closes for a fireworks barge.

## Pair it with November's other river nights

- **6 November:** the [Royal Barge Procession](/stories/royal-barge-procession-bangkok-6-november-2026) sails to Wat Arun. River boats pause in the old town that afternoon, then the light festival opens a week later.
- **24 November:** [Loy Krathong](/stories/loy-krathong-yi-peng-2026-dates) falls on the full moon Tuesday inside the Vijit window. Expect floating krathongs plus the light sites on the same river.
- **Stay idea:** a room near the river (Rattanakosin, Thonburi riverside, or Iconsiam area) saves late Grab rides after the shows. Use the stay box on this page for Bangkok options.
- **Check before you go:** TAT Contact Centre **1672**, and re-check the programme the day you visit in case a fireworks night moves.
