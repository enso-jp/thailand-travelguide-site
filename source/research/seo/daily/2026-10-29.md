## What shipped

1. **Visas and TDAC.** H1, lede, facts, and FAQs now state 30-day tourist visa-free from 15 September 2026, with TDAC on the official Bureau site within 3 days before arrival. A lead section sits after the lede: file TDAC, then book Sukhumvit nights and a BKK or DMK airport car. Hub facts on `/` and `/plan` match.
2. **First week.** Lead fork table plus `.aff-row` links for Sukhumvit hotels, BKK-CNX, and BKK-HKT. Day 3 names Krung Thep Aphiwat and the SRT 9 18:40 to 07:15 board.
3. **South islands 10 days.** Title aligned to Andaman vs Gulf. Lead section books Kata/Karon, Chaweng, and Phuket Phi Phi boats instead of generic city and "Thailand" tour searches.

No Stories and Deep Dives post. The three money-page upgrades were the conversion work.
