Maya Bay

Maya Bay is known for a 400 THB park visit with no swimming, and it fits a dry-season morning from Tonsai, Phuket, or Krabi. Swim at Pileh instead.

Maya Bay sits on Phi Phi Le, the park island; timed entry and a 400 THB fee apply. The conservation rule still bans swimming in the bay; pileh Lagoon is the usual swim stop on the same loop. Viking Cave is a look, not a climb-through; read the board at the desk that morning.

From Don, charter or join a 06:30 longtail if you already sleep in Tonsai, and from Phuket or Krabi, a day speedboat covers the loop. November to April is the window, and May to October boats cancel. Full ticket notes sit on Phi Phi islands.

What the bay actually allows

  • Fee: 400 THB. Often not inside a cheap tour price.
  • Swim: No in Maya Bay. Yes at Pileh unless the board says otherwise.
  • Time: Morning slots beat the midday crush.
  • Sleep: On Don, not on Le.

Common questions

Can you swim in Maya Bay?
No. The conservation rule still bans it. Use Pileh.
Is the 400 THB fee in the ticket?
Often not. Read the inclusions. The park desk is the one that matters that morning.
